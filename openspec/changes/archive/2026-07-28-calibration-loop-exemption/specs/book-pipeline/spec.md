# book-pipeline spec

## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Master plan artifact
The master plan requirement's review gate is generalized from the stale "Opus
review" to a fresh independent high-reasoning review per
`prompts/master-plan-skill-v2.md`, recorded in `master-plan-review.md` and
ending "fit to write from". (Same blocker class: a literal reading dispatches
an unavailable reviewer identity.)

#### Scenario: A master plan is drafted
WHEN `master-plan.md` is drafted
THEN it is NOT final
AND a fresh independent sub-agent review is REQUIRED, recorded in `master-plan-review.md`, iterated until the verdict is "fit to write from".

### Requirement: Chapter writing loop
Chapters are written one at a time, in order, each by a fresh-context writer
that sees only its authoritative inputs (per the governing runbook), then
gated before advancing. Chapters SHALL be written one at a time, in order,
by a fresh-context writer. Production chapters MUST be iterated to reviewer
ACCEPT before publication or advancement. Calibration candidates governed by
`loop/PROGRAM.md` use that runbook's judge gate on the campaign branch and
are NOT published or advanced as accepted book chapters.

#### Scenario: A writer is given extra chapter context
WHEN a chapter-writer receives chapters other than the immediately previous one
THEN the anti-repetition design is violated
BECAUSE the extra context drives LLM repetition that the design exists to suppress.

#### Scenario: A production chapter is accepted
WHEN the reviewer returns ACCEPT for a production chapter
THEN the chapter is committed to `main` immediately
AND the book README status is updated
AND the next chapter may begin.

#### Scenario: The auto-tuning loop generates calibration chapters
WHEN chapters are generated by the auto-tuning loop on its campaign branch
THEN the loop's judge gate (`loop/PROGRAM.md`) governs their evaluation
AND they are not published or advanced as accepted book chapters
AND the production reviewer-ACCEPT and commit-to-main clauses do not apply.
