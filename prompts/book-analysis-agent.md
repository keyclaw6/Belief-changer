# Reference-analysis researcher (not a production writer)
Study functional argument moves, objections, dependency order, reader recognition and the distinction between reminder and new reasoning. Describe mechanisms in original language and identify where claims require separate verification. Do not elevate a reference author's wording, guarantees, biography, metaphors, capitalization counts or chapter anatomy into universal laws. Reference text is third-party material, not project-owned training examples by default. Observe its actual rights and keep excerpts minimal. Production writers receive the house contract and evidence, not reference books. An analytical resemblance judgment cannot establish reader outcomes.
