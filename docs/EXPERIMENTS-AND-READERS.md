# Empirical validation program (not executed by the software upgrade)

## Calibration before optimization
Build retained case artifacts covering invented authority, unsupported outcome promises, omitted objections, wrong dependency order, decorative empty scenes, excessive repetition, necessary factual qualifications, excellent original writing, label reversal, same-text repeatability, unseen subjects and an honest countercase. At least two human raters review blinded material independently before reconciling disagreements. Preserve both raw ratings and consensus; compare the selected external judge with the consensus. Do not tell the judge which text is a reference or require an identity-match perfect score.

Use the calibration.example.json schema. Its case hashes must resolve to retained artifacts in the actual study. The minimum twelve cases and 80% agreement are initial operational thresholds, not a scientifically validated guarantee. Critical false negatives and failed order/repeatability controls block use. Expand cases across domains and record disagreement patterns before relying on the judge at scale. Do not manufacture a calibration file from software fixtures or claimed human ratings.

Measure same-text judge variation separately from repeated-generation variation. A model switch, changed rubric, prompt or context policy needs a fresh registration/calibration. The generator's family may not substitute for the external judge. Human ratings, executable validators and independent model reviews serve different purposes; none alone establishes efficacy.

## Architectural ablation
The initial comparison is the archived factory against v2, treated as a bundled exploratory intervention, NOT a claim that one individual patch caused improvement. Do not silently execute retired unsafe code in production. Any historical reproduction must be isolated, explicitly authorized, prevented from publication and paid only under the owner's budget decision.

Then test a preregistered factorial study:

| Condition | Writer contract | Editorial context |
|---|---|---|
| A | Archived/current-complex contract | Local chapter review |
| B | Simplified unified contract | Local chapter review |
| C | Archived/current-complex contract | Actual manuscript-informed review |
| D | Simplified unified contract | Actual manuscript-informed review |

Hold brief, research, accepted plan and generator settings constant within each paired comparison unless the explicit hypothesis concerns them. Choose opening, mechanism, strongest objection, handoff and ending work for exploratory screening; preserve fixed preceding manuscript context. Confirm surviving changes on complete books. Treat multiple comparisons and chapter selection as exploratory until replicated under a new preregistration. A plan change is not a free regeneration; compare its own replicated intervention.

Preregister all samples and stopping rules. Three independent generation pairs per subject can expose large problems, but cannot pass the default confidence requirement even with three wins. Choose a larger confirmatory allocation based on initial variance/budget rather than repeatedly extending a failed study until a favorable draw appears. The runtime's conservative per-subject promotion rule does not estimate medical efficacy or make the judge's judgments objectively true.

Develop on known subjects, then test unfamiliar habitual behavior, a capability/learning belief, and a behavior with real benefits requiring selective reduction or a revised goal. Keep held-out subjects and cases out of prompt-tuning contexts. A general book factory should adapt its mechanism, not replace nouns in a fixed addiction argument.

## Reader pilot protocol
Participation must be voluntary and informed. The reader chooses the change they want to examine; the study must not pressure a belief outcome. Compare the candidate book with a simpler, credible, well-written alternative and, where appropriate, a waitlist or existing evidence-based resource. Randomize allocation when feasible. Do not withhold needed support or tell participants to abandon treatment.

Before reading, capture the specific target belief, confidence, strongest objection, chosen goal, relevant baseline behavior and anticipated sacrifice. Immediately afterward, assess comprehension, confidence calibration, perceived recognition, autonomy, relief, shame/rigidity and unsupported new beliefs. Include free-text objections and an honest option that the argument was unconvincing. Follow up at preregistered intervals suitable for the subject; distinguish self-reported intention from behavior and sustained outcomes. Record attrition and nonresponse; do not count only readers who finish or claim success.

Preregister the primary outcome, measurement instrument, sample allocation, analysis and harm/stop criteria. Use appropriately qualified oversight for health/high-risk topics and applicable ethics/privacy requirements. Publish uncertainty, limitations, negative outcomes and comparator differences. Do not turn changes in an LLM judge score into a claim about reader benefits.

`factory/reader-pilot.example.json` is a planning template, not collected participant data. Collect only necessary data with appropriate consent, retention and access limits. Avoid committing identifiable reader records or sensitive health information to the repository. Retained study artifacts can be referenced by non-identifying hashes/approved locations.
