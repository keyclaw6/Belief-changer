# Book Factory Vision — v2

For a reader-chosen subject, produce an original, coherent book that helps the reader feel accurately understood, examine the strongest opposing case, discover a better-supported view, and act with agency and realistic relief.

Borrow functional lessons from successful belief-change writing, not an author's identity, protected expression, unsupported certainty or life story. The evidence may change the thesis. Different subjects may need different mechanisms, outcomes and structures.

A successful factory preserves factual scope and necessary safety, makes actual manuscript progression visible, supports useful editorial cuts and whole-book revision, and produces a source-traceable final publication with honest authorship.

Scores and passing software tests are tools, not evidence of real reader change. Reliable improvement requires controlled, calibrated experiments; effectiveness requires voluntary reader studies. Publication must not depend on the founder silently repairing chapters, but consequential releases still require accountable editorial/rights and qualified safety review.

The 2026-09-11 owner-authorized v2 upgrade supersedes the earlier indistinguishable-author objective. The original vision is retained with campaign-001 runtime history.
