**Our passage:** “I want to be careful here. These are two people with two different targets, two different approaches, two different lives. **What Carr's voice does here:** Carr speaks directly into the reader’s history, recognizes previous disappointment without blaming them, and then gives a firm verdict about the method. The acknowledgment of likely doubt makes his confidence feel reader-facing rather than abstract: he knows why the reader is wary, but he does not retreat from the promise.

The factual caution itself is legitimate; the material weakness is that the chapter relies on this detached register for too long at the point where direct recognition and earned authority are needed.
