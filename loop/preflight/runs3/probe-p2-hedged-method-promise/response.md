**What Carr's voice does here:** Carr makes a direct, confident promise about what the book will do for the reader. The reader is left wondering whether the method applies to them and whether difficulty should be expected. Rather than lending relief and confidence, the wording creates distance and makes the speaker sound like a liability-conscious self-help marketer.
