## Root cause

The 019 plan's cards double-own the same correction, and nothing in the planner contract or the reviewer gate makes that a defect. Quoted from the accepted plan (`loop/iterations/022/replicate-a/traces/chapter-10/chapter-card.md`, `chapter-11/chapter-card.md`):

- CH-10 `Job: enacted transition — inside BAD SUGAR there is no safe cut-down, special occasion, tomorrow or substitute; totality is only stable state` … `Reader-state: negotiator with house-rule and tomorrow promise`.
- CH-11 `Job: enacted transition — waiting is the Trap; freedom is now …` … `Belief-now: enters lingering that tomorrow is safer; leaves knowing delay is the Con` … `Resolves: I'll start after holidays / when ready someday`.

CH-10's totality already forecloses "tomorrow"; CH-11 then re-enters from "tomorrow is safer" as its entry belief and makes the same demolition its whole job. The writer executes what the card owns; every writer-side handoff rule (021, 022) is overridden by a card that legitimately assigns the rebuild — the trace's own mechanism note: *"Plan schedules tomorrow/delay as CH-10's totality job and again as CH-11's entering belief and section title."* The same pattern produces CH-04 I-04 (*failure was the method*) vs CH-09 I-10 (*their struggle was the wrong method talking*), and CH-12 `Structure: pity-not-envy script; reframe-thought-not-suppress speech; slip-forgiven as rumble lesson` on a card whose own guardrail says `no thought-curriculum`.

Why the factory lets it through — two instructions, one per file:

1. `prompts/master-plan-skill-v2.md`, **Arc and length**: *"The next argument-bearing card enters from the belief now just installed. Adjacent cards must use distinct encounters and build cumulatively rather than repeat a plan-wide inventory."* Uniqueness is required only of *encounters*; the *belief corrected* may be declared on any number of cards, and "enters from the belief now just installed" is satisfied by any entry belief the planner writes. 020 bound the *continuity* field (token-echo of settled jobs); it never touched the job/Resolves/belief-now declaration, which is where the double-ownership lives.
2. `prompts/master-plan-reviewer-v2.md`, **Whole-book cumulative walk**: *"Block adjacent cards that repeat the same principal encounter, re-argue settled work, restage a debuted scene's full job (later cards token-echo only), break a handoff, or reserve work backward."* "Re-argue settled work" has no checkable test, and the sentence is scoped to *adjacent* cards, so CH-04→CH-09 is out of scope by wording. The 019 plan passed this gate with the CH-10/CH-11 pair in plain text.

The N←N−1 body rebuilds where cards do *not* assign overlap (B ch06–09) are model-attributed and are not targeted here.

## Targeted fix

Budget: D ≥ 3 in the 019 baseline (`willpower-lexicon`, `factory-speech`, `re-argument` ×3 lanes, `coach-register`, `wrong-register`, `copied-mannerism`, `pre-debut-spend` all present in both books), so up to three changes. Two are proposed — producer law plus its gate — both replacements, no additions. Plan regenerates (as in 020); writer prompt and style guide stay at the accepted 014 state. Not a replay: 020's continuity bullet is untouched and not re-proposed; this is a different section (job declaration, not continuity field) and the reviewer has never carried a `re-argument` test under the census instrument.

**Change 1 (PRIMARY) — `prompts/master-plan-skill-v2.md` — class: journey `re-argument` — component: plan-card (Arc and length, enacted-transition/belief-now declaration) — replace**

> The next argument-bearing card enters from the belief now just installed. Adjacent cards must use distinct encounters and build cumulatively rather than repeat a plan-wide inventory.

**with:**

> The next argument-bearing card enters from the belief now just installed, and only from it: a card's entry belief is never a justification, relapse door, or valuation that an earlier card's enacted transition has already corrected. One correction, one card: every justification, relapse door, and false valuation the book demolishes is named in exactly one card's enacted transition, and no other card — adjacent or not — may name it in its job, the objection it resolves, its belief now, its encounter, or its structure as still open. An enacted transition that gathers several doors into one totality forecloses every door it names; a door that deserves its own chapter is left out of the totality and given to that chapter. Later cards receive a corrected belief only as one spoken sentence or its frozen token. Adjacent cards must use distinct encounters and build cumulatively rather than repeat a plan-wide inventory.

**Change 2 — `prompts/master-plan-reviewer-v2.md` — class: book-arc `re-argument` — component: plan-card (reviewer whole-book walk gate) — replace**

> Block adjacent cards that repeat the same principal encounter, re-argue settled work, restage a debuted scene's full job (later cards token-echo only), break a handoff, or reserve work backward.

**with:**

> Block adjacent cards that repeat the same principal encounter, break a handoff, or reserve work backward. Block any card, adjacent or not, whose job, resolved objection, belief now, encounter, or structure names a justification, relapse door, or valuation that an earlier card's enacted transition already corrected — including one door out of an earlier card's totality — or that restages a debuted scene's full job (later cards token-echo only). Name the pair (C-NN corrects it; C-MM re-opens it) and the smallest correction: strike it from one card or move the ownership.

No change to `prompts/chapter-writer.md`, `prompts/style-guide.md`, `prompts/research-agent.md`, or `loop/config.yaml`. No model, fallback, or route change.

**Why this component:** The trace names plan-card as root of the one `re-argument` cluster present in both books at the PRIMARY seam, the double-ownership is legible in the card text before any writer runs, and writer-prompt already holds two strikes on this class — a card that assigns the rebuild defeats any downstream handoff rule.
