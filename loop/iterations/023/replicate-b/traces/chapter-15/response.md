Chapter 15
NOW GO AND LIVE SWEETLY

**IN THIS CHAPTER**
- A photographed list on the fridge door
- A kitchen cleared without ceremony
- An open door and a life to grow into

*You are not losing sweetness, you are growing back into it.*

### What You Carry Now

You ate your last sweet meal with open eyes. You spoke and you were free. You have lived your mornings, your shops, your quiet evenings since, and you know.

We lived for years inside the same con, paying for a lift that only borrowed from the next hour, paying for love in a wrapper that only borrowed from the moment. You have walked out of the sugar trap.

Feel the difference now. No drawer calls. No afternoon dread waits at the clock. Hunger comes and real food answers it cleanly. That is not luck and it is not effort. It happened easily, at once and for good.

I have seen it thousands of times, and I tell you with all my heart: you do not need to guard your behaviour with rules. Guard the sight that freed you. When a sweet thought drifts by — thin as smoke on a cold evening — do not chase it and do not fight it. Let it turn by itself into joy:

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE FROM BAD SUGAR!

Think that and get on with enjoying your life. That is the whole art after freedom. No mourning. No envy. Only relief that you no longer feed what fed on you.

Keep the margin as quietly as a seatbelt you never plan to use. It is there for a jolt on the road, never as permission to steer toward the wall. Steer free and forget it.

### The List To Photograph

If you opened this book here at the end, close it now and start at the beginning. All you have to do is follow all the instructions.

For you who have read and are free, here is your portable memory. Photograph it. Pin it on the fridge. Clear the kitchen without ceremony and keep walking:

1. KEEP AN OPEN MIND
2. CARRY ON EXACTLY AS NORMAL FOR NOW
3. BEGIN WITH ELATION
4. NEVER JUDGE THE AFTERNOON BY THE LIFT
5. LET CELEBRATION PROVE ITSELF WITHOUT SUGAR
6. TRUST HUNGER AND EAT REAL FOOD WITH JOY
7. SEE THE NIBBLER AS SMALL AND DYING
8. IGNORE ANY ADVICE THAT ASKS YOU TO QUIT FROM FEAR
9. GIVE NO SCENE CREDIT TO SUGAR
10. TAKE NO SPECIAL DOSE IN ANY FORM
11. IGNORE ANYONE WHO QUIT BY THE WILLPOWER METHOD
12. AVOID BORROWING DOUBT FROM SWEET-EATERS
13. TAKE YOUR LAST ORDINARY SWEET MEAL AND DECIDE YOU ARE FREE

Read them once and smile. You already understand every line because you have lived the belief behind each one. They are not rules to carry. They are what free people remember.

### Growing, Not Quitting

This was never about quitting.

Quitting sounds like something taken away. Look at your life and tell me what was taken. Your mornings are clearer. Your food tastes brighter. Your celebrations are fully yours. Your evenings are calm.

Sweetness never left you. You left the trap that blocked sweetness. Your appetite was the compass all along, and now it points true again.

So go outward. Do not preach and do not hide. If a friend asks why you shine, let your ease answer. If a sweet-eater offers you a dose, feel only warmth for them and joy for yourself. You are not being deprived. They still pay for the con.

You are free for the rest of your life. Not for a week. Not until a test. For good, because there is nothing to go back to.

Marvellous, wonderful days wait — ordinary shops, ordinary meals, ordinary laughter made extraordinary because nothing dulls them now. Rejoice in them. Celebrate them. Grow into them.

Whenever BAD SUGAR crosses your mind, in a shop, at a birthday, late at night when old habit whispers, answer with the thought that is yours forever:

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE FROM BAD SUGAR!

**SUMMARY**
- The lift, the reward and the taste joy belonged to the moment and the body, never to the dose.
- Freedom came from seeing the con, and seeing keeps you free.
- A sweet thought turns at once into relief and passes.
- Ordinary life, lived unchanged, tastes brighter because nothing is missing.
- This is growing back into sweetness, not leaving it.
