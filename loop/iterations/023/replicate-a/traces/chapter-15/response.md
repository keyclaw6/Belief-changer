Chapter 15
GET ON WITH LIFE

IN THIS CHAPTER
- Photographed list on the fridge door
- Cleared kitchen shelf without ceremony
- Open street and the morning ahead

*You have not lost sweetness, you have grown back into it.*

What You Already Know

You have lived the proof now. The same mornings, the same shops, the same food — and no gnaw running the day.

We spent years believing BAD SUGAR gave us a lift and a reward and made life sweeter. We mistook the ending of its own discomfort for pleasure. You see it plainly now. There was never anything to miss.

The fact is, you walked out of the sugar trap, and you did it easily, at once and for good. That is not boasting. That is simply what happens when the false benefit collapses. All you have to do is follow all the instructions. You did, and you are free.

I have seen this moment thousands of times. It always has the same bright note: relief, lightness, surprise that it feels so ordinary. That ordinariness is freedom.

The List To Keep

Take a photograph of this and keep it where you see it. If you came straight here without reading, put this down and start at the beginning — the list means nothing without the understanding that earned it.

1. KEEP AN OPEN MIND
2. CARRY ON EXACTLY AS NORMAL FOR NOW
3. BEGIN WITH ELATION
4. NEVER JUDGE THE AFTERNOON BY THE LIFT
5. LET CELEBRATION PROVE ITSELF WITHOUT SUGAR
6. TRUST HUNGER AND EAT REAL FOOD WITH JOY
7. SEE THE NIBBLER AS SMALL AND DYING
8. IGNORE ANY ADVICE THAT ASKS YOU TO QUIT FROM FEAR
9. GIVE NO SCENE CREDIT TO SUGAR
10. TAKE NO SPECIAL DOSE IN ANY FORM
11. IGNORE ANYONE WHO QUIT BY THE WILLPOWER METHOD
12. AVOID BORROWING DOUBT FROM SWEET-EATERS
13. TAKE YOUR LAST ORDINARY SWEET MEAL AND DECIDE YOU ARE FREE

That is the whole method. No small print. Live by that list and you stay free for the rest of your life.

Clear the shelf, keep the sweetness. Think of that seatbelt in your car. It sits there quietly for an accidental jolt. You never use it as permission to drive into a wall. Keep your mind the same way — guarded, settled, done.

And whenever a picture of a sweet floats past, answer it at once with your joyful shout:

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE FROM BAD SUGAR!

Growing, Not Quitting

This was never about quitting. Quitting sounds like something sweet was taken from you.

Nothing sweet was taken. Sweetness never left you. Real food is still sweet, fruit is still sweet, your appetite is still sweet — the trap stood between you and all of it, dulling hunger, dulling taste, dulling afternoons. You left the trap that blocked sweetness.

Your appetite was the compass all along. Trust it. Eat what you love, stop when you are satisfied, live your days without calculation.

So get on with enjoying your life. Celebrate meals, celebrate mornings, celebrate evenings that ask nothing of you. You are not a person missing something. You are a person grown past something.

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE FROM BAD SUGAR!

SUMMARY

- You did not lose sweetness, you grew back into it — get on with enjoying your life
