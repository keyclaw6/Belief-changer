# Master Plan — Quit Sugar
`production-books/quit-sugar/master-plan.md`

## 1. Book core

**Target behavior:** compulsive consumption of refined / added sugar and junk carbs — the craving-snacking loop and its grip, not nutrition pedantry.

**Reader (planner-facing state):** an adult in mid-life food fatigue who grazes, binges or yo-yos daily, has tried diets, moderation rules and white-knuckling and watched them all fail, suspects the whole approach is wrong, and reads with a mix of hope and embarrassment that this book could be another trick.

**Load-bearing false belief:** bad sugar is a genuine treat or lift that makes life sweeter and functional, and life without it would be deprivation.

**Through-line:** escape not sacrifice — from con to appetite reclaimed. Every chapter makes stopping feel like walking out of a trap, never giving up pleasure.

**Format:** full-length Carr book, 15 chapters, ~60,000 words planned. Front matter authority dossier + contract, middle demolitions on installed ground, vow as last ordinary sweet meal, one ordinary-life chapter, short photographable recap. Raw citations quarantined to back-matter appendix.

**Fork decisions:**
- Fork-1 inner state: full Carr personification. Little creature to starve: the Nibbler. Belief-system that feeds it: the Sweet-Talker.
- Fork-2 outcome: total cessation of BAD SUGAR, commanded with cheerful certainty. Moderation foreclosed with pincer and cliff image. No autonomy to keep a special dose.
- Fork-3 science weight: hard facts at full Carr force — vivid, flat, unhedged, frightening where true is frightening — then relief. Never quitting out of fear. No literature-review texture. Raw citations quarantined.
- Fork-4 villain: two villains hit hard. The engineered trap — sugar industry, bliss-point formulation, ubiquity and marketing — and the named anti-method — the Willpower Method and its equivalents — that kept the reader in it.
- Fork-5 void: change nothing else in life. Body returns to natural baseline on its own. Inhabit old contexts confidently to prove freedom. No replacement system, no environment-redesign doctrine.

**Redefinition and margin-for-error doctrine:**
Redefinition decreed in C01, boxed, CAPS name carried thereafter. When this book says sugar it means BAD SUGAR = refined sugar, added free sugars, sugary drinks, confectionery, pastries, desserts and junk / heavily processed starchy carbs engineered for craving. GOOD sweetness — whole fruit, vegetables, plain whole foods, milk in food, and glucose the body itself manufactures — is not the target and is part of freedom's menu.
Margin doctrine frozen as token T-H: body copes with accidental blip, mind must not reopen door. Seatbelt image: there for accidents, not for driving erratically. Guard the belief, not the behaviour.

**Clinical / eating-disorder safety perimeter:** method territory is belief about BAD SUGAR for general adult. Not medical nutrition therapy, not diabetes management, not eating-disorder treatment. Diabetes, glucose-affecting medication, pregnancy, history of eating disorder, underweight or medical fragility route to clinician via plan-wide advisory CA-A. No diagnosis, no prescription, no weight-loss mechanics. Fear material honours evidence limits and never becomes personal sentence.

**Strongest pro-behaviour scene:** late-night cinema celebration — lights low, beloved film, shared chocolate paid as self-reward after hard week. Met head-on in C09.

**Destination state:** whenever BAD SUGAR crosses mind, immediate relief and joy that reader no longer feeds it — happy to be free, not abstaining. Last ordinary sweet meal taken with attention on ugliness and solemn vow, freedom conferred at once, ordinary mornings/shops/food lived confidently, slips pre-forgiven as warning never licence.

**Saved ending reframe (appears only C15):** growing, not quitting — sweetness never left you, you left the trap that blocked sweetness; appetite was the compass all along.

**Method preserved:** escape not sacrifice; warm to person / vicious to trap, never contempt; no willpower as solution; fear raised at full force then disowned by escape where assigned; immediate freedom after belief change; autonomy of conclusion inside commanded frame; original prose; Fork-1 line per style guide.
