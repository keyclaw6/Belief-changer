Chapter 13
GET ON WITH ENJOYING LIFE

**IN THIS CHAPTER**
- A small photographed list kept in a pocket
- A closed book on the table
- An open doorway and the street beyond
- A plain meal waiting ahead, eaten hungry

*You have lost no sweetness; sweetness has been handed back to you.*

You are living it already.

There is nothing more to learn about BAD SUGAR. The con is seen. The hunger is clean. The mornings, the shops, the meals, the evenings — you have walked them free. What is left is to keep the memory short enough to carry and then go live.

I have seen this moment thousands of times. The reader sits with clear eyes and says, in a quiet voice: but will I need to keep studying to stay free? Will I need to remind myself every day? Will I need a new teaching to hold this?

No. You need no ongoing teaching to stay free. What keeps a person free is not effort. It is seeing. And you have seen.

We spent years believing BAD SUGAR was a genuine treat or fuel that made life brighter, that took the edge off, that paid us for being good, that made love warmer. We chased the lift and woke in the low and called the next dose the cure. That was the Sugar Trap working exactly as it was built to work. The dose created that empty, twitchy, slightly shaky, need-something-sweet-now feeling and then offered itself as the rescue.

Now you know. It does plenty TO you. It does nothing FOR you. The lift never lifted you above where a free eater already stands. The treat never treated you. Once that is seen it cannot be unseen, unless you let the old script back in and call it your own voice again.

So keep the meaning guarded and let the doing look after itself. All you have to do is follow all the instructions.

KEEP THE MEMORY IN YOUR POCKET

Photograph this list. Keep it where you keep what matters. It is the whole method in twelve sentences. You do not need to argue them again. You lived them.

KEEP AN OPEN MIND
Question what you think you know about sweetness.

DON'T STOP OR CUT DOWN UNTIL YOU FINISH
Carry on exactly as normal while you read.

JUDGE ONLY BY WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU
Forget harm versus benefit and ask for the benefit.

NEVER BLAME YOURSELF AGAIN
Every failure was the method, never you.

BEGIN WITH ELATION, NOT DREAD
You are escaping, not losing.

TRUST HUNGER AND SATISFACTION
Eat when hungry, stop when satisfied, enjoy real food.

SEE THE GRUMBLE AS DYING, NOT DEMANDING
That feeling is the Nibbler starving.

IGNORE ANY ADVICE THAT CONFLICTS WITH THIS METHOD
Other voices rebuild the Con.

IGNORE ANYONE WHO QUIT BY WILLPOWER
Their struggle was the wrong method talking.

NEVER KEEP A SPECIAL SWEET
One exception keeps the whole Trap alive.

TAKE YOUR LAST ORDINARY TREAT AND VOW FREEDOM
Pay attention to the ugliness, then rejoice you are free.

NEVER REOPEN THE DECISION
A passing thought is a dead enemy, not an order.

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!

Read that last line whenever a sweet picture crosses your mind, and mean it with joy. That joyful answer turns every echo into proof of escape. Each time you think it, freedom grows stronger.

GO LIVE IT NOW

Close the book. Get on with enjoying your life.

Go to breakfast hungry. Go to the shop with clear eyes. Go to birthdays with love in your chest. Go to cinemas for the story and the laughter. Go to evenings on the sofa with nothing to hide. Eat what you want, when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and never punish yourself again.

Do not stand watch over yourself. Do not count hours. Do not weigh your brightness on scales. A free eater does not patrol freedom. A free eater lives.

If you live with diabetes, take medicine that affects blood sugar or appetite, are pregnant, or carry any history of troubled eating or medical risk, talk to your clinician and follow their advice first.

Pity the trapped eater beside you, kindly, and never envy him. He queues in the rain for a bus you have stepped off. You know the bright minute and the flat hour that follows. You know being flat, foggy and never satisfied at dusk with the box empty. He still believes comfort lives in a wrapper. You know comfort never lived there. Smile, refuse with ease — no thanks, I am happy as I am — and turn back to your own plate, your own company, your own clear head.

When doubt whispers once in a blue hour: was it really nothing? Answer with your own days. Are mornings duller without fog? Are meals greyer without the coating that dulled them? Are you weaker without the hit that made the low? You know the answer because you live it. Marvellous relief. Wonderful cleanness. Exciting plain hunger met and left satisfied.

I tell you as one who was conned the same way: you will not miss the slavery. You will wonder, more and more, how you ever believed chains were treats.

THE CHOICE THAT WAS ALWAYS THERE

Look plainly at the two lives.

One is a life of chasing fixes for the rest of your life — bright packs, loud mouth, dull head, hand moving before the mind has decided, the same low returning every afternoon and every evening, forever.

The other is a life of clear hunger and true satisfaction for the rest of your life — food tasting of itself, evenings calm, mornings even, mind quiet, no debt, no fog, no queue.

Which rest of life do you want? You have chosen. Keep choosing by never choosing again. The question is closed. A thought may visit. The decision does not move.

WHAT REALLY HAPPENED HERE

Here is the fresh truth to take with you, and it changes everything.

You have not lost sweetness. Sweetness was stolen from you and has now been handed back.

Think of a child with a peach. Juice on the chin. Hunger leading. Satisfaction stopping. No counting. No guilt. Real food favourite because hunger makes it so. That eater was you before the Sweet Con taught you that joy lived in bright wrappers and loud hits. Year by year the doses shouted louder and food grew greyer, and you believed the grey was life without treats.

It was the other way around. The grey was the Trap. BAD SUGAR borrowed the light of meals, of company, of celebration, of tired evenings that needed rest, and sent the bill later in craving and fog. You never needed it to feel. You needed to be free of it to feel.

This was never about quitting. It was about growing back into the eater you were.

You are not less now. You are more. More taste. More hunger. More satisfaction. More laughter that belongs to the room, not to the box. More of every bright hour the Trap pretended to give and always took away.

So walk out light. Morning, shop, table, sofa — all lived, all easy, all yours. Whenever sweetness crosses your mind, let the old bell ring the new joy:

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!

**SUMMARY**
- Freedom needs no further study; seeing the con keeps you free without effort.
- A short photographed memory carries the whole method into ordinary life.
- Hunger eaten to satisfaction makes real food favourite again.
- Other users invite warm pity, never envy, because their reach ends in emptiness.
- The closed decision stays closed; passing thoughts only deepen joy.
- Sweetness was stolen by the Trap and has been returned; you have grown back into your true eater.
