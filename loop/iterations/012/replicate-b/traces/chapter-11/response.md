### NATURE'S GUIDE — AND HOW TO READ IT

There is a guide that never left you. It was only drowned out.

I call it Nature's Guide.

Nature's Guide is not a diet. It is not willpower. It is the body's own signalling when you stop pouring syrup in the font. It returns on its own. You do not have to build it. You only have to stop overriding it.

Nature's Guide comes with instruments. Simple. Physical. You have used them since childhood before the Con taught you to doubt them.

First, **the Raw Test**.

The Raw Test is this: would this food be appealing raw, unsweetened, unprocessed — Nature's palatability test. An apple passes. A handful of nuts passes. A carrot passes. A biscuit built in a factory to hit a bliss point does not pass raw. It only appeals after sugar, salt, fat and flavourings are layered to hijack the tongue. The test unmasks Secondary Foods at a glance.

Second, **the 0–20 Hunger Gauge**.

The 0–20 Hunger Gauge is this: 0 = empty/ill, 10 = neutral, 20 = stuffed; eat at true hunger, stop at satisfaction. Not at boredom. Not at agitation. Not at 15 because the packet is open. At 10 you are neutral and free to do anything. At true hunger — perhaps 4 or 5 — you eat. At satisfaction — perhaps 12 or 13 — you stop. The body speaks clearly when BAD SUGAR is not shouting over it.

Third, **Primary Foods vs Secondary Foods**.

Primary Foods are whole foods eaten as Nature intended — fruit, vegetables, fish, meat, eggs, nuts, beans, whole grains intact — foods that pass the Raw Test and satisfy the 0–20 Hunger Gauge without any BAD SUGAR. Secondary Foods are processed constructs containing BAD SUGAR — sweets, biscuits, cakes, chocolate, ice cream, sugary drinks, white bread, pastries, crisps and all the rest that behave as sugar in the body. Primary Foods are what Nature's Guide asks for. Secondary Foods are what the Sugar Trap sells.

Do you feel the difference? One set needs you. The other needs your craving.

The fact is eliminating BAD SUGAR is not deprivation. Your body manufactures glucose from protein and glycerol. There is no essential import you are removing. This is not a ketogenic prescription. It is not a plan to eat zero carbohydrate. It is the simple return of a guide that was always there.

Will this guide leave you flat? The opposite. When taste recovers, fruit shocks you with sweetness. When hunger is heard, eating satisfies. When the after-dip is not being manufactured, afternoons smooth.

Do you remember tight shoes? Wearing them all day for the pleasure of taking them off? BAD SUGAR is tighter shoes sold as comfort. Nature's Guide is simply taking the shoes off and feeling the floor as it is.

Try it once more with your next real meal. Before eating, pause. Ask the Raw Test. You will hear Nature's Guide answer clearly. You will also hear the Con try to argue. Notice both. Choose curiosity over resistance.

**Perception Homework 2 — The 0–20 Hunger Gauge (one meal).**

At your next meal, score yourself before you start. Where are you on the 0–20 Hunger Gauge? 0 = empty/ill, 10 = neutral, 20 = stuffed; eat at true hunger, stop at satisfaction.

Afterwards, score again. Where are you? How does the body feel an hour later — steady or roller coaster? The roller coaster is not personality. It is the schedule.

Did you need BAD SUGAR to reach satisfaction? Did your body fail to make energy? Or did it handle it perfectly, as it always does, when you let Nature's Guide lead?
