## 1. BOOK CORE

**Target behavior:** Compulsive consumption of BAD SUGAR — refined/added sugar and junk carbs taken as a craving–snacking loop. Not nutrition pedantry, not whole-food carbohydrates.

**Reader:** One clear adult. Has tried diets, moderation rules, willpower and watched them all fail. Feels trapped, suspects the method was wrong, not themselves. Addressed as "you" throughout.

**Functional personas (P-01 to P-04):**
*   **P-01 The Loss-of-Control Binger** — one-bite-becomes-the-box, night/weekend binges, secrecy, shame cycle, failed restriction. *Job: prove moderation is the trap.*
*   **P-02 The In-Denial Moderate** — daily grazing framed as "just a treat," carve-outs, "I don't keep it in the house." *Job: dissolve complacency.*
*   **P-03 The Comfort/Identity Eater** — sugar as reward, love, coping, nostalgia, "I deserve it." *Job: demolish the reward illusion.*
*   **P-04 The Energy-Crash Yo-Yoer** — sugar as fuel for focus/energy, 3pm slump, lift-crash rollercoaster. *Job: demolish the fuel illusion.*

**Load-bearing false belief:** *Bad sugar gives me a genuine pleasure, treat, comfort or energy lift that makes life sweeter, and I would be deprived without it.*

**Through-line:** Bad sugar does nothing FOR you; it does plenty TO you. It creates the low it pretends to relieve. Once that inversion is seen, there is nothing to give up — only a Sugar Trap to escape, easily, immediately and permanently.

**Format:** Full-length Carr (17 chapters, ~60,200 words). Pocket-book spine expanded via §B10: redefinition chapter, fear chapter, anti-method chapter, perception homework, embedded testimonial, myths battery, knowledge recap, ritual, relapse-proofing, recap.

**Fork decisions (explicit):**
*   **FORK-1 Inner state — Carr default:** Full personification. Little Monster = **the Squeak** — the trivial, mild physical gnawing that dies in days if starved. Big Monster = **the Con** — the lifelong brainwashing/belief-system that feeds the Squeak and interprets its whimper as "you need sugar." Both named and frozen.
*   **FORK-2 Outcome — Total cessation inside redefinition:** Total abstinence from BAD SUGAR, commanded with cheerful certainty. Moderation foreclosed by the pincer and the cliff-jump. GOOD sugar (natural sugar in whole fruit/vegetables, unrefined whole foods) is not the target and is part of freedom.
*   **FORK-3 Science weight — Carr scare-then-disown:** Hard facts delivered at full flat Carr force — vivid, unhedged, frightening where true — then explicitly disowned as the motivator. No literature-review texture; raw citations quarantined to appendix.
*   **FORK-4 Villain — Two villains, both hit hard:** (a) The engineered trap — sugar industry / product engineering / bliss-point formulation / supersized cues. (b) The named anti-method — the Willpower Method and its willpower-culture apparatus that re-blames past failures on the reader.
*   **FORK-5 Void — Default Carr:** Change nothing else in your life. The body returns to its natural baseline on its own; freedom is proved by inhabiting old contexts confidently. No Burgeon-style replacement system; no root-craving doctrine.

**Redefinition and margin-for-error doctrine:**
Definition boxed in C01 and decreed: *When I say sugar in this book, take it to mean BAD SUGAR.* **BAD SUGAR = refined/added free sugars + processed junk carbs + starchy carb products that behave as sugar in the body** (sweets, biscuits, cakes, chocolate, ice cream, sugary drinks, white bread, pastries, crisps, etc.). **GOOD SUGAR / GOOD FOOD** = natural sugar inside whole fruit/vegetables and intact whole foods eaten as Nature intended — part of Nature's Guide, not restricted.
Margin for error: Your body can cope with an occasional accidental blip; your mind cannot cope with entertaining the Con again. A slip revives nothing physically — it only reopens the belief trap if you let it. Guard the Big Monster (the Con), not the behaviour with white-knuckle vigilance. Seatbelt image: the buffer is there for accidents, not for driving erratically. Clinical specifics routed to CA-01.

**Clinical / eating-disorder safety perimeter (CA-01):** This book is not medical nutrition therapy, diabetes management, or eating-disorder treatment. Do not change prescribed medication, insulin, or a clinically supervised eating plan because of this book — speak to your clinician. If you have a current or past eating disorder, a history of restrictive purging, or use sugar in a self-harm context, use this book only alongside professional support. Crisis/Eating-disorder pointers in front matter appendix. No fasting/detox prescription.

**Strongest pro-behaviour scene:** The warm, deserved celebration — cinema chocolate, family dessert on a date, "I deserve this treat after a hard day" in soft light with people you love. All the pleasure reassignable to company, occasion, relief, and rest — sugar only sneaking a ride.

**Destination state:** Whenever the reader thinks about BAD SUGAR they feel *relief and freedom that they no longer do it* — the thought "FANTASTIC! I'M FREE!" fires automatically. Stopping feels like escape, not sacrifice. Behaviour follows belief without willpower.

**Fresh ending reframe (saved for final movement, not previewed):** You never gave up sweetness — you got your sweetness back. BAD SUGAR was not making life sweeter; it was dulling the taste buds, muting real flavour, and borrowing tomorrow's energy. Freedom is not less sweetness, it is sweetness returned to its proper place, tasted properly for the first time since childhood.

**E-S01 — Mild but well-defined dependency (animal)**
Finding: Intermittent sugar bingeing shows bingeing, withdrawal, craving, cross-sensitization; characterized by the pro-addiction lab as "mild but well-defined."

**E-S02 — Dopamine hit + withdrawal dip (animal)**
Finding: Each binge releases accumbens dopamine re-triggering reward circuit; withdrawal after bingeing is low accumbens dopamine with rise in opposing transmitter — a real measured dip after sugar.

Prohibited: that dopamine equals pleasure; that human physiologic withdrawal proven
Empirical limit: rodent; smaller than drugs; human withdrawal not a diagnosis
Safety limit: present dip as mild, passing; do not medicalize craving

**E-S03 — Schedule is the trap's form**

Research units: SEU-S5, SEU-S9 | Sources: S-5, S-9 | Grade: SUPPORTED
