## 5. Lexicon and instruction spine

Trap register (always for the behavior): dose, fix, hit, feed, the trap, the Con, the Nipper, con, brainwashing, slavery, escape, free. Cigarettes are doses of nicotine, never treats, rewards, friends, or pauses that help.

Freedom register (shown as lived moments named only by mantra lines): the clear morning, the meal closed clean, theGroup quiet aisle at the shops, breath taken easy on the stairs, the patio sitting empty of kit, money staying home. ## 6. Arc and length

Fifteen chapters. First third installs the world: easy contract and reading instructions, the reader already hooked, the Nipper and the Con named in passing when the trap is first seen, mornings and the body as concrete encounters. Middle demolishes on that ground: stress, pleasure, social belonging, concentration and boredom, then the inversion mechanism, then escape routes, then the industry indictment and the anti-method with fear collapsed. One chapter inhabits ordinary non-smoking doing as its primary job before the mechanism deepens. After the vow: the last cigarette as an ordinary instance with full attention, one ordinary-life chapter of mornings, shops, drinks, and driving, then a short photographable recap. Prevalence and attempt statistics appear once, in C-10. The embedded long testimony lives in the main flow inside C-12. Harm facts appear in the argument in C-01 and C-10 only, never in an appendix.

Concept debuts: contract and ease C-01; trap, creatures, sensory phrase, cost formula C-02; axis verdict and illusion token C-03; Nipper starvation logic C-08; totality token C-09; villain receipts C-10; anti-method and fear collapse C-11; terminal mantra and vow C-13; saved enemy-burial reframe C-14; recap C-15.

Freedom curve: promised C-01, suppressed through C-04 to C-11 demolition, rising C-12, detonated C-13 to C-15. Demolition curve: peaks C-04 to C-11, hands off to freedom vocabulary after the vow.
