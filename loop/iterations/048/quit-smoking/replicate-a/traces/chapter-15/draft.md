Chapter 15

KEEP IT

IN THIS CHAPTER: one pocket page to carry, the list that brought you here, and the one joyful thought you keep for life.

*You are free now and you hold everything you need to stay free.*

THE PAGE YOU CARRY

You did the work and the work is done. You came as a smoker who believed cigarettes calmed you and pleased you and helped you belong. You leave as a non-smoker who sees the truth and feels glad to be out.

I do not want you to carry this whole book in your head. I want you to carry one page. What follows is that page. It holds every instruction I gave you in the order I gave it, in the words I gave it. Keep it where you keep what matters. If doubt ever knocks, you will not need to argue it out again. You will read the list and you will remember who you are now.

THE THOUGHTS YOU KEEP

Keep one name for everything you left. The nicotine trap. When a stray thought comes about stress or pleasure or company or a hard day or a bright one, you give it that name and the thought has nowhere to go. It was never your voice. It was the voice of the trap you walked out of.

Keep one thought for every time cigarettes cross your mind, on a good day or a bad day, among smokers or alone, for the rest of your life. Think it exactly as I taught you:

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!

Think it with a smile when you wake and when you eat and when you drink and when you drive and when you wait. Think it when someone lights beside you. Think it when the evening runs late among friends. Each time you think it, the old cue becomes a small celebration. You do not debate the thought. You do not test it. You replace it with gladness and you get on with the moment, which was always yours.

I ask nothing more of you. You changed nothing else in your life to win this. You keep nothing burning and you mourn nothing gone.

SUMMARY

Keep this list. Photograph it if you wish and carry it with you.

1. KEEP AN OPEN MIND.
You cannot be conned twice if you check everything.
2. DO NOT STOP YET.
Let the belief change first, then stopping is easy.
3. BEGIN WITH GLADNESS.
There is no doom in what we do here.
4. NOTICE WHO IS IN CHARGE.
If it were free choice you would not need this book.
5. ASK WHAT GOOD IT DOES AT ALL.
Refuse the harm debate; demand one real gift.
6. WATCH ONE CIGARETTE CLOSELY.
See what it actually does, not what you credit it with.
7. REFUSE THE SPECIAL ONE.
If none is special, every exception story ends.
8. IGNORE ANY ADVICE THAT CONFLICTS WITH THIS METHOD.
Mixed methods rebuild the trap.
9. IGNORE ANYONE WHO SAYS STOPPING MUST BE HARD.
They used the method we name, not the truth.
10. SMOKE YOUR LAST CIGARETTE WITH FULL ATTENTION.
Notice the ugliness, then close the door.
11. NEVER THINK ONE WILL NOT HURT.
The thought is the Con talking, not you.

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE! Go and enjoy life free.
