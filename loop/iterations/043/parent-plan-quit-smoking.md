# Master Plan — Quit Smoking

## 1. Book core

- Target behavior: compulsive cigarette smoking. Total stop. Not cutting down, not vaping as a destination, not NRT as the method.
- Reader: an adult who smokes daily, wants to stop, has failed through willpower, patches, gum or abrupt unaided attempts, and believes life without cigarettes would be miserable, flat, or impossible to manage.
- Load-bearing false belief (frozen): My cigarettes relax me, help me cope, and give me real pleasure, and life without them would mean losing something I need.
- Through-line: the relief credited to the cigarette is the ending of a need the previous cigarette created; seen clearly, there is nothing to give up.
- Format: full-length belief-change book, front matter plus fifteen chapters, planned 61,800 words.
- Fork 1 — mechanism characters: full Carr personification. The trivial physical loop is named the Ember Mite, starved to death in days. The belief system that feeds it is named the Smokescreen, killed by understanding. No no-monster override.
- Fork 2 — destination: total cessation, commanded with warm certainty. The last ordinary cigarette, then none. Moderation foreclosed by the pincer and the cliff image. No autonomy/moderation override.
- Fork 3 — facts: hard facts stated flat and frightening where true, then fear disowned as the motive in the same chapter. No literature-review texture. Raw citations quarantined to back matter if needed. The scare stays in the argument, never in an appendix.
- Fork 4 — villains: two. The nicotine industry that designed the dose unit and recruited the reader, and the Willpower Method with its substitutes and tapers that kept the reader failing. Warmth to the person, contempt only for the trap and the wrong method.
- Fork 5 — void: change nothing else in life. The non-smoker has no desire to fix; the body returns to its baseline on its own. No replacement system, no positive-direction override.
- Redefinition: none. Cigarettes are quitted as a whole category. No Good-X / Bad-X split, no CAPS bad subset, no margin-for-error buffer for deliberate use. A slip revives nothing unless the belief is let back in; guard the belief.
- Safety perimeter: no medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis or illness pointers only. The last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial attention on the ugliness, not a dare to smoke more.
- Strongest pro-behavior scene: a night out with drinks, friends laughing in the smoking area, the feeling of a last night on earth with a cigarette in hand.
- Destination state: whenever smoking crosses the mind, the reader feels relief and quiet joy at being free, with no sense of loss.
- Saved ending reframe (appears only in the final movement): the cigarette never attended any moment of your life; it only charged admission to moments that were already yours.

Reader line: "When cigarette smoke enters the lungs, nicotine is absorbed rapidly in the blood and delivered quickly to the brain, so that nicotine levels peak within 10 seconds of inhalation. Scope: smoked nicotine pharmacokinetics. Permitted inference: the "relief" is replenishment of a rapidly fading dose, not a gift that solves the original stress. - E-08 — product is nicotine. Research unit SEU-002. Source S-091. Grade SUPPORTED as historical industry document. Reader line: "The cigarette should be conceived not as a product but as a package. the product is nicotine." Scope: internal manufacturer definition of pack, cigarette, puff as layers around a dose. Permitted inference: an industry scientist defined the cigarette as a nicotine package and dispenser, not as a flavour accessory. Prohibited inference: quoting an internal memo as if it were a public-health consensus paper, or as a dare to smoke more to "see the product." Empirical limit: one company's planning language, not a clinical trial. Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.
- E-09 — lights do not cut the dose. Research unit SEU-003. Sources S-010, S-080, S-089. Grade SUPPORTED. Reader line: ventilated/"light" cigarettes do not reduce human intake; smokers compensate with bigger puffs. Scope: human smoking versus machine yields. Permitted inference: machine-measured lower tar/nicotine does not mean the smoker took less; compensation is the designed human response to a nicotine-seeking dose. Prohibited inference: every smoker compensates identically, or that unventilated cigarettes are a harm-reduction recommendation. Empirical limit: population compensation pattern; not identical dose in every smoker. Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.
- E-10 — trial odds versus how people try. Research unit SEU-004. Sources S-077, S-078. Grade CONTESTED as a single slogan; both component facts SUPPORTED in their own scopes. Reader line: licensed NRT vs control raises 6+ month abstinence (pooled RR 1.55); most smokers who try to quit do so unassisted, with unaided success around 7–8%. Scope: trial efficacy versus population attempt pattern; different questions. Permitted inference: NRT can raise odds versus placebo in trials; most attempts are still unmedicated; neither fact means the reader cannot quit, and neither fact is a prescription. Prohibited inference: "you cannot quit without patches" or "cold turkey never works." The two statistics answer different questions and must not be collapsed. Empirical limit: motivated trial quitters versus population attempts; not lifetime fate. Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.
- E-11 — withdrawal short peak. Research unit SEU-005. Sources S-009, S-081. Grade SUPPORTED as population typical, not every reader. Reader line: withdrawal symptoms typically peak in the first few days and usually subside within a few weeks; they may begin within hours; MedlinePlus places the sharpest edge about 2–3 days after last use. Scope: days-two-to-three irritability, craving, restlessness. Permitted inference: the restless empty feeling is withdrawal pharmacology with a short typical peak, not proof the cigarette was the real self. Prohibited inference: withdrawal is weeks of physical torture for everyone, or that it is harmless for every person in every circumstance. Empirical limit: typical course; genetics and heaviness shift severity. Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.
- E-12 — starters hooked, first times unpleasant. Research unit bank-direct, no LEU/SEU; packets bank-08 V-017 and bank-07 M-012. Source S-090. Grade SUPPORTED as internal planning statement plus cited paper finding. Finding: starters begin for image, conformity, defiance and experiment, not a pre-existing craving; first experiences are often unpleasant until tolerance develops. Scope: recruitment versus maintenance. Permitted inference: the first cigarettes were endured for image and belonging, then tolerance taught the need. Prohibited inference: every starter had the same motive, or that unpleasant first times prove anyone is safe to experiment. Empirical limit: historical planning memo plus youth finding; verify before plan-freeze language is spent — use only as stated. Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.
- E-13 — cues outlive chemistry. Research unit bank-direct, no LEU/SEU; packets bank-07 M-006 and M-026. Source S-009 with NCI withdrawal fact sheet scope. Grade SUPPORTED. Finding: feel, smell, sight and lighting ritual can worsen craving even when some physiology is covered; later wanting can be a learned trigger, not ongoing nicotine need. Scope: conditioned cues distinct from acute withdrawal. Permitted inference: the after-coffee or after-meal want can be a learned cue; its presence does not prove the body still needs nicotine. Prohibited inference: all cravings forever mean physical dependence persists, or that cue exposure must be avoided by hermit quitting. Empirical limit: cue effects vary; not every quitter feels every cue. Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.
