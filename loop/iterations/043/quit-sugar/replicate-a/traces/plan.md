# Master Plan — Quit Sugar (ITER 043, replicate-a, candidate r1)

## 1. Book core

- Target behavior: compulsive consumption of BAD SUGAR — refined/added free sugars and junk carbs eaten in the craving-snacking loop — and the grip of that loop, not nutrition pedantry.
- Reader (planner-facing state): an adult who grazes, binges, or both; has failed at diets, moderation rules, and willpower; suspects the whole approach is wrong and is reading in secret hope mixed with open skepticism.
- Load-bearing false belief (one sentence): Bad sugar is a genuine treat and lift that makes life sweeter, and life without it would be deprivation.
- Through-line: every page reassigns one more drop of the treat's credit to its true source until nothing is left to miss, so stopping reads as escape from a con, never sacrifice of a pleasure.
- Format: full-length Easyway book, front matter plus fifteen chapters, circa 65,000 words, one belief-move per chapter, short recap to close.
- Fork 1 (inner state): full Carr personification. The trivial physical craver is the Sugar Mite; the belief-system that feeds it is the Treat Spell. Both named in passing in CH-02, then permanent vocabulary.
- Fork 2 (outcome): total cessation of BAD SUGAR, commanded with cheerful certainty. Moderation foreclosed by the pincer and the cliff image. The vow is instructed; readiness is gated, then crossed joyfully.
- Fork 3 (science weight): hard facts delivered at full force inside the argument, then relieved in the same chapter by the escape. Facts arrive as settled flat statements honoring evidence limits; raw citations quarantined to back matter. The harm account is never routed to an appendix.
- Fork 4 (villain): two villains, both hit hard — the engineered food industry that built the trap (bliss-point formulation, stomach-share business, child targeting, suppressed science) and the named anti-method, the Willpower Method, which kept the reader in it and owns every past failure.
- Fork 5 (void): change nothing else. The body returns to its natural baseline on its own; real food, hunger, and instinct are the positive authority met in the first third. No replacement system, no environment-redesign program.
- Redefinition: BAD SUGAR names the bad subset — added and free sugars plus refined junk carbs (sweets, pastries, sugary drinks, processed snacks, sugar-free lookalikes that keep the loop). Fruit, vegetables, plain staples, and ordinary meals stay; they are the positive authority's menu, not a concession. The decree issues in CH-01 and the CAPS name carries the definition in every later sentence.
- Margin-for-error doctrine: the body copes with an occasional accidental blip, but the mind must not reopen the question; a slip revives nothing unless the Treat Spell is let back in. Seatbelt logic: the belt exists for accidents, not for driving erratically. Stated once in CH-13, guarded in CH-14.
- Clinical and eating-disorder safety perimeter: general-adult edition only. Readers on glucose-lowering medication or insulin, readers who are pregnant, and readers with a current or past eating disorder are routed by the boxed plan-wide advisory to involve their clinician; the book teaches no medical nutrition therapy, no diabetes management, no weight-loss mechanics, and offers no crisis counseling. Advisory defined once as CA-01, printed boxed in front matter, one plain spoken sentence on the vow card, nowhere else.
- Strongest pro-behavior scene: the wedding dessert table under candles, the shared cake with beloved people, everyone happy — met head-on in CH-11.
- Destination state: the reader who, whenever BAD SUGAR crosses their mind, feels relief and freedom at not taking it — happy to be free, free from day one of the vow, guarding the belief instead of the behavior.
- Saved fresh ending reframe (debuts only in the final movement): you have not lost your sweet tooth — you have got your taste back.
