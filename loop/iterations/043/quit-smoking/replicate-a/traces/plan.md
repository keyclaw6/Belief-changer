# Master Plan — Quit Smoking

## 1. Book core

Target behavior: compulsive cigarette smoking. Total stop at a last cigarette, then none. Not cutting down, not vaping as a destination, not NRT as the method.

Reader (planner-facing state): an adult who smokes daily, wants to stop, has failed by willpower, patches, gum or cold turkey, and believes life without cigarettes would be miserable or impossible.

Load-bearing false belief (frozen): Cigarettes calm me, help me cope and give me pleasure, and life without them would be deprivation.

Through-line: The cigarette never gave you anything; it only briefly quieted a craving it created. See that and stopping is escape, not loss.

Format: full-length book, 16 cards (front matter + 13 numbered chapters + ordinary-life + short recap), 60000 words planned.

Fork decisions (Carr default throughout; brief concurs):
- F1 mechanism characters: full Carr personification. The little creature is the Ember Mite, a trivial physical nicotine hunger that complains faintly and starves within days. The big creature is the Smokescreen, the lifelong brainwashing that reads the Mite's twinge as proof of pleasure, relief or need. No no-monster override.
- F2 outcome: total cessation, commanded with cheerful certainty. Last ordinary cigarette, then none. Moderation foreclosed with the pincer (if there is no benefit in much, there is none in little) and the cliff image.
- F3 science weight: hard facts plainly and frighteningly where true, then the reader is told not to change from fear; the escape carries the change. Nicotine creates the need it relieves; bodily recovery is brief; the trap is mental. Citations quarantined to back matter; consequence lists and the opening scare stay in the argument, never in an appendix.
- F4 villains: two, both hit hard. The industry and trap that designed the dispenser and retained the would-quit, and the Willpower Method that sold quitting as suffering and blamed the reader. The reader is never blamed.
- F5 void: natural baseline. The non-smoker has no desire to smoke; the body returns on its own. Change nothing else in life. No replacement ritual as the method, no root-craving extension.

Redefinition: not needed. Smoking is quitable wholesale. No Good-X/Bad-X line, no CAPS bad subset, no margin-for-error buffer. One puff re-arms the Mite because it re-admits the belief; a slip revives nothing unless the belief is let back in.

Safety perimeter: no medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis or illness pointers only. The last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial attention, not a dare to smoke more. Pregnancy-specific, disease-treatment and youth-prevention material are out of scope. Plan-wide advisory CA-A is defined once in section 5, printed boxed in front matter, and cited on exactly two cards (front matter and vow).

Strongest pro-behavior scene: the perfect after-dinner coffee on a warm evening with people you love, the cigarette believed to seal it. Met head-on in C11.

Destination state: whenever the thought of a cigarette arises, the reader feels relief and freedom at being free; happy to be free, not merely abstinent.

Saved ending reframe (withheld until the close): the cigarette was rent you paid for a room you already owned. Appears only in C14/C15, never earlier.

Method preserved: escape not sacrifice on every page; warm to the person and vicious to the trap, never contempt for the reader; no willpower as solution; fear raised in the corpus way where assigned and returned to escape-joy in the same chapter; freedom conferred immediately at belief change and at the vow; the reader follows all instructions as their own smart choice; original prose throughout.

Reader line: If anything stressful happens in my life, a cigarette takes care of it.
Units: LEU-001. Sources: bank-02 B-001; S-001. Grade: n/a (lived account).
Scope and context: stressful moment; hand already reaching; first puff named as taking care of it.

Prohibited inference: that cigarettes treat the underlying problem, or that stress without a cigarette is medically dangerous.

EV-07 — ten-second hit and rapid fade.
Reader line: When cigarette smoke enters the lungs, nicotine is absorbed rapidly in the blood and delivered quickly to the brain, so that nicotine levels peak within 10 seconds of inhalation. But the acute effects of nicotine also dissipate quickly, along with the associated feelings of reward; this rapid cycle causes the smoker to continue dosing to maintain the drug's pleasurable effects and prevent withdrawal symptoms.
Units: SEU-001. Sources: bank-07 M-003; S-009. Grade: SUPPORTED.
Scope and context: a puff; credited calm timed to replenishment speed.
Permitted inference: the "relief" is replenishment of a rapidly fading dose, not a gift that solves the original stress.
Prohibited inference: every puff is a unique pleasure; "you cannot quit."
Empirical limit: smoked nicotine pharmacokinetics; no per-puff uniqueness claim and no cannot-quit claim.
Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.

EV-08 — product is nicotine.
Reader line: The cigarette should be conceived not as a product but as a package. the product is nicotine.
Units: SEU-002. Sources: bank-08 V-012; S-091. Grade: SUPPORTED (historical industry document).
Scope and context: pack, cigarette and puff treated by the manufacturer as layers around a dose.
Permitted inference: an industry scientist defined the cigarette as a nicotine package and dispenser, not as a flavour accessory.
Prohibited inference: quoting an internal memo as if it were a public-health consensus paper, or as a dare to smoke more to "see the product."
Empirical limit: internal planning statement, not a clinical trial and not a consumer brochure.
Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.

EV-09 — lights do not cut the dose.
Reader line: ventilated/"light" cigarettes do not reduce human intake; smokers compensate with bigger puffs.
Units: SEU-003. Sources: bank-07 M-010, M-018, M-037; S-010, S-080, S-089. Grade: SUPPORTED.
Scope and context: the mild or light cigarette as the moderate bargain.
Permitted inference: machine-measured lower tar/nicotine does not mean the smoker took less; compensation is the designed human response to a nicotine-seeking dose.
Prohibited inference: every smoker compensates identically, or that unventilated cigarettes are a harm-reduction recommendation.
Empirical limit: human smoking versus machine yields; no identical-compensation claim and no safer-cigarette recommendation.
Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.

EV-10 — trial odds versus how people try.
Reader line: licensed NRT vs control raises 6+ month abstinence (pooled RR 1.55); most smokers who try to quit do so unassisted, with unaided success around 7–8%.
Units: SEU-004. Sources: bank-07 M-013, M-015, M-039; S-077, S-078. Grade: CONTESTED as a single slogan; both component facts SUPPORTED in their own scopes.
Scope and context: failed quitter told patches are the method, or that unaided stopping never works.
Permitted inference: NRT can raise odds versus placebo in trials; most attempts are still unmedicated; neither fact means the reader cannot quit, and neither fact is a prescription.
Prohibited inference: "you cannot quit without patches" or "cold turkey never works." The two statistics answer different questions and must not be collapsed.
Empirical limit: motivated trial quitters for the RR; population attempts for the unaided rate; neither licenses a cannot-quit or a prescription claim.
Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.

EV-11 — short peak, not a new personality.
Reader line: withdrawal symptoms typically peak in the first few days and usually subside within a few weeks; they may begin within hours; MedlinePlus places the sharpest edge about 2–3 days after last use.
Units: SEU-005. Sources: bank-07 M-004, M-005, M-021, M-029; S-009, S-081. Grade: SUPPORTED (population typical, not every reader).
Scope and context: day two or three free; irritability, craving, restlessness.
Permitted inference: the restless empty feeling is withdrawal pharmacology with a short typical peak, not proof the cigarette was the real self.
Prohibited inference: withdrawal is weeks of physical torture for everyone, or that it is harmless for every person in every circumstance.
Empirical limit: population typical course; not an hour-by-hour promise for one reader and not a harmless-for-all claim.
Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.

Body-count and disease-catalogue material is held under evidence-unavailable treatment: the book does not argue from population death totals, which the synthesis limits against using as the method of change. The opening scare is the lived enslavement plus the short-peak pharmacology owned by C01, returned to escape-joy in the same chapter.
