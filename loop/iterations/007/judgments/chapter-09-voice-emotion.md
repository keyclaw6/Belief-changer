### Voice Gap 1: Instruction framed as chapter craft

**Our passage:** "This is your instruction for this chapter, and I give it to you warmly, at the climax where every loophole is closed."

**Real passage:** "*TWELFTH INSTRUCTION: GL IS ONLY A GUIDE. TO BE SURE THAT YOU ARE FREE FROM BAD SUGAR ADDICTION, CUT OUT REFINED SUGAR ITEMS, PROCESSED CARB ITEMS AND STARCHY CARB ITEMS (THIS INCLUDES PRETTY MUCH ALL READY-MADE MEALS AND PROCESSED FOODS)*"

**What Carr's voice does here:** Delivers the directive as relief from a trusted voice already inside the reader's head — no announcement that a "instruction moment" has arrived, no map of the chapter's dramatic structure. The reader feels equipped, not briefed.

**Where our voice fails:** The sentence reads as a narrator explaining where we are in the manuscript ("at the climax where every loophole is closed"). That creates distance and a faint compliance/docent register — the reader is being shown the machinery of the book at the exact moment they should feel decisively handed the method.

**Initial suspicion (speculative):** plan-card — the instruction slot is being signposted explicitly rather than enacted as lived direction.

### Voice Gap 3: Fourth-wall break at the cliff

**Our passage:** "Let me give you the image Carr would have drawn for this."
