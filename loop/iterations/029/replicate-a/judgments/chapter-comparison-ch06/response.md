MOVE G13-M1: PRESENT — Carr inhabits fruit and natural food as mankind’s true favourite through baby/chimp evidence and the promise that you will eat your favourite food freely; ours does the same job by walking the reader hungry through market, noon kitchen, soup, bread, and peach until hunger is met, satisfaction is felt, and the stop lands clean. Evidence: “Eat what you want, when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and never punish yourself again.” and “You ate what you loved, when hunger called, and stopped when hunger was answered. That is eating as favourite.”

MOVE G13-M2: PRESENT — Carr names the suspicion that “favourite food” was wordplay, then turns it: there is no con, the genuine pleasure was always in natural food; ours names the parallel grey fear that joy lived in the wrapper, then proves through lived eating that the pleasure is real and nothing is being taken away. Evidence: “Do you truly believe joy lived in the wrapper?” and “If the joy was never in the dose, no joy leaves when dosing leaves.”

MOVE G13-M3: PRESENT — Carr reframes fruit and vegetables not as the boring healthy fallback but as what we were designed to love; ours reframes ordinary market shopping, home cooking, and a plain noon meal as the feast itself — freedom in daily clothes, not a consolation prize after sugar leaves. Evidence: “We were taught to call this nothing. Shopping. Cooking. Plain eating.” and “Come with me on an ordinary Saturday, because freedom is ordinary and marvellous from the first step.”
