**The book's journey in five sentences:** The reader enters half-hoping and half-scoffing, given an easy contract, BAD SUGAR line, and permission to keep eating while the belief work happens. Through the first third they are moved from self-blame to capture, empty benefit, and a killed Willpower Method; the middle escalates through fuel inversion, hunger authority, Nibbler/Sweet Con split, manufactured want, and head-on demolition of celebration, perception, and carve-outs. Peak demolition lands CH-07–CH-10 with creatures argued, aisles exposed, cinema and sofa reassigned, tea enactment, and totality. **Overall arc assessment:** Read as one cumulative book, this replicate completes the Carr inversion: the reader who believed sweets gave treat, fuel, comfort, and celebration should finish knowing the column is empty, the trap manufactured, and freedom is immediate escape with nothing sacrificed. The mantra system executes on schedule — all nine mantras and five frozen tokens appear verbatim at debuts and echoes; the instruction spine issues chapter by chapter and recaps complete in CH-13; M-I debuts CH-11 and hands over through CH-12–13. Escalation follows the plan curve (low entry, rising demolition CH-04–CH-10, freedom crescendo CH-11–13) without early peak or endgame maintenance (no streaks, white-knuckle recovery, or “stay strong” strategy — only post-freedom commands and joyful script). The largest cross-chapter weakness is semantic re-argument: the Monday–Friday yo-yo diary and the evening box/hand-before-decision scene each get full re-stagings in later chapters after CH-02 settled them, which flattens momentum slightly but never blocks the journey or breaks the mantra system. Against the GSBS skeleton, every planned belief-move is performed; the ending lands genuine relief and excitement, not sacrifice or ongoing vigilance.
