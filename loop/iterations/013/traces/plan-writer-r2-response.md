# `production-books/quit-sugar/master-plan.md`

## 1. BOOK CORE

**Target behavior (locked):** Compulsive consumption of BAD SUGAR — added/refined sugar, syrups, honey, sweetened drinks, and ultra-processed starchy/junk carbs that behave like sugar — as a craving-snacking loop. Not nutrition pedantry, not whole-food sweetness.

**One reader (human handle):** **Maya** — 38, runs on tea/biscuits and the 3pm chocolate, hides the empty packet, has done every Monday diet, tells herself "just a treat" while knowing she is not choosing. She wants to stop without willpower and without feeling deprived.

**Load-bearing false belief (one sentence):** *BAD SUGAR gives me a genuine pleasure, treat, comfort and energy lift that makes life sweeter and I would be deprived without it.*

**Through-line:** Escape not sacrifice. Every page reframes stopping as *escaping a con that was doing nothing FOR you*, never as giving up a pleasure.

**Format:** Full-length Carr — ~60,000 words, 14 chapters + front matter, short recap close. Body, instinct and real food underfoot from the first third; freedom language crescendos in the final quarter; demolition vocabulary peaks mid-book.

**Fork decisions (explicit):**

* **F1 — Inner monsters:** DEFAULT CARR — **full personification.** Two original characters frozen as vocabulary: **the Nibbler** (the trivial physical creature, the little sugar gremlin to starve) and **the Confectioner** (the belief-system that feeds it, the sweet story). Never "it was me all along."
* **F2 — Abstinence vs autonomy:** DEFAULT CARR — **total cessation inside the line.** BAD SUGAR = zero is the only stable state. Pincer ("if there's no benefit often, there's no point occasionally") + cliff-jump forecloses moderation. Autonomy is preserved by the redefinition — GOOD food remains unlimited.
* **F3 — Science weight:** DEFAULT CARR — **hard facts at full Carr force, then relief.** Vivid, flat, unhedged where true (metabolic, dental, mood, trap mechanism) — then explicitly disowned as the motivator: you don't quit *from* fear, you quit *to* be free. Facts arrive as "The fact is…" never as a literature review; citations quarantined.
* **F4 — Villain:** DEFAULT CARR — **two villains, both hit hard:** the sugar/food industry that engineered bliss-point craving + **the Willpower Method** (named anti-method) that kept Maya failing. Warm to Maya, vicious to the trap and the wrong method.
* **F5 — Void:** DEFAULT CARR — **change nothing else in your life.** The body returns to its natural baseline on its own via real food; no elaborate replacement system. Inhabit every old context confidently to prove freedom.

**Redefinition & margin-for-error doctrine:**
Boxed ch.1 decree: **When I say sugar, take it to mean BAD SUGAR.** Defined once, named in CAPS thereafter — naming *is* the repetition.

* **BAD SUGAR** = all added/free sugars (sucrose, glucose, syrups, honey, sweetened drinks, jams, confectionery, pastries, white bread, crisps and any ultra-processed starchy carb formulated to hit the bliss point).
* **GOOD** = water, whole fruit in whole form, vegetables, meat/fish/eggs, dairy if tolerated, whole grains/pulses eaten as real food — eaten to appetite via body/instinct.

**Margin for error (seatbelt image):** Your body can cope with an occasional, accidental trace — but your mind cannot. One deliberate "just one" feeds the Confectioner and reawakens the Nibbler; you haven't blown your health, you've re-lit the belief. Guard the belief, not the bite. No catastrophizing, no license.

**Clinical / eating-disorder safety perimeter (plan-wide):**
Boxed advisory CA-SAFE (see §5) routes all medical, diabetes, pregnancy, eating-disorder and weight-loss-therapy questions to a clinician. Method territory = belief-change about BAD SUGAR; crisis-pointer territory = diagnosis, prescribing, meal-plan therapy. No suggestion that quitting BAD SUGAR treats disease; no body-shame, no compensatory purging, no calorie-counting.

**Strongest pro-behavior scene (met head-on, late):** Maya's **Friday-night sofa reward** — kids finally asleep, tea steaming, the biscuit tin open on her lap; soft lamps, quiet house, the feeling *this is the one thing today that is mine.* Alternative full-cast version saved for the book's test: the **summer birthday table** — everyone laughing around a cake that seems to *be* the celebration.

**Destination state:** Whenever Maya thinks of BAD SUGAR she feels *relief and freedom that she no longer feeds it* — pity for users still trapped, joy at a dead enemy, not envy; free as of the vow on day one, not after a countdown.

**One fresh ending reframe (saved):** **You didn't give up sweetness — you got your taste back.** The whole book was never about quitting a food; it was about recovering the pleasure your tongue already owned before the trap muted it. Revealed only in the final movement.
