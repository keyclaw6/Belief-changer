### I WAS YOU

I know how you come to this page, Maya.

You are 38, you are clever, you are tired. You run on tea and biscuits and that 3pm chocolate that you tell yourself is just a little lift. You have hidden the empty packet at the bottom of the bin. You have started again on Monday more times than you can count. You have told yourself "just a treat" while knowing, in that quieter, honest moment, that you are not choosing at all.

And you are sceptical. I know.

If someone tells you that you can stop compulsively eating BAD SUGAR easily, immediately and permanently, the sensible part of you thinks: too good to be true. That was my reaction too. I lived inside that loop for years. I believed BAD SUGAR helped me cope, that it smoothed the edges, that life without it would be greyer and harder. I tried to be good. I tried to be strong. I kept failing and I blamed myself.

Then I got free. Not by grit. Not by white-knuckling through cravings. By seeing something so plain I wonder how I missed it for so long. Since then I have watched person after person — people who came in saying "this will never work for me" — walk out free on the first day. Not months later. Day one.

That is why you can trust what I tell you here. I am not speaking to you as someone above you. I am speaking as someone who was conned exactly as you were, and who escaped. We've all been there — reaching for a dose we swear we want, feeling worse after it, and doing it again anyway. I will be warm to you the whole way. I will be vicious to the trap that did this to you.

And I need you to feel something else before we argue anything at all: excitement.

This is not a punishment. This is an escape. What you are about to do is get on with enjoying your life with more energy, more calm, and more real taste than the trap ever let you have. Cast aside doom and gloom right now. There is no need to be miserable here. You are about to see through a con that has had its hand in your pocket for years.

That is why I can make you two promises in the first few pages and mean them completely.

First: you have nothing to lose and everything to gain.

Think about it. If I am right, you walk away from slavery to a junk that was doing nothing FOR you, and you get your freedom, your appetite, your afternoons back. If I am wrong — if by some miracle BAD SUGAR really was a genuine friend — you can always go back to it. But you know the truth, don't you? You are not happy as you are. You want to be free. So wonder beats cynicism here. Allow yourself to wonder, just for this read, what if it really could be easy?

Second: you can get free easily, immediately and permanently.

Not after thirty days. Not once you have earned it. Easily, immediately and permanently — from the moment the belief changes, which is from the moment you finish this book and make one simple, joyful vow. I state that with total confidence because it is how this method works. You don't need to believe me yet. You only need to keep reading with an open mind and let the arguments do their job. The feeling will follow the seeing.

Which brings me to the only thing I will ever ask you to do by effort. It is not to resist. It is not to be strong.

All you have to do is follow all the instructions.

Not most of them. All of them. They are small, they are kind, and they are the rails that carry you out. Follow them as written and freedom takes care of itself.

### THE CON, NOT THE CHARACTER

Let me remove shame right now, because shame is one of the trap's favourite ropes.

You were not foolish. You were conned.

Picture the con trick as it works in the world. Someone warm, credible, well-dressed sells you a fraudulent investment. The brochure is glossy. The offices are beautiful. Everyone you trust says "everyone does it." You hand over money because you were given phoney information and every cue said this was smart and normal. Later, when you discover the fund never existed, do you call yourself greedy or stupid? No. You were conned on phoney information by people who profit from your trust.

This is exactly what happened to you with BAD SUGAR.

We were told from childhood that sweet is love, sweet is reward, sweet is comfort, sweet is fuel. Adverts sold it with smiling families. Kitchens sold it with "you've earned it." The bliss-point was engineered in labs to hit the cannot-stop spot, and the result was placed at every till, in every meeting room, in the drawer of every tired parent at 3pm. Then, when we found we couldn't stop, we were handed a second con to keep us failing: the Willpower Method.

The Willpower Method says you are weak and must fight yourself forever. It says freedom is sacrifice, that you must resist, count days, stay strong, beat cravings by force. So you tried. You were good all week. You kept the packet out of the house and then ate your children's sweets at 10pm standing at the fridge. You cut down and found each dose became more precious. You told yourself next Monday would be different. Each failure then proved what the Willpower Method told you: you have no discipline.

The fact is you were never weak. You were outgunned by an engineered trap and then handed the wrong method for getting out. No wonder you failed. This time is different because we will not fight the trap on its terms. We will see it.

Here is the liberating flat verdict: the desire you feel is not yours. It was installed. And anything installed on phoney information can be removed by true information.

You may have heard that some people worry they will be lost without BAD SUGAR, that the house will feel empty, that they will sit with the open door of freedom and feel too frightened to step through. There is a creature in a zoo who lives it. The keeper opens the cage after years and the lion hovers at the threshold. The door is open. The plains are there. But the habit of the cage hums in his body so loudly he mistakes habit for need. He hovers, not because he loves the cage, but because he has lived in it so long he has forgotten what his legs are for.

We will come back to that lion. For now, just know: the hovering you feel is not proof you need the trap. It is proof you have lived in it. And the moment you see the door is truly open, the hovering ends.

### THE WORD THAT SETS YOU FREE

Before we go further I have to define a word, because the whole book turns on it. I will define it once, box it, and then use it in capitals so you always hear the definition inside the word.

> **WHEN I SAY SUGAR, TAKE IT TO MEAN BAD SUGAR.**
>
> **BAD SUGAR =** all added/free sugars — sucrose, glucose, syrups, honey, sweetened drinks, jams, confectionery, pastries, white bread, crisps and any ultra-processed starchy carb formulated to hit the bliss point. These are doses of junk, not food. They are poison at the bliss point, engineered to make you feed again.
>
> **GOOD =** water, whole fruit in whole form, vegetables, meat/fish/eggs, dairy if tolerated, whole grains/pulses eaten as real food — eaten to appetite via your body, your instinct and real food. This is eating. It needs no company to make it work.

Think of it like a seatbelt. Your body can cope with an occasional, accidental trace — a crumb in a sauce when you are out — but your mind cannot cope with one deliberate "just one." One deliberate bite does not ruin your health, but it re-lights the belief that there is something in BAD SUGAR worth having, and that belief reawakens the wanting. So we guard the belief, not the bite. No catastrophizing. No license. Just clarity: we eat as much real food as we like, whenever we like, and never count, crave or punish yourself again. Freedom is not a diet. It is an escape into eating that actually satisfies you.

You don't need to memorise this. Just hear it whenever you see the word: BAD SUGAR always means the con. GOOD always means the life you already know how to live.

### THREE AGREEMENTS — THE ONLY COST OF FREEDOM

We now make the contract. Three agreements. They are small. They are non-negotiable. They are how you keep the mind open long enough for the belief to change.

I will give them to you as commands, because that is what they are — kind, cheerful commands from someone who knows the way out and wants you on the plains, not hovering at the cage door. Each has its reason right beside it, no lecture attached.

1. KEEP AN OPEN MIND — wonder beats willpower

2. DON'T CUT DOWN OR QUIT UNTIL I TELL YOU — so belief changes first

3. BEGIN WITH ELATION — you are already escaping

That's it.

Why must you keep an open mind? Because the trap survives on one hidden belief: that BAD SUGAR gives you a genuine pleasure, treat or lift. We will demolish that together. But demolition only works if you allow the questions to land. So when I ask you a trap question — "Do you enjoy the taste so much that you add frosting to plain chicken the way you add sugar to mask tea?" — answer honestly. If you find yourself tightening, just remember you have nothing to lose and everything to gain and let the wonder back in.

Why must you not cut down or quit until I tell you? Because cutting down while the belief is intact makes BAD SUGAR more precious, makes each hit feel like relief, and guarantees the next binge. We change the wanting first. The doing then stops on its own, without a battle. So carry on exactly as you do now while you read. No secret reduction. No test of strength.

Why begin with elation? Because dread is part of the brainwashing. The Willpower Method taught you that stopping is deprivation and will hurt. It won't. From this page forward, you are not preparing to lose a friend; you are preparing to see that this friend was the one who kept pushing you in and then offering a hand to pull you half out. You are not waiting to become free after a white-knuckle month. You are free the moment you see it clearly. So let yourself be excited now. That excitement keeps the door open.

If you can give me those three, I can give you everything else.

All you have to do is follow all the instructions — and you have just read the first three.

You have guessed the direction now, haven't you? That BAD SUGAR was never doing anything FOR you, that the Willpower Method was the second half of the con, and that the escape is not about becoming someone new but about stepping out of a cage that was never locked?

Good. Keep that wondering alive.
