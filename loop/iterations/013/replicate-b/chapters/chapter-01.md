### WHY YOU ARE HERE, AND WHY THIS TIME IS DIFFERENT

Maya, if you are reading this, you are exhausted.

You run on tea and biscuits. You do the 3pm chocolate. You hide the empty packet at the bottom of the bin so no one sees. You tell yourself "just a treat" while knowing you are not choosing. You have done every Monday diet. You have told yourself you lack discipline. You have promised yourself you will be good next week.

I know. We've all been there.

I lived in that same loop for years. I believed BAD SUGAR was my comfort, my treat, my little lift that made life sweeter. I believed I would be deprived without it. I tried to be strong. I tried to control it. And every attempt ended the same way — another empty box, another quiet shame.

Then I saw it for what it was. And the moment I saw it, it was over. Not after weeks of white-knuckling. Straight away. I am not writing to you as someone who mastered willpower. I am writing as someone who escaped and then helped thousands of others walk out the same open door. The fact that you are sceptical that this could be easy is perfect. That was my reaction too. Keep that scepticism — and bring curiosity with it.

You do not need more discipline. You do not need to become a different person. You need to see the trap.

Which brings us to the method that kept you failing. Its name is the Willpower Method, and this book will be vicious to it. It tells you that stopping is about sacrifice, that you must fight yourself, count days, and prove how strong you are. The Willpower Method is the con's best friend. It guarantees you feel deprived, and a person who feels deprived always goes back.

We will not use it. Not once. Be warm to yourself. Be harsh to the trap.

And here is the promise that makes reading on safe. You have spent years worrying that if you stop you will lose something. The truth is the opposite. You have nothing to lose and everything to gain. That is not a pep talk. It is the literal accounting of what BAD SUGAR does. It takes. It does not give. When you stop feeding it, you do not lose a pleasure. You escape a con.

You can stop eating BAD SUGAR easily, immediately and permanently. I say that with total certainty because I have seen it happen again and again, for people who were sure they were the exception. The ease is not that you are strong enough. The ease is that there is nothing to fight once the belief that kept you feeding it is gone.

All you have to do is follow all the instructions. That is it. Not most of them. Not the ones that suit you. All of them, in order, as written. They are not heavy. They are small agreements that keep your mind open long enough for the belief to change. If you follow them, the freedom follows.

And freedom, here, means something precise. Not that you abstain and feel deprived. Not that you manage a habit. That you feel relief whenever BAD SUGAR crosses your mind that you no longer feed it. That is the destination: happy to be free, pity for the trapped, not envy.

### THE CON THAT STOLE YOUR CHOICE

We were conned.

Think of the classic con trick. A smooth operator sells you a fraudulent investment on phoney information. He shows you glossy charts, promises ease, tells you everyone is doing it. You hand over your money not because you are foolish, but because the information you were given was false. Later, when you discover the papers were forged, you do not blame yourself for being weak. You realize you were deceived.

That is exactly what happened with BAD SUGAR.

We did not choose this loop freely. We chose the first sweet drink, the first biscuit from the tin, on phoney information — that it was a treat, that it was joy, that it was fuel. The family table told us so. The adverts told us so. The bright wrappers told us so. The craving itself, once installed, told us so. We made a reasonable decision on bad information, and then the trap removed our choice.

Do you want proof you are not freely choosing? Let me ask you: if you truly wanted to stop a hobby you genuinely enjoy, would you need a book on how to stop it?

Of course you want to stop. That is why you are here. The very fact you are reading this proves the other voice — the one that says "I could stop if I wanted" — is part of the con.

I want to show you another picture. A lion born in a cage who has lived behind bars his whole life. One day the keeper leaves the door wide open. The plains are right there. The lion could walk out. But he hovers by the open door, pacing, anxious, convinced he still needs the cage. His anxiety is not proof he belongs inside. It is cage-habit.

You have felt that hover. The nervousness at the idea of life without your fix. That feeling is not need. It is the habit of the trap talking. Keep that picture lightly in your mind. We will come back to it when the door is fully open.

So let us be clear now, warmly and firmly: you are not weak. You were outgunned by an engineered trap and then blamed for losing. That blame was another part of the con.

### WHEN I SAY SUGAR, TAKE IT TO MEAN BAD SUGAR

Before we go further, we need a clean line. Otherwise we will argue about the wrong thing.

> **WHEN I SAY SUGAR, TAKE IT TO MEAN BAD SUGAR.**
>
> **BAD SUGAR** = all added/free sugars (sucrose, glucose, syrups, honey, sweetened drinks, jams, confectionery, pastries, white bread, crisps and any ultra-processed starchy carb formulated to hit the bliss point).
>
> **GOOD** = water, whole fruit in whole form, vegetables, meat/fish/eggs, dairy if tolerated, whole grains/pulses eaten as real food — eaten to appetite via your body, your instinct and real food.
>
> Your body can cope with an occasional, accidental trace — but your mind cannot. One deliberate "just one" feeds the Confectioner and reawakens the Nibbler; you haven't blown your health, you've re-lit the belief. Guard the belief, not the bite. No catastrophizing, no license. Think of a seatbelt: it is there for accidental bumps, not so you can drive erratically.

That is the line for the rest of your life. Outside it, there is no restriction. Inside it, there is zero benefit. You are not reading this book to quit sweetness. You are reading it to quit the con that muted it. And the wonderful clause that goes with this line is this: eat as much real food as you like, whenever you like, and never count, crave or punish yourself again. That is not a slogan. It is the natural baseline your body returns to when you stop interrupting it.

> **CA-SAFE — PRACTICAL SAFETY GUARDRAIL.** This book changes a belief, not a medical treatment. If you have diabetes, take medication affecting blood sugar, have an eating disorder, or are under medical advice about food, do not change medication, dosage or prescribed eating solely on the basis of this book. Speak to your clinician about any health steps. If food feels out of control or distressing, seek support promptly. This notice is not part of the belief argument.

### HOW TO READ THIS BOOK — THREE AGREEMENTS

Every person who found this easy did three small things while they read. They cost nothing. They protect the belief-change while it happens. By agreeing now, you give yourself the same chance.

I am going to state them as plain commands, because clarity is kindness. Each comes with its reason — one short line, no lecture.

1. KEEP AN OPEN MIND — wonder beats willpower
You do not have to believe me yet. Just allow that your settled view might be the brainwashing talking, and let the argument do its work. An open mind here is not gullibility — it is the investigator's mind.

2. DON'T CUT DOWN OR QUIT UNTIL I TELL YOU — so belief changes first
I know this sounds strange. But do not try to reduce or stop BAD SUGAR while you are reading. Carry on exactly as you were. The trap survives by making you feel deprived between doses. If you start fighting it now, you will close your mind to the very reframe that makes quitting easy.

3. BEGIN WITH ELATION — you are already escaping
Cast aside all feelings of doom and gloom. There is no need to be miserable. You are not about to lose a friend. You are about to see an enemy clearly and walk away. From this page on, you are already escaping. Let yourself feel that relief now.

That is it. Three agreements. You have nothing to lose and everything to gain by keeping them, and you keep them easily, immediately and permanently by simply doing as they say while you read. All you have to do is follow all the instructions — and you have just agreed to the first three.

You may be thinking: is this really possible for me? After all those Mondays?

Yes. Because the difficulty was never in you. It was in the method you were given and the story you were sold. Once the story changes, the wanting changes. Once the wanting changes, the behavior follows without a struggle. You do not have to wait to feel ready. You only have to keep the door open and keep walking through the pages.

We will not rush. The trap will be named plainly. The belief that BAD SUGAR gives you a genuine pleasure will be examined until there is nothing left of it. The energy lie, the treat lie, the moderation lie, the engineering behind your craving, the hard truth and then the relief — each will have its turn. Your job, right now, is simple: stay open, stay elated, keep reading as you are.
