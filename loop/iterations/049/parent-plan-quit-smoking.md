# Master Plan — Quit Smoking (plan candidate)

## 1. Book core

- Target behavior: compulsive cigarette smoking ended by total stop. Last cigarette, then none. Not cutting down, not vaping as a destination, not NRT as the method.
- Reader (planner-facing state): an adult daily smoker who lights up through stress, meals, coffee, drinks, breaks, and waiting; has failed before by resolve, patches, gum, or abrupt suffering; believes cigarettes calm, help, reward, and please; dreads life without them as empty and impossible.
- Load-bearing false belief (frozen): Cigarettes relax me, help me cope, and are my pleasure, and I would be deprived without them.
- Through-line: stopping is escape from a con, not sacrifice of a gift. Every chapter makes continuing feel like the ongoing burden and stopping feel like reclamation.
- Format: full-length belief-change book, fifteen chapters, about 60,000 words. One clear adult edition.
- Fork decisions:
  - Fork 1 (inner state): full Carr personification. The trivial physical tug is named the Nipper; the belief-system that feeds it is named the Con. The Nipper starves on its own once the Con dies. Never a mighty enemy demanding resolve.
  - Fork 2 (outcome): total cessation, commanded with warm certainty. Moderation foreclosed by the pincer and the cliff image. No autonomy hedge on the destination.
  - Fork 3 (science weight): hard facts stated plainly at full force where the truth frightens, then the reader is told not to change from fear but from escape-joy. Facts arrive as settled statements, never as literature review. Raw source detail stays out of the argument.
  - Fork 4 (villain): two villains, both named hard: the nicotine industry that designed the dose and recruited the reader, and the Willpower Method that sold impossibility and kept every failure personal.
  - Fork 5 (void): change nothing else in life. The non-smoker baseline returns on its own. No replacement ritual as the method, no environment-redesign programme.
- Redefinition: not needed. Smoking is quitable wholesale; no Good-X/Bad-X line, no CAPS subset name, no margin-for-error doctrine.
- Clinical perimeter: plan-wide advisory CA-A (defined in section 5). No medical advice, no instruction to ignore a doctor, crisis and illness pointers only. The last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more.
- Strongest pro-smoking scene: the night-out smoking area with drinks among friends, the circle that feels like belonging itself.
- Destination state: the reader thinks of a cigarette and feels relief and gladness at being free, never deprivation; the thought converts at once into celebration.
- Ending reframe (saved for the final movement, used nowhere earlier): you bury an enemy, not a friend — grief would be the only mistake left, and there is nothing to grieve.

- EV-05 — reward for almost everything. Reader line: "Cigarettes were my reward for…well, almost everything. Teaching a class. Finishing a story. Finishing a paragraph. Driving 500 miles. Permitted inference: the "reward" can attach to almost any completed act; the party is the cigarette, not the work. Prohibited inference: that work or driving requires nicotine, or that quitting means life has no celebrations. Empirical limit: one writer's reward pattern generalized only as attachability. Safety limit: none beyond standard perimeter.
- EV-06 — just one after months. Reader line: "I know I can never have just one. I learned that the hard way, thinking after 5 months I could control myself and just have one. Nope." Research units: LEU-006. Source: S-006. Tier: lived, control-illusion warning. Scope and context: five months free; the moderate voice offers one; the chain returns. Permitted inference: a long quit does not make "just one" safe; the smoker who learned this the hard way names it as never. Prohibited inference: that a slip dooms the reader forever if the belief is not let back in; no dare to test "just one." Empirical limit: retrospective warning, not a doom sentence. Safety limit: slips pre-forgiven as warnings; never licensed as experiments.
- EV-07 — ten-second hit, rapid fade. Reader line: "When cigarette smoke enters the lungs, nicotine is absorbed rapidly in the blood and delivered quickly to the brain, so that nicotine levels peak within 10 seconds of inhalation. But the acute effects of nicotine also dissipate quickly, along with the associated feelings of reward; this rapid cycle causes the smoker to continue dosing to maintain the drug's pleasurable effects and prevent withdrawal symptoms." Research units: SEU-001. Source: S-009. Grade: SUPPORTED. Scope and context: smoked-nicotine pharmacokinetics; the credited calm timed to replenishment speed. Permitted inference: the "relief" is replenishment of a rapidly fading dose, not a gift that solves the original stress. Prohibited inference: every puff is a unique pleasure; "you cannot quit." Empirical limit: population pharmacokinetics, not a per-puff pleasure meter. Safety limit: standard perimeter.
- EV-08 — product is nicotine. Reader line: "The cigarette should be conceived not as a product but as a package. the product is nicotine." Research units: SEU-002. Source: S-091. Grade: SUPPORTED (historical industry document). Scope and context: pack, cigarette, and puff treated by the manufacturer as layers around a dose. Permitted inference: an industry scientist defined the cigarette as a nicotine package and dispenser, not as a flavour accessory. Prohibited inference: quoting an internal memo as if it were a public-health consensus paper, or as a dare to smoke more to "see the product." Empirical limit: internal planning statement, not a clinical trial. Safety limit: no dare framing.
- EV-09 — lights do not cut the dose. Reader line: ventilated/"light" cigarettes do not reduce human intake; smokers compensate with bigger puffs. Research units: SEU-003. Sources: S-010, S-080, S-089. Grade: SUPPORTED. Scope and context: the mild cigarette as the moderate smoker's bargain; human smoking versus machine yields. Permitted inference: machine-measured lower tar/nicotine does not mean the smoker took less; compensation is the designed human response to a nicotine-seeking dose. Prohibited inference: every smoker compensates identically, or that unventilated cigarettes are a harm-reduction recommendation. Empirical limit: human compensation pattern, not identical dose in every smoker. Safety limit: no product recommendation of any kind.
- EV-10 — trial odds versus how people try. Reader line: licensed NRT vs control raises 6+ month abstinence (pooled RR 1.55); most smokers who try to quit do so unassisted, with unaided success around 7–8%. Research units: SEU-004. Sources: S-077, S-078. Grade: CONTESTED as a single slogan; both component facts SUPPORTED in their own scopes. Scope and context: the failed quitter told patches are the method, or that unaided stopping never works. Permitted inference: NRT can raise odds versus placebo in trials; most attempts are still unmedicated; neither fact means the reader cannot quit, and neither fact is a prescription. Prohibited inference: "you cannot quit without patches" or "cold turkey never works." The two statistics answer different questions and must not be collapsed. Empirical limit: trial odds and population attempt rates answer different questions. Safety limit: neither fact is a prescription; standard perimeter.
- EV-11 — withdrawal is a short peak. Reader line: withdrawal symptoms typically peak in the first few days and usually subside within a few weeks; they may begin within hours; MedlinePlus places the sharpest edge about 2–3 days after last use. Research units: SEU-005. Sources: S-009, S-081. Grade: SUPPORTED (population typical, not every reader). Scope and context: day two or three without a cigarette; irritability, craving, restlessness. Permitted inference: the restless empty feeling is withdrawal pharmacology with a short typical peak, not proof the cigarette was the real self. Prohibited inference: withdrawal is weeks of physical torture for everyone, or that it is harmless for every person in every circumstance. Empirical limit: population typical course, not an exact hour-by-hour promise. Safety limit: standard perimeter; vulnerable readers keep professional advice.
- EV-12 — daily cost already felt. Finding: cough and phlegm, stairs leaving the smoker winded, hourly clock, cold hands and feet, dulled smell and taste, the carried smell, the daily buy ritual. Bank packets: bank-03 L-001, L-004; bank-07 M-004, M-025. Sources: S-001, S-009. Grade: SUPPORTED for the withdrawal cluster; lived for the daily-cost items. Scope: dependent users and community daily-cost reports. Permitted inference: the restless empty feeling sits inside a known withdrawal cluster; the daily costs are already being paid. Prohibited inference: every quitter feels identical symptoms, or that listing symptoms predicts one reader's exact course. Empirical limit: cluster and community pattern, not a personal prognosis. Safety limit: standard perimeter.
- EV-13 — cues and the habit smoke. Finding: feel, smell, sight, and the lighting ritual can worsen craving even when some physiology is covered; the after-coffee and after-meal smokes are learned cues. Bank packets: bank-07 M-006, M-026. Source: S-009. Grade: SUPPORTED. Scope: conditioned craving distinct from acute withdrawal. Permitted inference: a later want-one may be a learned trigger, not an ongoing nicotine need. Prohibited inference: all cravings forever mean physical dependence persists. Empirical limit: cue effects vary by person and scene. Safety limit: no avoidance-as-resistance doctrine.
- EV-14 — industry design receipts. Finding: cigarettes designed as attractive nicotine dosage forms; starters begin for image, conformity, defiance, or experiment with often unpleasant first experiences until tolerance; filters and lights sold as reassurance while humans compensated; youth recruitment and retail scale. Bank packets: bank-08 V-001, V-004, V-005, V-016, V-017; bank-07 M-010, M-037, M-040. Sources: S-010, S-089, S-090. Grade: SUPPORTED as historical document and review findings. Scope: industry planning and marketing history. Permitted inference: the industry designed dose units and reassurance products; starters did not begin with a nicotine craving. Prohibited inference: quoting planning memos as clinical consensus, or identical compensation in every smoker. Empirical limit: historical and review scope, not a per-smoker dose claim. Safety limit: anger at the industry, never at the reader.
- EV-15 — consequence burden. Finding: tobacco kills more than eight million people each year including more than a million non-smokers from second-hand smoke; smoking causes about 480,000 premature deaths yearly in the United States; smoke exposes the smoker to thousands of chemicals including dozens that can cause cancer; even one cigarette per day over a lifetime can cause smoking-related cancers; there is no safe level. Bank packets: bank-07 M-035; bank-08 V-041, V-042, V-043, V-044. Sources: S-087 and institutional fact sheets via the synthesis. Grade: SUPPORTED. Scope: population burden, not individual fate. Permitted inference: the stakes of remaining in the trap are real. Prohibited inference: using body-count as the book's method of change, or as a dare, or as a personal sentence on the reader. Empirical limit: population burden; pack history correlates with illness severity but does not fix one reader's fate. Safety limit: facts stated once at full weight, then the book returns the reader to escape; never ends on fear.
- EV-16 — freedom testimonies arc. Finding: long-form escapes across all reader states — patio reclaimed without intrusive thoughts, nine years with breath and money returned, garage hider quit on a dog walk, lake-weekend relapse then freedom, twenty-attempt failure then a permanent quit, loved-every-cigarette then quit. Bank packets: bank-10 T-001, T-002, T-006, T-011, T-023. Sources: S-001, S-004, S-005, S-006. Tier: lived arcs, moment-of-revelation present. Scope: retrospective first-person escapes. Permitted inference: ordinary smokers with long histories and many failures have stopped completely and found ordinary life better. Prohibited inference: any one arc promises any reader's exact course or timeline. Empirical limit: testimony, not trial evidence. Safety limit: no method-book naming; original prose only.

## 5. Lexicon and instruction spine

Trap register (always for the behavior): dose, fix, hit, feed, the trap, the Con, the Nipper, con, brainwashing, slavery, escape, free. Cigarettes are doses of nicotine, never treats, rewards, friends, or pauses that help.

Freedom register (shown as lived moments named only by mantra lines): the clear morning, the meal closed clean, theGroup quiet aisle at the shops, breath taken easy on the stairs, the patio sitting empty of kit, money staying home. Ease and relief arrive inside concrete pictures, never as distributed adjectives.

Banned register: give up, resist, stay strong, discipline, abstain, sacrifice (except when naming the illusion of sacrifice), trying to stop, one day at a time, recovery journey, cut down (as advice), just this once. Quit itself is acceptable as a plain verb.

If you ever feel you may act on thoughts of harming yourself, stop reading and contact your local crisis line or clinician now.

## 6. Arc and length

Fifteen chapters. First third installs the world: easy contract and reading instructions, the reader already hooked, the Nipper and the Con named in passing when the trap is first seen, mornings and the body as concrete encounters. Middle demolishes on that ground: stress, pleasure, social belonging, concentration and boredom, then the inversion mechanism, then escape routes, then the industry indictment and the anti-method with fear collapsed. One chapter inhabits ordinary non-smoking doing as its primary job before the mechanism deepens. After the vow: the last cigarette as an ordinary instance with full attention, one ordinary-life chapter of mornings, shops, drinks, and driving, then a short photographable recap. Prevalence and attempt statistics appear once, in C-10. The embedded long testimony lives in the main flow inside C-12. Harm facts appear in the argument in C-01 and C-10 only, never in an appendix.

Concept debuts: contract and ease C-01; trap, creatures, sensory phrase, cost formula C-02; axis verdict and illusion token C-03; Nipper starvation logic C-08; totality token C-09; villain receipts C-10; anti-method and fear collapse C-11; terminal mantra and vow C-13; saved enemy-burial reframe C-14; recap C-15.

Freedom curve: promised C-01, suppressed through C-04 to C-11 demolition, rising C-12, detonated C-13 to C-15. Demolition curve: peaks C-04 to C-11, hands off to freedom vocabulary after the vow.
