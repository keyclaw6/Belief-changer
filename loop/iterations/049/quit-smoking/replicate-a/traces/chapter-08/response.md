Chapter 8
MORNINGS, BREATH AND HANDS

IN THIS CHAPTER: the bedside table untouched, the stairs in morning light, the kitchen cup, the short walk round the block, the pause with empty hands

*The morning was never thin; you only watched it through smoke.*

### The Bed Without The Reach

The light comes through the curtain and the room shows itself plain. The blanket holds warmth round your shoulders and the pillow holds the shape of sleep and the bedside table stands with a glass of water and curtains half open and no paper burning.

We knew another waking. We woke and the hand moved before the eyes opened and the fingers found the pack in the dark and the lighter clicked before thought started. We coughed into the pillow and we lit into the cough and we called that starting the day. You know that reach and you know how fast it came. I do not scold that reach. I ask you to live one morning apart from it and watch what stays.

You wake and you lie a minute and you feel breath move in the chest without tending. You swing your feet to the floor and you stand and you feel the floor cool underfoot and you stretch with arms wide and breath runs full to the bottom. You wash your face and the water wakes the skin and you look in the mirror with clear eyes. Nothing is missing. Nothing calls to be added. The minute holds together complete.

You know the voice that says the day cannot start without lighting. Hear it plain: "I need something before I can face the morning." You lived inside that sentence for years and you called it truth because hunger spoke inside waking. The tiny physical craving whispers when unfed and the brainwashing voice lays one sentence over the whisper until waking itself sounds thin. Lighting only quiets the whisper while the morning waits. The morning then does what mornings always did.

Try the separation in your own bed and the proof arrives without argument.

You wake with clear air and the head clears as you move. You drink water and the mouth wakes clean. You open the curtain and the street shows itself and the air at the window smells of rain and bread and traffic. We woke with tending and the mouth woke stale and the head woke fogged and we called fog normal. Separate waking from feeding and waking stays bright. People who never fed wake exactly this way and they call it ordinary. They miss no layer. They start the day with water and light and movement, and the day starts full.

If waking needs paper, why does waking arrive complete where no paper burns? If the first dose opens the day, why did the day open fogged and coughing when fed, while mornings lived with clear air open bright? And if the hand must reach before thought, whose hand reaches in houses where no pack sits on the table?

You know the honest answers because you woke both ways. Waking arrives complete where no paper burns because waking always did the opening. The fed day opened fogged because smoke laid its coat over breath and mouth while sleep still clung. No hand reaches where no hunger was fed, which proves reaching never belonged to waking. People do not call a thing a start when the start must be coughed through before it starts. They call it a toll that posed as dawn.

The bed gave rest. The morning gives the start.

Keep that difference with you into your first hour. You wake and you drink water and you open the window and you breathe. You feel the pull to reach and you let it pass as dying hunger, not an order. You wash and you dress and you move through rooms with clear air round your head. That keeping is not thin. It is the same morning, minus the billing.

### Stairs And Breath

The stairwell holds morning light and dust moves in the shaft and your feet find the steps. You take the first flight and breath comes and you take the second and breath stays with you. At the landing you pause and you look out at roofs and you draw air deep and the chest opens wide.

We knew other stairs. We climbed and the chest tightened halfway and we stopped on the landing to cough and we lit at the top to cover the cough and we called stopping age. We carried the pack everywhere and we counted landings by breaths short and we told ourselves breath shortens for everyone. You know those stairs and you know the burn low in the chest. I am warm to you in this and harsh only to the teaching that called that normal.

Watch breath itself and the helper shows its hands empty.

You stood at the kerb on cold mornings and you drew smoke and you felt the chest grip and you coughed and you called cough clearing. You climbed with paper in the pocket and each climb asked for a pause to tend and you called pausing rest. Separate climbing from feeding and climbing tells another story. Legs carry. Lungs fill. The heart knocks and settles. Breath taken at the landing tastes of air, not ash. Children run these stairs without counting and men and women who never fed climb them talking the whole way. They miss no help. They reach the top with breath to speak, and the top is reached full.

You know the objection that rises here, quiet and reasonable: "My chest is already marked; clear mornings belong to others." Hear it and answer with your own feet.

You climb one flight with clear air and breath comes. You climb talking and words still order themselves. You carry a bag up and arms work and breath works with them. We mistook hunger heard in the chest for the chest itself, and you know that mistaking by the way the hand checked the pocket when breath shortened. Once hunger is separated from lung, the sentence about damage as destiny loses weight without being mocked. It remains true that years of smoke left their mark. If breath lives in paper, why do stairs climb complete where no paper burns? If smoke opens the chest, why does the fed chest tighten halfway while the unfed chest carries on talking? And if pausing to cough proves age, why do older legs that never fed climb past you without pausing?

You know the honest answers because you climbed both ways. Stairs climb complete without paper because legs and lungs always did the climbing. The fed chest tightened because smoke narrowed what it claimed to open. Older legs pass because years never took what feeding took. People do not call a thing opening when the opened chest must stop to cough. They call it narrowing that learned to speak as help.

Breath carried. The paper only narrowed.

Take that verdict plain to your next landing. You climb and you breathe and you look out and roofs hold light. You feel the old pull to cover short breath with a light and you read it as hunger asking to close, not lung asking for help. You reach the top with words intact and air clean round your head. That reaching is not less. It is the same body, with air owned.

### Coffee That Tastes Of Coffee

The kitchen holds its morning sounds and the kettle clicks off and the cup warms your palms. Steam rises and the smell fills the small room and you sit at the table with the window cracked and you drink.

We knew another cup. We poured and we carried cup and pack together to the step and we lit before the first sip and we called the pair made in nature. We drank through smoke and the tongue dulled and the cup cooled while we tended and we called cooling savouring. You know that step and you know how the cup always waited on the paper. I do not mock that pairing. I dispute only what belonged, flatly and kindly, because cup and paper were never tried apart.

Watch taste itself and the host shows itself.

You lift the cup with clear air and bitter meets warm and the flavour runs clean from first sip to last. You eat a slice of toast and butter and crumb taste whole and the mouth stays clean after. You drink water after and the mouth resets bright. People who never fed close breakfast exactly this way and they call the close satisfying. They miss no depth. They finish the cup and they set it down and the morning tastes finished. Take the same cup with tending and smoke coats the tongue and coffee turns flat and the aftertaste turns stale before the cup is half done. The cup did the giving. The paper only stood between mouth and cup.

You know the quiet claim that coffee and paper belong together by chemistry. Test it at your own table without argument.

You drink one cup with clear air and flavour holds to the bottom. You drink with food and food tastes of itself. You sit with talk and talk loosens while the cup warms your hands. We learned to call nearness nature where every cup came with ash, and you know that teaching by the saucers stained beside clean cups. Separate cup from paper and cup still warms. Talk still loosens. The morning still closes. The cup never asked for ash. The tongue never asked for coat.

If the pair lives by nature, why does coffee taste complete where no paper burns? If paper deepens flavour, why does flavour thin halfway when fed while the unfed cup holds to the bottom? And if mornings need the match beside the mug, why do kitchens without ashtrays close breakfast laughing while fed kitchens step out to tend and return to cold cups?

You know the honest answers because you drank both ways. Coffee tastes complete without paper because coffee always did the tasting. Flavour thinned when fed because smoke dulled what it claimed to deepen. Kitchens without ashtrays laugh through because talk and warmth always did the holding. People do not call a thing a pair when one half cools the other before it is done. They call it a hanger-on that learned to sit beside.

The cup warmed. The paper only cooled.

Hold that distinction through your next kitchen hour. You pour and you sit and you taste clean to the bottom. You feel the old pull to carry the pack with the cup and you let it pass as hunger, not as thirst. You finish and you rinse the cup and the mouth stays bright. That finishing is not thinner. It is the same breakfast, with taste owned.

### The Short Walk With Nothing Lit

The street waits with its ordinary business and you step out with keys and no tending. The pavement runs damp from night rain and shops lift shutters and a dog pulls its walker and the air moves cool on your face. You walk to the corner and back with hands in pockets and nothing lit.

We knew another walk. We stepped out and the hand lit before the gate closed and we measured distance by finishes and we turned back when the pack ran light. We walked the same corner to feed, not to walk, and we called walking thinking. You know that gate and you know how the feet waited on the lighter. I tell you flat the feet never needed that permission.

Watch walking itself and the filler shows its hands empty.

You walk with clear air and eyes open and roofs and faces and shopfronts arrive complete. You hear traffic and talk and gulls and your own steps, and the minutes pass kind. You breathe as you go and shoulders loosen and thought orders itself without tending. People who never fed walk this exact loop and they call it clearing the head. They miss no company. They return with cheeks bright and mind settled, and the loop closes full. Take the same loop with tending and attention breaks to shield the ember and eyes drop to count and breath shortens against wind. The street did the clearing. The paper only cut the walk into tends.

You know the small fear that walks will feel long and bare with hands empty. Meet it on the pavement, not in argument.

You walk one block with clear air and the block ends complete. You stand at the crossing and you watch and the watching fills the wait. You turn into wind and breath stays yours and the cold tastes clean. We called empty hands bare because hunger spoke loudest when legs moved and hands hung idle, and you know that speaking by the pocket checked before leaving rooms where walking might find you. Once hunger heard on the move is separated from moving, the fear of bareness thins without scorn. It stays an honest record of when you reached. It no longer proves walks need feeding.

If walks need paper, why do loops close complete where no paper burns? If tending clears the head, why does the tended walk break into ember-watching while the untended walk returns settled? And if empty hands make miles long, why do miles walked with eyes open leave a small clean memory while miles lit leave only taste stale and no picture kept?

You know the honest answers because you walked both ways. Loops close without paper because streets and air and movement always did the closing. The tended walk broke because fed hunger cuts attention into pays. Miles with eyes open leave memory because looking and breathing leave the hour lived, while closing hunger leaves nothing behind. The street cleared. The paper only cut.

Keep that with you on your next corner. You step out and you walk and you look and you breathe. You feel the pull when hands hang idle and you watch it pass as noise, not need. You return with air in the chest and pictures in the head. That returning is not barer. It is the same street, with steps owned.

### Hands That Rest Empty

We knew another pause. Carry that into your next break. You sit and you breathe and you look and hands lie still. You feel the old pull to fill the stillness and you read it as hunger, not as need for company. You rise when ready and work takes you back. That rising is not thinner. It is the same day, with rest owned.

### Favourite Hours, Owned

Evening comes with its lamps and plates and talk and you stay inside through the whole meal. You eat and taste holds and you drink and talk loosens and you clear the table and the night closes without stepping out. Morning, noon and night run their course with clear air, and each hour tastes of itself.

I tell you flat the hours never asked to be cut.

Watch an owned evening and the meaning shows itself whole.

Nothing special holds. Nothing thin asks to be filled. You credit the minute, not the paper, and the minute pays you direct.

You know the last whisper of doubt: "But will ordinary hours satisfy a person like me, through weeks and seasons?" Answer with your own lived week, not with my promise.

You live one morning complete and the morning holds. You live stairs and cup and walk and pause complete and each holds. You stack mornings into a week and the week holds. The hours held. Nothing was missing.

Hold that as you go into your days. You wake and you climb and you taste and you walk and you pause, each complete. You feel pulls rise and pass as noise. You keep your coffee and your walks and your window chair, with clear air round your head. That keeping is not fewer hours. It is the same life, with hours owned.
