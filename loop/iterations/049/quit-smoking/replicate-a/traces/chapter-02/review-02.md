REVISE
- FRAGMENT — runs of three or more sentence fragments in a row with no finite main clause. Quote 1: "Clear stairs without cough. Patio owned again without stepping out. Deep breath taken at the kerb without planning. Meal closed without leaving the table. Drive taken without counting stops." Quote 2: "Not a lecture. Not a label on you. A description of the circle." Quote 3: "Tired, because sleep is broken by the clock even at night. Wheezy, because smoke passes where only air belongs. Chained to the next cigarette, because attention never fully leaves the coming feed." Require rewrite of each run as complete conversational sentences carrying the belief verb. The Plain Carr sentence rule is the only authority; no new diction demand.
