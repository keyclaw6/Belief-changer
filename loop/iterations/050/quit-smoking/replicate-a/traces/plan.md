# Master Plan — Quit Smoking

## 1. Book core

- Target behavior: compulsive cigarette smoking. Total stop at a last ordinary cigarette, then none. Not cutting down, not smoking only on occasions, not vaping as a destination, not gum or patch as the method.
- Reader: an adult who smokes daily, has failed by straining, gum, patches or abrupt unsupported attempts, believes quitting means losing calm, pleasure and company, lights up while hating being a smoker, and fears life without cigarettes would be miserable or impossible.
- Load-bearing false belief, frozen: Cigarettes calm me, help me cope and give me genuine pleasure, and life without them would be miserable deprivation.
- Through-line: the cigarette never gave, it only closed a discomfort it had opened; once that is seen, stopping is escape from a trap, not sacrifice of a pleasure.
- Format: full-length, 15 chapters, about 61,000 words planned. One reader, general adult edition. No narrator autobiography; testimony is composite from research banks, written original.
- Redefinition and margin: not applicable. Smoking as a category is quittable wholesale. No Good-X / Bad-X line, no CAPS subset name, no margin-for-error doctrine for consumption. A slip revives nothing unless the old belief is let back in; the book guards the belief, not the behavior.
- Clinical perimeter: no medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis or illness pointers only. The last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial attention on the ugliness, not a dare to smoke more.
- Strongest pro-behavior scene: late night with drinks and friends in the smoking area, good vibes and buzzing cigarette, trying to keep the feeling before morning takes it away.
- Destination state: whenever smoking crosses the mind the reader feels relief and freedom at not doing it, pities smokers still inside, never reopens the decision, rejoices at the end of an enemy rather than mourning a friend.
- Saved ending reframe, reserved for the final movement only: you were never saying goodbye to the thing that carried you; you were laying down something that had been carrying you away, and ordinary moments were yours all along.

## 2. Fork decisions

- Fork 1, mechanism: full personification force through plain everyday common-noun description only. The book refers to the tiny physical craving that fades when unfed and the brainwashing voice that feeds it. No frozen proper names, no capitalized branded creatures, no mantra-sheet freezing of mechanism names, no invented tokens. The craving is small, starving and already dying; the belief story feeding it is the real target.
- Fork 2, outcome: total cessation, commanded with warm certainty. Follow-all-instructions is the contract. Moderation is foreclosed by the pincer that if there is no benefit in doing it often there is no point doing it occasionally, plus the cliff image for safe limits.
- Fork 3, science weight: hard facts arrive flat, vivid and frightening where true, then relief removes the loss-frame in the same chapter. Facts work as perception-shifters, never as literature review. Raw titles live in back matter if needed; the argument itself carries the consequence. Evidence limits are honored by not overclaiming.
- Fork 4, villain: two villains, both hit hard. The nicotine industry and its engineered dose units that set the trap, and the named anti-method, the Willpower Method with its substitutes, that kept the reader in it. Contempt belongs to the trap and the wrong method, never to the reader.
- Fork 5, void: change nothing else in life. The body returns to its natural baseline on its own; old contexts are inhabited confidently to prove freedom. No replacement system as the method.

## 6. Lexicon and instruction spine

Trap register: dose, fix, hit, feed, the trap, brainwashing, conned, chained, slave to the schedule where banks supply the word. Units are doses; practice is feeding; normal status is brainwashing; users are trapped with warmth.

Freedom register: freedom is shown as lived moments — clear morning stairs without cough, patio owned again, deep breath taken at the kerb, meal closed without stepping out, drive without counting stops — and named only by mantra-sheet frozen lines. No tag refrain, no sentence-tail ease, no opener rejoicing formula except the single final outward imperative.

Banned register: give up, resist, stay strong, discipline, abstain, sacrifice except when naming the illusion of sacrifice, trying to stop, one day at a time, recovery journey, quit cold turkey as framing. Quit itself as plain verb is acceptable.

Reader dialect for ventriloquized thought, from banks: the stick, ciggies, bumming cigs, one will not hurt, habit smokes, closet smoker, Febreeze the hell out of myself, little parties, lost best friend, lone guy outside, push through, boxed in, smober, I HAD to, just one, half a pack, two packs a day.
