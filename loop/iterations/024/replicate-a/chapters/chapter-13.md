Chapter 13
NO HIDING PLACE LEFT

**IN THIS CHAPTER**
A birthday table with candles still smoking.
A fruit bowl in morning light.
A lonely plate of plain crackers calling for sweet company.
A party bag held up as the last proof.

*Every what-about is the same old con in a fresh hat, and none of them holds for a minute.*

SO THE MIND GOES SHOPPING

You have seen someone exactly like you walk out and stay out. The tired drawer, the glovebox foil, the sofa chocolate, the third-evening wobble that passed like a cloud. You know it can be done, easily, by an ordinary grazer.

And now, right on cue, the mind goes shopping for loopholes.

Not because you still believe. Because we all did this. We who lived years topping up and grazing learned to negotiate for the trap when plain argument failed. If the big lie cannot live, perhaps a small exception can. If daily fixes cannot be defended, perhaps a tiny portion can. If sugar in a bar cannot be loved, perhaps fruit can be feared, or parties can be spared, or clever swaps can be smuggled in.

I welcome every one of them. Bring them into the light, one by one, and watch what happens. Each arrives already labelled, once you know the handwriting.

SMALL ALLOWANCES, LARGE LIES

"Just a treat. Surely just one, now and then, cannot hurt?"

If it gave you anything, why would you need to limit it so fiercely. Joy does not need a rule. The limit confesses the poison while begging to keep it.

"I've managed to be good this week, so once a week is fine?"

Good for what, and to whom. We called ourselves good for enduring a low we had paid to create, then paid again for the brief lift. That is not goodness. That is the loop billing twice.

"Can I not simply cut down and keep it under control?"

Moderation is jumping off a cliff provided you fall only a few metres. The bargain sounds careful until you hear what it asks. If there is no benefit in a lot, what point is there in a little.

Ask yourself plainly, and answer honestly. Did the small sliver ever stay small. Did the permitted bite ever stop the evening. Did cutting down ever make you think about it less, or did it make every dose more precious. You know the honest answer, and that answer closes the shop.

FRUIT AND REAL FOOD

"Does this mean I must fear fruit? Must I eye an apple like an enemy?"

No. When this book says BAD SUGAR it means added and refined sweetness with junk-processed carbs in drink, packet, bar, biscuit, cake, cereal or dessert-form dose. Sweetness inside real food as nature packed it is not the target and is not counted.

"But fruit is sweet, so is it not the same trap?"

Taste the difference with your own mouth. An apple eaten hungry satisfies and leaves you clear. A bar eaten hungry excites, dulls, drops you flat, and calls for another. One feeds you. The other feeds the loop.

Do you fear hunger itself now. Do you fear a proper breakfast eaten slowly to satisfaction. You need fear nothing that leaves you bright and done. The con wants you confused about apples so you keep buying packets.

THE LONELY PLATE THAT CALLS FOR COMPANY

Now to the pale cousins that pretend they are not sweet at all.

Picture a dull house party. On the table stands a plate of plain crackers, white bread, puffed cereal, salted crisps. Lonely. Dry. Flat. No one would cross a room for them alone.

So they send out invitations. They call for sweet company to make a party — cola to wash them down, ketchup and sweet dip to dress them, chocolate to follow them, a fizzy drink to lift them. Alone they bore you. Together they spike you, crash you, and leave you hunting the next hit.

That is why they belong inside BAD SUGAR. Not from pedantry. From lived effect.

"But bread is not pudding. Crisps are not chocolate. Surely they are different?"

Watch your own afternoon. The packet leads to the drawer. The drawer leads to the sofa bar. The bland carrier invites the loud fix, and the loud fix calls for another carrier. It was one party all along.

"I need something RIGHT NOW to concentrate. Something savoury will do?"

What you feel RIGHT NOW is an empty, slightly restless dip calling to be fed, then any quick spike will seem to help for ten bright minutes. Notice the hour after. Notice the fog, the heaviness, the next hunt. The help was the harm arriving early.

PARTIES AND SPECIAL DAYS

"What about birthdays? Holidays? My daughter's wedding cake?"

Take the kindest one. Candles, laughter, people you love, time unhurried. Did the cake make the sun shine. Did the sugar make your friends funny. Did the fix make the evening kind, or did the kind evening make the fix seem kind for a moment.

It never was a genuine pleasure, treat or lift. It was sun and leisure and company sneaking a ride, while the sweet dulled your mouth and borrowed tomorrow's brightness.

"But I will feel odd if everyone else is having some?"

You will feel odd for a minute, then marvellous for the evening. Pity the users still caught, negotiating their slivers, watching the clock for permission, leaving the party flat. You get to keep the laughter clear-headed and get on with enjoying your life.

THE CLEVER SWAPS

"What about the light versions? Diet drinks, sweeteners, honey, dark chocolate as a healthier dose?"

If the pleasure was real, why change its clothes. The swap confesses the fear while keeping the belief alive. The taste still teaches your mouth to hunt sweet hits, and the hunt still runs the day.

"At least honey is natural. At least dark is almost medicine?"

Ask what it does FOR you. Does it warm the kitchen. Does it quiet the kids. Does it give love. Or does it give a sharp hit, a dulled mouth, a flat hour, and the return of the very craving you are leaving.

There is no clever packet that gives where all packets took. The con loves a new uniform. See through it and the uniform falls off.

THE HOUSE RULE AND THE SLIP

"I don't keep it in the house. If I keep the house clean, I will be safe?"

A locked cupboard never freed anyone. We all learned the house rule, then bought emergency sweets for the bag, the car, the drawer, the coat pocket. The rule made the fix precious and the hunt heroic. Freedom needs no locks.

"What if I take one by accident? A biscuit offered before I can speak?"

Your body copes with a blip. The safety is there for accidents, the way a seatbelt is there for a jolt, never as leave to drive wild. An accident revives nothing unless the old lie is allowed back to whisper that you need it now.

So guard the belief, not the behaviour. If a crumb crosses you uninvited, smile. You have no reason to order the low again. The thought passes like a cloud, and you remain free.

IS THIS JUST ANOTHER TRICK

And now the last what-about, the one aimed at this book itself. I hear it because I once said it.

"This sounds too easy. Are you not just brainwashing me the other way?"

Fair question. Here is the plain answer. Brainwashing told you a fix lifts you, rewards you, loves you. This book asks you to check your own day — the lift and the drop, the cinema warmth that belonged to the film, the garden joy that belonged to sun and company. You are not asked to believe me. You are asked to believe your own eyes.

"But I failed so often. What if my will is simply too weak?"

That was the Willpower Method, and it was built to fail you. It takes a strong will to persist for years in something that goes against all your instincts, to diet on Monday and creep back by Tuesday and still get up and try again. You were never weak-willed. You were wilful, misled, using the wrong method with no finish line.

You do not need to push thoughts away. You cannot stop a thought by forbidding it, any more than you can stop picturing an elephant by ordering yourself not to. Let the thought come. Reframe it. When the old voice says treat, hear the con talking, remember what it never did FOR you, and feel relief that you no longer answer.

There is nothing left to negotiate. No portion to spare. No fruit to fear. No party that needs sweetening. No clever swap that gives. No locked cupboard that saves. Only one tired loop, seen plain, with no hiding place left.

**SUMMARY**
- Small portions and weekly treats do not survive honest memory, the sliver never stayed small.
- Fruit and real food eaten hungry satisfy and leave you clear, they are not the target.
- Bland starchy carriers belong with sweet fixes because they invite each other to the same spike and crash.
- Parties and special days were warmed by people and leisure, the sweet added nothing.
- Light versions and natural-sounding swaps keep the same hunt alive in new clothes.
- House rules never freed anyone, and an accidental blip revives nothing unless the old belief is let back in.
