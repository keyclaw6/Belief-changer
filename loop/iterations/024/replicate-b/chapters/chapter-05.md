Chapter 5
EATING AS IT WAS MEANT TO BE

**IN THIS CHAPTER**
- Pale morning kitchen and the kettle
- An empty belly remembered
- Eggs, fruit and plain food eaten slowly to satisfaction

*Hunger answered with real food is pleasure itself — BAD SUGAR never made it better.*

### THE HUNGER YOU FORGOT

When did you last feel truly hungry?

Not peckish. Not mouth-hungry. Not the restless thought of something sweet at three o'clock. I mean the clean, empty, alive hunger that comes in the morning when the belly is light and the body asks plainly for food.

Most of us in the trap cannot remember it. We graze. We top up. We sip something sweet at ten, chew something sweet at twelve, reach for the drawer at three, pick in the evening. We are never quite hungry and never quite satisfied. We live in the middle — dull mouth, dull belly, dull head.

And then we fear the very thing that would delight us. We think, if I lived without my sweet fixes, eating would be bland and hunger would be bleak.

“But food without something sweet would be dull.”

“I would be hungry all the time.”

I hear you, because I lived that fear. We all did. We confused grazing with eating and a full sweet mouth with satisfaction.

The fact is... hunger is not bleak. Hunger is the sauce.

Ask yourself honestly. When did plain food eaten in true hunger ever taste dull? When did you ever finish a real meal eaten slowly and think, I wish I felt emptier? When did grazing all day ever leave you feeling bright and light and done?

You cannot answer those and still fear hunger. The only honest answer concedes it. Hunger answered is joy. Grazing is what stole it.

### MORNING KITCHEN

Come with me into an ordinary morning.

Early light at the window. The kettle murmurs. The kitchen is cold and quiet. Your belly is empty — properly empty — and it growls a little as you move. That growl frightens no one who understands it. It is health calling.

You cook something plain. Eggs in the pan, fruit cut open, bread warm, nothing clever, nothing counted, nothing measured. You sit down because you are hungry, not because the clock says so, not because a wrapper shouted at you.

First bite. Eaten slowly. You taste it. Really taste it. The warmth, the salt, the juice, the chew. Second bite. The empty feeling softens. Third, fourth, unhurried. No rush to the next hit, no hand back in a packet. The body warms from the inside. The head clears. The eyes brighten.

And then — the marvellous moment we forgot — enough arrives. Not stuffed. Not heavy. Satisfied. The plate is left with pleasure, not regret. The belly is calm. The mind turns gladly to the day. You get up light and clear and free to get on with enjoying your life.

Do you recognise that? That is your body, your instinct, real food doing what they were always meant to do.

No lecture told you to stop. No rule told you what to weigh or count. Hunger spoke. Food answered. Satisfaction closed the meal. That is eating as it was meant to be.

### NOTHING IS MISSING

“But do I not need sugar for energy?”

That worry keeps many good people in the trap. Let me settle it so you never need to carry it.

The fact is your body makes the steady fuel it needs from real food, so living without BAD SUGAR leaves nothing missing. I offer no medical or diet advice — if you live with diabetes, take medicines that touch blood sugar, appetite or mood, or carry a history of eating trouble, keep your clinician in charge of that side.

Read that again and feel the relief in it. There is no gap to fill. No essential pleasure to replace. No void to brace against.

Think of a child who has played hard outdoors and comes in hungry. Give him plain food and watch his face. He does not analyse. He does not miss a fix. He eats, glows, stops when he has had enough, and runs back out. When did you last eat like that? When did a packet ever leave you glowing like that?

A packet never did. A dose never satisfied. It teased the mouth, clouded the belly, and left you looking for the next dose. The meal satisfied because hunger and food belong together.

This is why I tell you with such cheerful certainty: Eat what you want and never crave BAD SUGAR again.

Eat what you want means real food, eaten when hungry, stopped when satisfied. Not a rule. Not a diet. Not a new system to learn. Just the old delight returning.

Hunger is not the enemy. Hunger is the feast.

4. EAT WHEN YOU ARE HUNGRY AND STOP WHEN YOU ARE SATISFIED.
Trust hunger to do its job.

**SUMMARY**
- True hunger is a clean empty feeling that makes plain food delicious.
- Grazing on sweet fixes kept you neither hungry nor satisfied.
- A real breakfast eaten slowly ends in calm satisfaction, not regret.
- Your body asks for food and knows enough without sweet doses.
