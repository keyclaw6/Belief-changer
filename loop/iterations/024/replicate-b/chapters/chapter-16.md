*There is nothing to mourn and everything to celebrate.*

### THE EMPTY CHAIR

Picture a small kitchen table with one empty chair.

In one house the chair belonged to a dear friend. Kind. Loyal. Full of laughter. The chair stands empty and the room feels cold. Grief is natural. Something precious has been lost.

In another house the very same chair belonged to a cruel enemy. One who lied every day. One who stole money and health and peace of mind and never once did a single kind thing for anyone in the house. The chair stands empty and the room feels light. The air is sweet again. Grief would be madness. Only relief makes sense.

Same table. Same empty chair. Opposite emotions. Both true to what the chair really held.

We kept BAD SUGAR at our table for years and called it a friend. We dosed ourselves morning and afternoon, topped up in the evening, fed the craving and called the brief quiet a pleasure. We told ourselves life would be duller with that chair empty.

The fact is it was never a friend.

Did that fix ever love you back? Did it ever lift you above normal, or did it only quiet for minutes the flat want the last dose left behind? Did it ever cook the joy at the table, or was it only ever sneaking a ride on company and leisure and hunger satisfied?

You know the honest answers. You have lived them. The sweet gave nothing and took plenty. It left you tired and foggy and hunting the next hit. It conned you with bright packets and fond memories and the chatter of other users still caught.

So do not mourn the death of a friend. Rejoice at the death of an enemy who never once did anything for you.

That is freedom photographed in one picture. The chair is empty. You are free. You have absolutely no reason to feel low and every reason to celebrate, to feel relief and bright and clear for the rest of your days.

When the thought of BAD SUGAR drifts in, meet it with that picture. Smile from inside and say FANTASTIC! I'M FREE!

### THE CARD TO KEEP

You escaped easily, immediately and permanently because you saw the trap clearly. Keep the seeing clear and you stay free.

If you turned straight to these last pages, put them aside. Start at the beginning and read the book properly with an open mind. This card only works when the belief has changed first.

Photograph this list. Pin it in the kitchen. Carry it through shops and family tables and tired afternoons. All you have to do is follow all the instructions.

1. KEEP AN OPEN MIND. You cannot lose by hearing this out.
2. DON'T STOP OR CUT DOWN YET. Carry on exactly as normal while you read.
3. BEGIN WITH ELATION, NOT DREAD. You are finding out, not giving up.
4. EAT WHEN YOU ARE HUNGRY AND STOP WHEN YOU ARE SATISFIED. Trust hunger to do its job.
5. NEVER JUDGE BY THE LAST FIX. The lift lies; watch the whole day.
6. NEVER KEEP A SPECIAL ONE. If it gave nothing, no occasion changes that.
7. SEE THE DIP FOR WHAT IT IS. It is only the Mite dying.
8. NEVER DOUBT THE DECISION. Doubt is the Trap talking, not you.
9. IGNORE ANYONE WHO QUIT BY THE WILLPOWER METHOD. They still believe they sacrificed.
10. IGNORE ANY ADVICE THAT CONFLICTS WITH THIS METHOD. Confused advice rebuilds the Con.
11. AVOID THE INFLUENCE OF OTHER SUGAR USERS WHILE YOU READ. Their brainwashing is catching until yours is gone.
12. TAKE YOUR LAST ORDINARY BAD-SUGAR OCCASION AND MAKE THE VOW. Leave as a free non-user, not a mourner.

Read them as a free eater reads them, with joy. No dread. No loss. No private exception kept alive in a drawer. A free non-user needs no loophole because there is nothing worth looping back for.

I promise you this is enough. Guard the belief and the behaviour looks after itself. Pity users still caught, never envy them. Let a passing thought become a hit of relief. Let an accidental taste remain what it is, a tasteless blip that gave you nothing, and walk on rejoicing.

### GO OUT AND LIVE

This is where books about misery give you a plan to carry forever. I give you life.

Eat when you are hungry and stop when you are satisfied. Trust your body, your instinct, real food. Walk straight through bright aisles with indifference for what never fed you. Sit long at family tables and taste people and laughter and leisure. Meet a tired afternoon and notice it stays clear. Meet a sweet-thought and answer it with delight.

You are not missing out. They are. Does the trapped shopper negotiating by the packets look like enjoyment to you? We were him. Now we are free to leave him to his dose with pity and get on with our own bright morning.

No counting. No hiding. No white-knuckle watch over triggers. Freedom that needs hiding is not freedom. Yours grows marvellous and wonderful and exciting each ordinary week you live it.

You have lost nothing worth keeping. You have escaped the Sugar Trap. Celebrate the empty chair.

FANTASTIC! I'M FREE!

Get on with enjoying your life.
