## 1. Book core

- Target behavior: compulsive cigarette smoking. Total stop. Not cutting down, not vaping as a destination, not NRT as the method.
- Reader: an adult who smokes daily, wants to stop, has failed through willpower, patches, gum or abrupt unaided attempts, and believes life without cigarettes would be miserable, flat, or impossible to manage.
- Load-bearing false belief (frozen): My cigarettes relax me, help me cope, and give me real pleasure, and life without them would mean losing something I need.
- Through-line: the relief credited to the cigarette is the ending of a need the previous cigarette created; seen clearly, there is nothing to give up.
- Format: full-length belief-change book, front matter plus fifteen chapters, planned 61,800 words.
- Fork 1 — mechanism characters: full Carr personification. The trivial physical loop is named the Ember Mite, starved to death in days. The belief system that feeds it is named the Smokescreen, killed by understanding. No no-monster override.
- Fork 2 — destination: total cessation, commanded with warm certainty. The last ordinary cigarette, then none. Moderation foreclosed by the pincer and the cliff image. No autonomy/moderation override.
- Fork 3 — facts: hard facts stated flat and frightening where true, then fear disowned as the motive in the same chapter. No literature-review texture. Raw citations quarantined to back matter if needed. The scare stays in the argument, never in an appendix.
- Fork 4 — villains: two. The nicotine industry that designed the dose unit and recruited the reader, and the Willpower Method with its substitutes and tapers that kept the reader failing. Warmth to the person, contempt only for the trap and the wrong method.
- Fork 5 — void: change nothing else in life. The non-smoker has no desire to fix; the body returns to its baseline on its own. No replacement system, no positive-direction override.
- Redefinition: none. Cigarettes are quitted as a whole category. No Good-X / Bad-X split, no CAPS bad subset, no margin-for-error buffer for deliberate use. A slip revives nothing unless the belief is let back in; guard the belief.
- Safety perimeter: no medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis or illness pointers only. The last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial attention on the ugliness, not a dare to smoke more.
- Strongest pro-behavior scene: a night out with drinks, friends laughing in the smoking area, the feeling of a last night on earth with a cigarette in hand.
- Destination state: whenever smoking crosses the mind, the reader feels relief and quiet joy at being free, with no sense of loss.
- Saved ending reframe (appears only in the final movement): the cigarette never attended any moment of your life; it only charged admission to moments that were already yours.
