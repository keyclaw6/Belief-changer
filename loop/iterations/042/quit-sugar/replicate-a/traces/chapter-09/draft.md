Chapter 9
BUILT TO BE WANTED

IN THIS CHAPTER: the white room with small spoons, the long shelf with bright packets, the small pot and the tall box and the brown jar, the songs and tigers built for children, one three-year round told straight.

*You never wanted so hard because you are weak, you wanted so hard because want was built before you arrived.*

You left the counted bags behind you and you closed the weekday corridor and you saw the single daily dose grow past its fences.

We carry that plain sight with us when we walk into the next room because the next room answers the last charge the old story still holds against you. The old story says the food world stands neutral and your hand moves alone inside it. The old story says the lab had no part in your evenings and the shelf had no part in your mornings and the call that woke after sweet proves something soft in your character. I tell you with warmth that I held that charge against myself for years and it lied in the same voice every time.

Come with me first into the white room where your want was tuned before you ever pushed a cart.

### The White Room With Small Spoons

Picture a bright room without hunger in it. Long tables hold small cups in rows. Men and women in clean coats pass the cups hand to hand and sip and spit and mark papers. No one in that room eats because the body asks. Everyone in that room tastes because the paper asks. The paper asks how bright, how round, how long on the tongue, how soon gone.

We sat nowhere near that room while our evenings were decided inside it.

They start with plain sweetness and they raise it spoon by spoon until the mouth says now. That point has a workshop name among them, the point where brightness pleases most and least tires, and they hold the recipe there with care. Then they build the body around the point so the point lands harder. They whip air into chocolate so it melts to nothing and the mouth reads no weight and asks for more weight to answer weight unfelt. They tune crunch to burst and then to vanish so chewing never tires and the hand returns while the mouth still remembers brightness. They balance fat with sugar and salt with sugar until each hides the heaviness of the other and the whole bite slides past the place where fullness would have spoken. I lay this out without anger at you and with full anger at the bench where it was drawn because you never signed that drawing.

Then comes the meeting you never attended where shares of the stomach are divided on a board.

They speak of morning as a share to take and afternoon as a share to take and night as a share to take and the hours between as shares to open. They ask where sugar still rides thin and where a new carrier could bring it thicker. Bread that should close a meal is asked to carry more. Sauce that should sharpen a meal is asked to carry more. Yoghurt that claims plainness is asked to carry more. Cereal that claims morning strength is asked to carry more. When doubt rises in that meeting one sentence settles it, spoken so often it hangs like smoke, that when in doubt more sweetness carries the line. You never voted in that meeting though your cart pays for its minutes.

Ask what that room proves about your character and answer from inside your own kitchen rather than from shame.

Did you teach your tongue to tire less after vanishing bites or did vanishing bites teach your tongue to return sooner? Did you order fat and sugar to arrive paired for speed or did paired speed arrive built and priced and placed before you woke? You know because you ate before you knew the room existed and the room worked before you learned its name. Hands that never tuned bliss still answered bliss tuned elsewhere. Mouths that never asked for burst still followed burst placed on shelves at eye height. That order cannot be unseen once seen straight. Design moved first. Want followed. Want never proved weakness. Want proved tuning.

It is the other way around. You did not shape the bite. The bite shaped the want.

Hold that line while we walk the shelf where the tuning waits for hands.

### The Shelf That Never Sleeps

Walk with me down one ordinary aisle on one ordinary Tuesday with money for plain food in your pocket.

Light falls bright from above. Packets stand shoulder to shoulder in colors that shout though no voice shouts. Chocolate bars lean near the till where waiting thins patience. Ice cream waits in glass cold with pictures of pouring and breaking that promise brightness without weight. Pastries sit in clear boxes with sugar shining like frost. Cookies stack in tubes built to open and never to close well. Cake stands boxed with windows cut so the eye eats before the mouth. Doughnuts glaze under plastic that sweats a little. Sugary drinks rise in towers, red and orange and green, cold sweating, two for the price of almost one. Sweet snacks hang at child height with tigers and songs you know by heart though you never chose to learn them.

We told ourselves we walked that aisle with plain choice in our pockets.

I stood in that aisle telling myself I chose while pictures chose for me. We all did. The aisle never sleeps and never rests and never stops pointing. New packets pay for the eye corners where old packets stood. Old packets drop a coin in price and rise to greet the hand. The till line that bored you for ninety seconds last month now holds small brightness at every side so waiting itself teaches reaching. You call this normal shopping because every shopper around you does the same reach. Normal here means tuned, not natural. Tuned looks normal when every cart carries it.

Now lift three quiet carriers that claim to stand outside the round.

A small pot says plain yoghurt with fruit on the label and shows berries falling in slow motion. Turn the pot and read the small print under the berries and count what rides with the berries. Spoonfuls hide behind fruit names, honey and cane and concentrate dressed as orchard kindness. A tall box says morning strength with whole grains stamped large and shows wheat waving in sun. Pour a bowl and taste past the crunch and count the brightness that stays after milk thins. Flakes built to shatter fast carry sweetness fast and the fast carry teaches the morning call to knock at ten. A brown jar says slow cooked sauce with garden pictures and names tomatoes first in large letters. Spoon it over plain food and taste the round edge under the sharp and know that edge was ordered in a meeting to widen the share. None of these announce themselves as sweets. All three train the same lesson across weeks, that brightness belongs everywhere and often.

Ask three plain questions while the cart rolls and answer from weeks lived rather than from labels trusted.

Did those quiet carriers quiet your evenings or did evenings grow louder while quiet carriers stood in the cupboard? Did mornings after tall boxes hold plain till noon or did pictures of more arrive before ten? Did sauce nights close simple or did the round edge invite a bright close after a plain close? Go by the nights those carriers gave you, not the fields painted on them. You know them from cupboards kept with hope. Quiet names knocked as usual. Fast mornings invited pictures. Round edges invited closes. That triple dissolves the claim of neutral shelves though paint looked honest. Paint promised fields. Nights showed training. Training outlived packets. Packets emptied. Training called.

By four on those days you sit tired, heavy and foggy and call it your nature though nature never ordered that afternoon.

Speak that triple aloud as plain description rather than as blame because it names what the round leaves rather than what you are. Mornings start passable. Midday thins. Afternoon clamps. Pictures of a bright top-up arrive dressed as hunger. The top-up lifts for minutes and then drops lower than before and the lower place is named by the triple. We blamed age and work and sleep and weather while the same carriers stood in every blamed week. I blame the carriers now with warmth toward you and hardness toward the bench because blame placed rightly loosens what blame placed wrongly tightens. You never brewed that afternoon in your character. The afternoon was portioned on a board and tuned in cups and placed at eye height and taught by quiet pots.

Which owned more of your afternoons across a year, your will or the aisle you walked through twice a week?

Only honest answers count. You know them from diaries kept though diaries never named the aisle. Aisle weeks emptied packets. Plain weeks without the aisle held longer though days held the same work. That pair cannot be argued past because you lived both sides of it. Hands did not change between those weeks. Shelves changed. Change the shelf and the hand steadies though character stays the same person. Keep the shelf and the hand reaches though vows stand fresh on Sunday. Hands follow training while training stands. Training stood in every shop you entered.

That is how the Sugar Trap hires hunger to do its selling.

Say that sentence slowly in the aisle where lists are made and feel how it moves weight off your shoulders onto the bench where weight belongs. The trap never needed your permission to tune bliss. It needed your eye and your hand and your hunger doing ordinary shopping. Hunger doing ordinary shopping cannot be blamed for answering brightness built to answer hunger fastest. Blame the bench that built fastest to answer hunger rather than the hunger that answered as hunger was built to answer. Warmth toward you. Hardness toward design. That division holds through every room left in this book.

Keep that division while we ask which foods call loudest and which never called at all.

### Which Foods Call Loudest

Stand the foods of your weeks side by side on one table and watch them the way you watched your own hands.

On one end stand apples with cool skin and bite that slows the mouth. Strawberries with seed and water and chew that asks for pace. Broccoli with bite and bulk that fills the mouth with work. Carrots with crunch that tires chewing into rest. Plain nuts with chew and quiet close. Beans and lentils with weight that holds. Fish and eggs and plain meat with substance that meets hunger and lets hunger speak plainly when met. Whole staples eaten as real meals with warmth and volume that close the sitting. These sit quiet on the table though colors please the eye. They meet hunger. They let fullness speak. They leave the evening without pictures pointing hard at ten.

On the other end crowd the foods that never sit quiet.

Chocolate with melt built to vanish. Ice cream with cold and melt paired to slide past fullness. Fries with salt and fat and fast starch built to call while crunch lasts. Pizza with cheese pull and soft base built to eat past the slice. Cookies with burst and chew tuned to return. Cake with soft and bright paired to shine at first bite and loosen after. Candy with pure brightness that spikes and drops. Pastries with flake and gloss that shatter and invite. Sugary drinks with cold brightness that moves fast past the mouth. Sweetened yoghurts and cereals with quiet brightness under health paint. Sauces and breads where brightness rides hidden under savory names. White refined starch binges eaten as sweets when evening thins. These never sit quiet though wrappers promise smallness. They open. They picture. They pull. They teach the mouth to expect brightness after brightness.

Ask what that table proves about blame and let the table answer rather than shame.

Which end emptied past taste on your sofa across years? Which end left wrappers hidden and counts rewritten? Which end stood at the center of every corridor week and every single daily dose and every counted bag that failed? You know because your bins kept score fairly though wishes spoke kindly of balance. Score shows the crowded end opened and the quiet end closed. Closed foods closed evenings. Open foods opened evenings. That difference never lived in your morals between apple and cookie. It lived in build. Build opened. Build closed. Hands answered build while build stood on the table.

It was never the strawberry.

Say that short line where shame talks loudest and hear how it cuts the old charge at the root. No one ever hid strawberry wrappers. No one ever counted strawberries with marker maths at noon and rewrote the number at nine. No one ever planned weekends around broccoli luxury or feared grey birthdays without carrots. The foods that called loudest share one build, processed and paired and fast to absorb, fat with sugar built to retrigger, brightness tuned to optimum and body tuned to vanish. The foods that never called share the opposite build, whole and watered and fibred and paced, chew and bulk and close. To place both builds under one charge of weak character mistakes bench work for soul work. Bench work built the call. Soul never ordered it.

Some readers hear this and say the table still leaves them choosing, so choice still proves fault.

Choosing among tuned tables while hungry and hurried and pictured at every corner never proved fault. Choice inside tuning answers tuning more often than wishes answer wishes. We chose the bright packet on tired Tuesdays the way thirsty travelers drink from the nearest well though wells were dug by others along the road. Diggers chose the road. Walkers chose among dug wells. To blame walkers for thirst answers nothing about who dug where. I write this without a scrap of scorn because we all drank at those wells with lists for plain food folded in our pockets. Lists did not fail from softness. Wells stood nearer and brighter and faster than hunger could argue past while pictures sang.

When did quiet foods ever need guarding across a month without pictures?

Never. Quiet foods needed no number on the bag and no weekday rule and no freezer office and no honey story to stay small. They stayed small because build stays small. Loud foods needed guards because build calls. Guards never changed build. Build changed hands. See build plainly and the charge against character falls though memory of wrappers remains as warning. Warning guides. Warning never owns.

Hold that table steady while we look at the wiring the loud end pulls.

### The Same Lines Pulled

There lives a wanting line inside every eater that lights when food pleases.

Plain food lights it quietly and then lets it rest when hunger meets and fullness speaks. The light rises with hunger, warms with taste, settles with enough. That is the line doing honest work, telling the body where fuel landed and when enough arrived. No one fears that light. No one hides wrappers under that light. That light closes.

The loud foods pull the same line harder and longer and more often.

The same wanting lines that answer plain meals answer bright doses with brighter flare. The flare feels like pleasure in the first bites because hunger meets brightness and attention meets novelty. Then flare outlives pleasure while wanting outlives liking. You know this split from inside your own evenings though no one gave you words for it before. Last bites rarely pleased like first bites. Last bites often tired the mouth and coated the tongue and dulled the very brightness they chased. Yet want stayed bright after pleasure dimmed. Hands reached past taste. Bowls emptied past hunger. Packets closed past joy of eating. That gap between reaching and tasting tells the whole mechanism without one hard word. Pleasure dimmed. Want stayed lit. Lit want moved the hand. Dimmed pleasure never stopped it.

Ask two plain questions while last bites sit in memory and answer from mouths lived rather than from pictures kept.

When did last bites ever taste better than first bites across your years? When did want ever dim with taste across those same nights? Only honest answers stand. First bites shone. Last bites labored. Want never dimmed with shine. Want grew while shine thinned. That pair proves the drive sits in wanting rather than in liking though pictures sold liking as the reason. Pictures sold taste. Mouths reported labor. Hands obeyed want. Trust mouths that labored over pictures that promised. Pictures promised brightness that lasts. Mouths showed brightness that thins and want that stays.

This is why one never stays one and why mornings after tall boxes knock at ten.

First entry flares the line. Flare fades fast. Fade reads as emptiness though body holds fuel enough. Emptiness pictures brightness as answer. Brightness re-enters and reflares the line and fades again lower. Lower pictures brighter. Round goes the evening while taste thins and want thickens. The line never needed your consent after entry to flare again. Entry flared it. Flare pictured. Pictures pulled. That chain never asked character for orders after the first bite. First bite gave orders for the chain.

I speak of grip here and nothing larger because larger would lie.

The same wanting lines that drugs pull hard also answer bright food. That is flat and must be said plainly or the loop stays mysterious. It is not the same strength and I claim no sameness of strength. Small lines pulled often still rule evenings though giants would rule harder. Grip needs no giant strength to steer a tired hand at nine. Grip needs only a lit line and pictures and entry. Say it with care and without fear dressed as science. Lines lit. Pictures pointed. Entry flared. Hands followed. That sequence explains want without branding you and without borrowing thunder that does not belong to this table. Thunder would frighten and frighten would not free. Plain sequence frees because plain sequence can be seen through.

"Food is just food."

I print your objection back with warmth because we all spoke it in clean kitchens. Food as a word sounds neutral and kind and far from benches and boards. Some food still stands neutral and kind on the table we set above. Loud food never stood neutral though neutral names covered it. Neutral does not tune bliss to optimum. Neutral does not whip air to vanish. Neutral does not divide stomach shares on boards. Neutral does not hang tigers at child height. To call tuned food just food mistakes paint for build. Paint says orchard. Build says flare. Flare ruled your nine o clock more often than orchards ruled your noon. Trust nine o clock that kept score over paint that promised orchards.

"Industry is neutral, it only answers what people want."

I print that second line because it arrives right after the first whenever design is named. Answering want never built vanishing melt. Answering want never tuned crunch to tire less. Answering want never hid brightness in sauce to widen shares. Those moves lead want rather than follow it. Led want then answers with wallets and wallets are read as proof of leading rightly. That circle never needed your weakness to turn. It needed benches and boards and shelves and songs. See the circle whole and the charge of personal fault finds nowhere to stand though circle keeps turning a little while you watch. Watched circles loosen. Unwatched circles steer.

It is the other way around. Hunger did not invent the flare. The flare retrained hunger.

Keep that wiring plain while we hear one round told straight with numbers.

### Three Years Told Straight

I think of weeks I watched close up, told to me in plain speech across many evenings, kept here as one arc because one arc carries what lists cannot.

She started neither heavy nor thin, working long days at a desk with a drawer that locked poorly. Mornings held with coffee and a tall box that claimed strength. By half past ten pictures arrived though breakfast stood only two hours behind. She answered with a bar from the drawer and told herself the bar closed the gap. The bar lifted for twenty minutes and then left a thinner place than before. Lunch held with bread that carried hidden brightness and sauce from a brown jar. By half past two the clamp sat in the stomach and the head filled with cotton and pictures of cold brightness arrived with unusual force. She answered with two cans from the cold cabinet and a packet shared though sharing meant most for her. Evenings closed late with tired cooking and a bright close because the day had been long and the close felt earned. Night stretched with scrolling and reaching though supper stood finished an hour behind.

Count the round in her numbers because numbers here tell truth rather than shame.

Three bright drinks a day on desk days. Two bars from the drawer. One tall box every six days. Four spoonfuls in tea though tea never asked. Fourteen extra kilos across three years though meals never grew larger, closes grew. Four dentist visits with two fillings though brushing never slipped. Six Monday vows with six Friday fades though hope stood honest every Monday. Those numbers never proved softness. Those numbers prove flare and fade and picture and entry across ordinary weeks. Ordinary weeks built the arc. Arc never needed extraordinary fault.

The crisis arrived without drama, which is how true costs often arrive.

A birthday passed with rich cake and songs and pictures taken. She ate to be fun and to belong and to mark the day, and the first bites shone while people laughed. Then bites labored while laughter stayed bright without her. She watched herself reach past taste while talk warmed around her and felt the split we named above, want lit while pleasure dimmed. That night the box at home emptied past plan though the day had been full of people loved. She woke at three with coated mouth and turning stomach and stared at the ceiling while the house slept. No single pain stood large enough to name emergency. The whole pattern stood large enough to see trap. She added the numbers she had avoided adding, money by month and hours by week and afternoons lost by year, and saw where brightness had billed her while promising pay.

The shift after held no heroics, which is why I keep it here rather than as lesson.

She changed no other corner of life. She kept the same desk and drawer and shop and hours and people. She stopped ordering entry though pictures still passed for a little. Pictures passed like weather across the first fortnight, bright at half past ten and half past two and after supper, then thinner in the third week, then rare and flat by the sixth. Hunger spoke plainer without flare shouting over it. Fullness spoke louder without vanish hiding it. Real meals tasted brighter without coating dulling them. Mornings held longer without tall boxes spiking them. The drawer stayed shut without guards posted on it because no flare ordered opening. Evenings closed after supper though packets still stood on shelves elsewhere. No single day proved anything large. Many plain days proved everything needful. Plain days kept score fairly though bright pictures had promised score could never hold.

Ask what changed in her across those weeks and what stayed the same person throughout.

Did character harden into new metal or did training thin when unfed? Did hunger change nature or did flare stop shouting over hunger? You know because you have lived thin weeks after flu or travel when bright closes paused and hunger spoke plainer though character never entered the story. Change never came from new strength. Change came from closed entry while pictures thinned. Thin pictures never needed wrestling. Unfed flare never needed chaining. Hands steadied when flare quieted though hands belonged to the same person with the same history. The same person walked the same aisle with different eyes. Different eyes bought different packets though pockets held the same money.

That arc carries no proof about all people and I claim none.

One round told straight never decides all rounds. Told straight it lights one path through woods many walk, with numbers that match too many cupboards to dismiss and a shift that asks no heroics to follow. Take from it what matches your own numbers. Set aside what does not match. What remains after setting aside still stands, that flare can thin when unfed and hunger can speak plainer and days can close plain though shelves still shout. Plain days never needed her to become another woman. Plain days needed entry closed while eyes stayed open. Open eyes close entries that tuned eyes once opened without asking.

I hold that story here in the main flow rather than apart because apart would say appendix and appendix would say doubt.

Main flow says this belongs where argument lives. Doubt would send lived lines to the back while benches keep front shelves. Front shelves already had their hearing above. Lived lines deserve the same room and the same light. No label stands over this room. No box holds it apart. It stands where evenings stand in this book, after wiring and before tables of people, so hands can touch what tuning and lines leave behind in real weeks. Touch it and test it against your own numbers rather than against thunder. Thunder would frighten. Numbers steady. Steady numbers move weight rightly from shoulders to bench.

Keep her plain weeks beside your own counted bags while we settle where anger belongs.

### Where Anger Belongs

We have walked the white room and the long shelf and the loud table and the lit line and one plain arc. One question remains that decides whether this chapter frees or only informs.

Where will you place the anger that rightly rises?

Anger placed on yourself tightens the very pictures this chapter loosened. Self anger says soft hands and weak blood and failed Mondays prove fault. Fault pictures need soothing. Soothing pictures point at brightness. Brightness re-enters and reflares the line. Round goes the week while anger at self hires the very entry anger meant to punish. I have watched that circle turn in myself and in many kitchens and it never once steadied a hand past nine. It never once quieted pictures. It only fed flare with shame dressed as resolve.

Anger placed on the bench loosens what self anger tightens.

Bench anger says tuning tuned and boards portioned and shelves pointed and songs taught children before children could read labels. Bench anger names bliss held at optimum and vanish built to tire less and shares divided without your vote and quiet carriers hiding brightness under orchard paint. Bench anger never touches your worth. It touches drawings and meetings and placements and songs. Drawings can be seen through. Meetings hold no office in your kitchen once seen. Placements lose command when eyes know height tricks. Songs fade when heard as lessons rather than as memory. Seen design steers less though shelves still shout a little. Shout heard as design passes like weather. Weather never owned the house.

Ask what anger ever steadied in your own months and let months answer rather than heat.

Did self anger ever shorten an evening or did it lengthen the story that evenings prove fault? Did nights after scolding close plainer or did scolded nights reach sooner for soothing? You know because you scolded across years with honest heat. Scolding never starved the call. Scolding fed pictures of fault that invited soothing entry. Entry refed the call that scolding meant to starve. That circle never broke from hotter scolding. It breaks from sight placed rightly. Sight says bench built, hands answered, eyes now see. Seen benches lose office. Lost office needs no scolding to stay closed. Closed stays closed while eyes stay open.

We were conned shoppers using lists inside tuned aisles, not weak shoppers failing neutral tables.

Speak that division aloud where bills are paid and feel how it steadies the hand without hardening the heart. Conned never meant foolish. Conned meant led by phoney brightness while hunger did honest work. Honest hunger deserves warm dealing. Led hands deserve plain sight rather than scolding. Warmth toward you never excuses the bench. Hardness toward the bench never chills you. Both stand together through every aisle left to walk. Warmth steadies. Hardness clarifies. Clarified aisles need no guards posted at every corner. Steadied hands buy plain food though bright packets shout on both sides.

Some readers fear that bench anger will sour into quarrels with shops and kin.

Souring never steadied anyone and I do not ask it. Quarrel with no clerk who stocks what boards send. Quarrel with no kin who serves what songs taught. Pity the carts around you that still answer tuning though tuning steers them. Your cart can roll quiet past towers without preaching at towers. Quiet carts persuade more than loud carts because quiet shows plain days hold though shelves shout. Show plain days. Speak plain sight when asked. Leave preaching where quarrels breed. Breed no quarrel. Keep sight. Sight keeps hands steadier than heat ever did.

It is the other way around. Anger at self feeds the round. Anger at design starves it.

Hold that verdict where ropes once pulled and test it nightly. When pictures of fault rise, answer with bench named rather than self blamed. Tuning tuned. Boards portioned. Shelves pointed. Hands answered. Eyes now see. When pictures of grey rooms without brightness rise, answer with credit returned to people and hours and hunger met. Light belonged to rooms rather than to packets though packets stood in rooms. Rooms hold light after packets leave though pictures said otherwise. Both answers slacken fault at once though strain echoes a little. Echoes thin when unfed. Fault falls when bench stands exposed. Exposure needs no new metal. Exposure needs looking held steady till looking completes.

Keep this anger warm and plain while ordinary tables wait with people loved and hours to live.

That sentence hands nothing away and promises no later thunder. It keeps eyes open for what stands nearest, shelves still shouting and packets still bright, while hands learn steadier buying. Steadier buying needs no middle seat because middles were scripts and scripts lost office when tuning showed. Scripts arrive still for a little with your own voice behind them though the writer stands elsewhere. Hear each as a tuned line when it surfaces and it arrives labelled rather than as reasoning. Labelled lines lose command though words stay clever. Clever without command passes like weather. Weather never owned the house.

### SUMMARY

- Want was tuned in white rooms and portioned in meetings before your cart ever rolled, so strong want never proved weak character.
- Long shelves point and teach across every week, and quiet pots and boxes and jars train the same lesson under plain paint.
- Loud foods share one build that opens and pictures and pulls, while quiet foods close, and no hidden wrapper ever proved otherwise.
- The same wanting lines answer plain meals quietly and bright doses with flare, and want outlives taste on every night the round runs.
- One plain arc with counted numbers shows flare thinning when unfed and hunger speaking plainer across ordinary weeks.
- Anger placed on design steadies hands while anger placed on self feeds pictures, so warmth stays with you and hardness stays with the bench.
