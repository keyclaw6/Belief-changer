## 1 Book core

**Target behavior.** Compulsive consumption of refined and added sugar and junk carbs — the craving-snacking loop, the 3pm top-up, the evening box, the celebration exception. Not nutrition pedantry, not cooking skill.

**Reader.** Planner-facing state: an adult who feels trapped in the loop, has tried diets and moderation rules and watched them fail, suspects the whole approach is wrong, eats sweets daily while calling it normal, feels tired and foggy and ashamed after, and wants out without another fight. No proper-name handle. No pupil label.

**Load-bearing false belief.** Bad sugar is a genuine pleasure, comfort or lift that makes life sweeter and would be deprivation to lose.

**Through-line.** The lift is the last low returning toward normal, then guaranteeing the next low. There is nothing to lose. Escape is immediate.

**Format.** Full-length belief-changer, circa 62,000 words planned, in short single-purpose chapters. Front matter carries authority and boxed advisory. Body demolishes on ground already installed. After the vow: last ordinary sweet, one ordinary-life chapter, short photographable recap.

**Fork decisions.**
- Fork 1 inner state: full Carr personification. Name the trivial physical creature to starve and the belief-system that feeds it under original behavior-fitted names frozen in the mantra sheet. No-monster stance is brief-level override only and is not used.
- Fork 2 outcome: total freedom from BAD SUGAR, commanded with cheerful certainty. If there is no benefit in doing it often there is no point doing it occasionally. Moderation is foreclosed with pincer and cliff image. Radical-autonomy moderation is brief-level override only and is not used.
- Fork 3 science weight: hard facts at full Carr strength, vivid and flat and frightening where true is frightening, then reader is told not to change from fear. Facts arrive as settled fact, never literature review. Raw citations may live in back matter. Scare stays in argument, never routed to appendix.
- Fork 4 villain: two villains, both hit hard — the engineered trap and industry that set it, and the named anti-method that kept the reader in it. Past failure is reattributed to method, never reader. Broader anti-recovery crusade is brief-level widening only and is not used.
- Fork 5 void: change nothing else in life. Body returns to natural baseline on its own. Reader inhabits old contexts confidently to prove freedom. Elaborate replacement systems are willpower-era scaffolding and are not used. Real food menu is baseline, not replacement.

**Redefinition and margin-for-error doctrine.** BAD SUGAR is the bad subset. Definition boxed early in CH-01 with decree: when this book says sugar, take it to mean BAD SUGAR.
BAD SUGAR means free and added sugars in any name plus the ultra-processed fat-sugar fast-absorbing foods built to retrigger the loop: candy, chocolate bars, ice cream, pastries, cookies, cake, doughnuts, sugary drinks, sweet snacks, sweetened yoghurts and cereals, sauces and breads where sugar rides hidden, white refined starch binges eaten as sweets. Whole fruit, vegetables, unrefined staples eaten as real meals are not BAD SUGAR and are free.
Inside the line runs full total-abstinence logic: there is no healthy level of BAD SUGAR other than zero. Outside the line, real food is free and trusted.
Margin: body can cope with an occasional accidental blip, mind cannot be given the belief back. Seatbelt image owns this: belt exists for accidents, not for driving erratically. Slip revives nothing unless the belief is allowed back. Guard the belief, not the behavior.

**Clinical and eating-disorder safety perimeter.** General adult edition for everyday loop. Non-goals: medical nutrition therapy, diabetes management advice, eating-disorder treatment. Crisis-pointer territory, not method territory. One plan-wide boxed advisory defined once, printed in front matter, cited on vow card with one plain spoken sentence for readers on medication to involve their doctor. No clinical tails inside instructions. No diagnosis claims from lived quotes. No pathological labeling. No moralizing sweetness as universally forbidden.

**Strongest pro-behavior scene.** Birthday dinner rich-looking chocolate cake on the menu, wedding table passing cake, holiday chocolate core memory, office birthday slice, family Friday ice cream — sweet plus people plus ceremony plus pressure to be fun. Met head-on late, pleasure reassigned drop by drop to people, leisure, hunger satisfied by real food, moment itself.

**Destination state.** Whenever BAD SUGAR crosses mind, reader feels relief and freedom at not doing it. Steady afternoons without top-up. Hunger trusted, fullness noticed, favorite real food enjoyed. No counting days. No reopening decision. Thought reframed to joy. Others pitied, never envied. Slip pre-forgiven as warning, never license.

**Fresh ending reframe, saved for ending.** Sweetness was never in the packet. Life was sweet underneath and the packet was covering it. Appears only in final movement, not argued earlier.

**Method preserved.** Escape not sacrifice on every page. Warm to person, vicious to trap, never contempt for reader. No willpower as solution; willpower reframed as symptom of belief intact. Fear raised at full strength where plan assigns it, then removed as motive by escape-joy in same chapter. Freedom immediate after belief change, conferred as identity, not earned over time. Autonomy: reader investigates and owns conclusion inside frame of flat assertion. Original prose throughout. Fidelity doctrine, founder 2026-07-12.

Narrator autobiography appears only where packets support it. This plan assigns no invented author escape. Authority comes from method, lived lines, and embedded long testimony drawn from banks and written original.
