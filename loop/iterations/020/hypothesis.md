## Root cause

**PRIMARY — plan-card, produced by `prompts/master-plan-skill-v2.md` § Compact chapter cards.** The trace is specific: "CH-12 card assigns `Job: non-argument — bridge — inhabits… without new thesis` but `Structure` lists full scripts (pity-not-envy, reframe-thought, slip-forgiven). CH-13 card says `short recap list… without callbacks` but assigns no entering belief and receives lived freedom — nothing forbids trap re-proof. CH-11 card `Structure: … two relapse doors warned` assigns full pre-arming of bad-day and just-one whispers after CH-10 foreclosed carve-outs." And: "CH-11 card `Belief-now: enters lingering that tomorrow is safer` … re-assigns delay and just-one as live entering beliefs, not token echoes."

The card contract makes this legal. Belief-now and the reserved-later fence are required only "for every argument-bearing card" — so bridge/recap cards (CH-12, CH-13) carry no entering belief and no fence at all. The one field every card does carry for received work is `continuity intent: what understanding it receives and hands forward` — a description, not a closure. A card can "receive lived freedom" and still list the relapse-door scripts as structure bullets; the writer executes structure bullets as full staging (Binding craft 1 and 7 tell it to complete the card's job). The plan-skill fences only forward (reserved-later), never backward (settled-earlier), so CH-11 can legally enter from "tomorrow is safer" one card after CH-10 installed totality.

**SECONDARY — style-guide `prompts/style-guide.md` §B5 operator 1.** "Fact-assertion — deliver reframes as flat settled fact: 'The fact is...' / 'The reality is...' Never hedge." Combined with the writer's evidence-honesty duty (honour limits by not overclaiming), the model resolves the conflict by fusing both: `The fact is` + a scoped, hedged evidence tail ("at population level… linked with"). The cadence becomes a sentence template carrying literature-review texture — exactly what judges tag as factory-speech/wrong-register.
