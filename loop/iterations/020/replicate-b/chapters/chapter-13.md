FREE FOR GOOD

**IN THIS CHAPTER**

*You have lost nothing, you mourn nothing, you are free — and sweetness is yours again.*

### NOTHING LEFT OPEN

You wake clear. You walk the bright aisle at ease. You sit to real food that satisfies. This is lived ease, not practice, and it is yours now.

All that was argued is shut, and I hold it shut so we do not turn back. The afternoon rescue is finished; the lift only made the dip it claimed to fix. The cinema warmth belonged to people and laughter, never to the packet. Clean hunger quiets and gentle fullness arrives when real food is trusted. The edgy low was built by the last dose and kept alive by a loud story. The failed diets belonged to the wrong method that had no finish line. The wired-this-way excuses were scripts, never character. The birthday table kept all its love with the dose gone. A hard day asks for no rescue fix. A single exception never stays single. The last ordinary sweet was faced fully, the vow was spoken, and ordinary mornings prove it.

Do you see? There is no benefit left to debate and no point left to prove.

We lived inside the sugar trap for years and called it choice. We believed a dose was a genuine pleasure, treat or lift and feared life would be dull without it. We woke tired, flat and craving and called that normal. You see it now for what it was.

Will it last? Ask yourself what there is to miss.

### WHAT YOU CARRY WITH YOU

You carry no system and no struggle. You carry seeing.

You came with nothing to risk, for you have absolutely nothing to lose and everything to gain, and that is still true. You stepped out easily, immediately and permanently, not over time, not on trial.

Eat whatever real food you like, whenever you are hungry, and you will never miss BAD SUGAR. That was my promise at the start. It is your experience now. Keep eating what you love when hungry. Keep your tables, your mornings, your shops. Change nothing else. Freedom needs no props.

If a faint whine stirs, smile — it is only the Crumb dying, not you wanting. You know that tug now for what it is — an empty, slightly edgy hunger that feels like it needs something sweet — and you know it passes. If a grand story plays about reward or comfort, pity the Confectioner voice and do not obey it. It rented warmth and charged craving.

Hooked, we faced edginess for the rest of your life. Free, you face clear mornings for the rest of your life. The belt is there for accidents, not for driving at the wall. An accidental taste puts nothing back unless the belief is invited back, so notice it and walk on.

I stood in that bright aisle myself, basket light, head clear, and laughed at how loud the packets once sounded. That ease has stayed with me every day since. It will stay with you.

Sweetness was never in the packet; you are returning sweetness to where it lived all along — in you, in the moment, in real food. That is marvellous, wonderful, worth celebrating. Rejoice that you no longer feed the con. Get on with enjoying your life.
