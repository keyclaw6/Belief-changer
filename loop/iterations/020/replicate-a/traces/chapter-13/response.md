Chapter 13
FREE FOR GOOD

**IN THIS CHAPTER**
A small list kept where glad eyes can find it, a quietly fastened seatbelt, sweetness home at last

*There is nothing to miss and nothing to keep — you are free and life is sweet again.*

MORNING LIGHT STILL CLEAR

You wake clear, and the day does not call for a fix.

That ease you felt yesterday was no accident. It is ordinary life without the noise. Hunger comes cleanly and fullness arrives quietly. Shops are calm. Plates satisfy. A passing sweet thought is met with joy.

I tell you plainly as one who escaped the same con: this is how free people live.

All that was proved now stands closed. The afternoon lift was seen as the drop it created. The cinema treat was returned to the people who owned it. Hunger and fullness were trusted to do their quiet work. The low was seen as the trap's own work. Every past collapse was laid at the wrong method. The scattered excuses lost their footing. The sweetest table stayed warm without the dose. The solemn vow was made on an ordinary sweet noticed fully. Mornings, shops and meals now prove the freedom in daily life.

You do not need to rehearse any of it. You live it.

WHAT YOU NOW OWN

You started because you have absolutely nothing to lose and everything to gain, and you finished because you saw the truth.

We were caught in the sugar trap together, you and I, feeding the Crumb while the Confectioner took thanks for the con. We believed in a genuine pleasure, treat or lift that never lived in the packet. We mistook an empty, slightly edgy hunger that feels like it needs something sweet for need, and paid for each brief quieting with tired, flat and craving.

You see it now. The packet never gave. It only lent back a little of what the last packet took, then left the want louder. Real food satisfies. Real hunger ends. Real moments hold their own warmth.

You escaped easily, immediately and permanently, not by force but by sight. You are free for the rest of your life because there is nothing left to want.

Eat whatever real food you like, whenever you are hungry, and you will never miss BAD SUGAR. That was my promise at the start. It is your experience now.

IF YOU OPENED HERE FIRST

If you turned to these last pages hoping for the ending without the journey, pause.

This list alone cannot free you. The beliefs behind it must be seen, not borrowed. Put this book down, go to the first page, read it all in order with an open mind, and keep living exactly as normal until the vow. Come back here when you are champing to be free. The ending will still be waiting, and it will be yours.

SWEETNESS WHERE IT LIVES

Look around at the life you already have.

Morning light. Work done well. Laughter at a shared table. The taste of fresh food eaten hungry. The calm of an evening with nothing edging you toward the cupboard. This was always where sweetness lived.

Sweetness was never in the packet. You are returning sweetness to where it lived all along — in you, in the moment, in real food.

That is why there is no sense of loss. No one mourns a trick once seen. You do not look back at tired shoppers under bright lights with envy. You feel warm pity, for you stood where they stand, and you stepped outside.

Keep your margin the way a careful driver keeps a seatbelt there for an accidental jolt, never for driving erratically. An accident revives nothing. A deliberate taste would only rebuild the want you starved. You will plan no such taste, bright day or dark, because you see there is no point in paying again for tiredness.
