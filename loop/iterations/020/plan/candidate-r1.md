# Master Plan — Quit Sugar
`production-books/quit-sugar/master-plan.md`

## 1. Book Core

**Target behavior:** Compulsive consumption of refined / added sugar and junk carbs — the craving-snacking loop, not nutrition pedantry.

**Reader (planner-facing state):** An adult who grazes, binges, or yo-yos daily, has failed diets and moderation rules, feels loss of control with shame after, and suspects the whole approach is wrong — but still believes sweetness lives in the sweet.

**Load-bearing false belief:** Bad sugar is a genuine pleasure, treat, reward and energy lift that makes life sweeter, and life without it would be deprivation.

**Through-line:** From “sugar makes life sweeter” to “sugar stole sweetness; seeing the con returns sweetness to life itself” — escape, not sacrifice.

**Format:** Full-length Carr, 13 chapters, ~60,000 words planned. Short recap only at end. Long testimony in main flow. Myths battery as distinct room. No mid-book recap.

**Fork decisions:**
- Fork 1 — Full Carr personification. Little creature: THE CRUMB. Big system: THE CONFECTIONER.
- Fork 2 — Total cessation of BAD SUGAR, commanded with cheerful certainty. Moderation foreclosed by pincer and cliff image.
- Fork 3 — Hard facts at full Carr force, flat and frightening where true, then relief in same movement. No literature-review texture. Citations quarantined to appendix.
- Fork 4 — Two villains: the engineered sugar trap / industry, and the named anti-method THE WILLPOWER METHOD. Warm to person, harsh to trap and wrong method.
- Fork 5 — Change nothing else in your life. Natural baseline returns. No replacement system, no void-filling curriculum.

**Redefinition:** BAD SUGAR means refined sugar, added free sugars, and ultra-processed sweet/starchy junk carbs engineered for craving. When this book says sugar, take it to mean BAD SUGAR. Real food, fruit as food, and ordinary meals are not the target and need no permission. Total-abstinence trap logic runs inside the line: there is no healthy level of BAD SUGAR other than zero as a habit.
Margin-for-error doctrine: the body can cope with an occasional accidental blip, but the mind cannot keep a deliberate exception. Seatbelt logic — there for accidents, not for driving erratically.

**Clinical / eating-disorder safety perimeter:** No medical nutrition therapy, no diabetes management, no eating-disorder treatment, no weight-loss mechanics, no diagnosis. Money / weight / dental gains are bonus, never motive. Lived quotes never become medical claims. Population facts never become personal sentences. All qualified limits live once in plan-wide advisory SAFE-A and in ledger safety limits, never inside instruction wording.

**Strongest pro-behavior scene:** A warm celebration shared with loved people — birthday table / cinema chocolate — where the sweet feels like love itself.

**Destination state:** Whenever the reader thinks about BAD SUGAR, they feel relief and freedom that they no longer do it; stopping feels like escaping a trap, not sacrificing a pleasure. Free on the vow, happy to be free thereafter.

**Saved ending reframe (appears only in final movement):** Sweetness was never in the packet; you are returning sweetness to where it lived all along — in you, in the moment, in real food.

**Method preserved:** Escape not sacrifice. Warm to person / vicious to trap, never contempt for reader. No willpower. Fear raised at full force where plan assigns it, then disowned by the escape. Immediate freedom after belief change. Autonomy of conclusion inside commanded frame. Original prose. Fork-1 line per style guide default.

**Moment-of-revelation prediction:** A dull afternoon, a supermarket aisle, a morning after — the reader notices the edgy hunger pass without a fix and feels ease where the loop used to shout.

**E-13 — Crash mechanism with limit**

- Research units: synthesis bullets S-21 / S-43. Sources: S-21, S-43.
