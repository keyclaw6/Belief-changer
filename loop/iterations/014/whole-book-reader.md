## What 013’s 14-chapter spine actually produced

### 1. Creatures / body ground in the first third — planted, not lived

**Creatures: closed vs 012.** Chapter 2 names both when the trap is first seen, in passing, as vocabulary not a unit:

**Body / instinct / real food: a refrain, not a world.** GSBS spends early rooms watching creatures eat, instinct as the diet you were born with, the body as the authority. 013 plants the triad as a slogan underfoot:

> And there, underfoot the whole time, is what you already own: your body, your instinct and real food. (Ch2)

**First third as lived:** Ch1 contract, Ch2 trap+creatures, Ch3 axis switch. That is method. It is not Carr’s world-first-third.

## What a 014 prompt batch can close (and what it cannot)

A batch on **writer prompt + style-guide + plan-skill/reviewer cards** can close the remaining *whole-book texture* without touching judges, PROGRAM, or models:

1. **You, not Maya.** Ban the name. Scenes stay second-person lived (“It is 3:02. The drawer rattles.”).
2. **IN THIS CHAPTER as rooms.** Destination names, no “we will / you will / Maya.”
3. **CA-01 box stays; header goes.** Ordinary boxed medical limit. No `CA-SAFE`, no “this notice is not part of the belief argument.”
4. **Silent fear hinge.** Facts, then relief. Never “I say this flat” / “I promised the hard truth.”
5. **Echo, don’t restage.** Con, coaster, shoes, cords, sofa, lion: token after debut. Cards must not re-assign a full staging.
6. **One mid-book eating inhabit.** Hunger, satisfaction, real food as favourite — a room, not a kill, not a Ch11 demo.
7. **First-third body as scenes.** Creatures already named; give instinct/body/real food encounters (watch eating, the body as authority) so demolitions stand on ground, not on the triad slogan.
8. **No IDs in prose.** SE-xx, S-xx, E-xx, CA-SAFE as a label.
9. **Post-vow: live, don’t teach.** Ch13 mornings/shops/table without sofa-rebuild or three-thought curriculum. Thoughts already owned, once, at the vow.
10. **Stop announcing the course.** No “in the next three chapters we will,” “I promised you a gate,” “this line debuts here,” “we will not write you a manual.”

A prompt batch **cannot** make Nature’s Guide appear if the plan still sequences fourteen kills. The plan-skill Arc must keep the 013 spine jobs (world → kills on that ground → one eating inhabit → fear → willpower → last meal → one life → list) and stop filling every card with a new demolition plus reserved-later.
