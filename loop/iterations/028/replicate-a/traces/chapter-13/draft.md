Chapter 13
GET ON WITH ENJOYING LIFE

**IN THIS CHAPTER**
A phone held over an open page, a closed book on a clean table, a warm meal waiting in the next room

*You need no more teaching to stay free — you need only to live.*

### IF YOU OPENED HERE FIRST

If you opened the book here, stop.

This page is not the method. This page is saved reminders for readers already free. It will make no sense on its own, and it will not free you on its own.

I speak plain because I respect you. Begin at the first page and read through with an open mind. Carry on exactly as normal while you read. Let each seeing do its work in order. Then come back here and this short list will hold you for life.

Why do I turn you away? Because freedom here came from seeing, not from studying. Seeing arrives in order. The morning makes sense after the night is named. The vow makes sense after the glow is killed. The list makes sense after the eyes are open.

We once tried to collect tips about BAD SUGAR. Tips about doses. Tips about fixes. Tips about hits at three in the afternoon and binges at ten at night. We underlined and nodded and stayed trapped. Tips never touched the belief that held us.

So hear me with warmth and do as I ask. Close this chapter now. Start at the beginning. Read the whole book. When you reach this page again you will laugh at how light the list feels.

And if you have read through, if you have taken your last ordinary treat with attention and vowed freedom and walked your first ordinary days with hunger clean and head quiet — then stay with me. This is your closing. This is your photograph. This is your permission to live.

Did tips ever free you before, or did seeing free you now?

Did you arrive here by force, or by truth recognised?

Is this page the work, or is this page the reminder of work already done?

There is only one honest reading. The work is done. This page keeps the meaning.

### YOU NEED NO FURTHER TEACHING

"I feel marvellous — but surely I must keep learning to stay free?"

I hear that whisper because we all heard it. We lived years believing sweetness was complicated, that bodies were mysteries, that appetite needed management, that one more article, one more rule, one more plan would at last give us control over BAD SUGAR doses.

See how false that fear was.

You do not stay free by study. You stay free by seeing kept. The con that held us was never lack of information. The con was a false belief that a coated dose gave love, lift, reward, rest. Once that belief dies, what is there to study?

Ask and answer honest, as a free eater.

Do hunger and satisfaction need a manual, or do they lead plainly when no loud dose shouts over them?

Does a clean mouth need lessons to taste bread warm and fruit cold and loud, or does it taste on its own?

Does a passing sweet thought need a course to meet it, or does relief answer on its own?

There is only one honest reading. Hunger leads. Satisfaction closes. Relief answers. No course runs them.

I lived trapped for years with cupboards full of rules. Good this week. Bad that night. Deserved treat after hard day. Short term lift then fast drop, then another lift to repair the drop. I called that knowledge. It was noise. The night I saw that BAD SUGAR gave nothing, the noise stopped. No further lesson was needed. Mornings cleared. Baskets quietened. Suppers satisfied. Evenings opened.

You have lived the same days. You woke with mouth clean. You walked the bright aisle with pity, never envy. You ate supper glad till rest came. You sat near old motions and felt no pull, only relief. Those days taught nothing new. They proved what seeing gave.

This matters because the old voice will dress itself as wisdom. "Keep reading about it. Keep watching yourself. Keep guarding." Hear the script for what it is. That voice keeps the question open to keep the glow alive. A free eater does not guard all day. A free eater lives all day, and a passing thought brings joy on its own.

You are not on a programme. You are not on an edge. You stand on ground. Ground holds without study.

It is the other way around. Study was the trap — counting, hiding, starting again on Mondays. Freedom is the end of study. All you have to do is follow all the instructions.

Hold that ease. That ease is the proof.

### WHICH REST OF LIFE DO YOU WANT

Stand a moment and look at time.

We all carry one phrase in the chest, heavy or light. For the rest of your life. Say it hooked and it sounds like a sentence. Say it free and it sounds like a gift. The words are the same. The meaning is opposite. Choose which rest-of-life you want, and choose now with eyes open.

Picture the hooked rest. Dragging a dose through fog for the rest of your life. Morning promise and afternoon chase. Evening box emptied with regret before swallow ends. Money paid. Teeth taxed. Hours lost. Head dim at the edges. Belly never met, only silenced for minutes, then louder. Every single day forever for something you never needed.

We lived that rest. We know its texture. Coated tongue seeking to scrape itself clean. Hands moving before mind decides. Laughter a shade flatter after. Love borrowed and billed. We called the chase appetite. We called the film pleasure. We called slavery normal because everyone ate it.

Does that look like enjoyment?

Does that look like love?

Would you sign for that rest with a clear head?

There is only one honest reading. No clear head signs.

Now picture the free rest. Living clear and light for the rest of your life. Hunger calling clean. Food tasting bright. Mornings beginning with hunger, never with repair. Shops walked rejoicing. Suppers ending in rest. Evenings owned from edge to edge. No bargain. No chase. No film. No fog. No head-noise after.

You have tasted that rest in your first free days. The light in the kitchen a little brighter because no film sits on the tongue. Fruit biting back. Bread chewing with pleasure. Hunger met in minutes by real food, then gone. The sofa holding you without bargaining. The night remembered whole.

Which rest holds mornings clear without watching?

Which rest holds baskets quiet without bracing?

Which rest holds tables glad without counting?

There is only one honest reading. The free rest holds all three.

I tell you flat, with total certainty, as one who walked both rests. The hooked rest gives nothing and taxes everything. The free rest takes nothing and returns everything. The difference was never the dose. The difference was the belief, and the belief is dead.

So choose. There is no middle rest where a Friday dose keeps the glow but drops the bill. The bill rides with the glow. The glow dies with the belief.

Choose the free rest. Marvellous. Wonderful. Yours from the belly up.

### SWEETNESS HANDED BACK

Hear the last reframe, saved for this closing, because only a free mouth can hear it true.

Nothing sweet was taken from you.

Sweetness was stolen from you by the trap and has now been handed back. This was never about quitting. It was about growing back into the eater you were.

We began as eaters who knew. Hunger hollow and clean. Food loud and glad. Stop in the middle of a bite to run outside, half a peach dropped and forgotten, because play called louder than fullness. No counting. No guilt. No coated call for more after rest. The body led. Joy followed.

Then bright packets taught us to doubt the lead. Family habit. Cartoon faces. Bliss-point packs built to ring the bell before the mouth speaks. Checkout ambush at tired height. Words like treat, share, love printed large to dress a small, loaded dose. We learned to answer every hollow with a named packet. We learned to call flood lift. We learned to call film pleasure. We learned to call chase appetite.

See what theft that was. Not theft of health alone, though health paid. Not theft of money alone, though money paid. Theft of sweetness itself. The tongue filmed cannot taste. The belly flooded cannot rest. The head fogged cannot remember laughter whole. We reached for sweetness and got coating. We reached for love and got rent due every afternoon.

Ask with the taste of free days still on the tongue, and answer honest.

Did the child leave hunger to run outside because food was grey, or because hunger met is complete?

Did your first free supper bore without a sweet close, or did the meal complete itself and rest hold?

Does fruit taste duller with a clean mouth, or sharper, colder, louder?

There is only one honest reading. Sweetness lived at the source all along. The packet borrowed it and taxed it.

I eat now with more pleasure than in all the years of chasing, because pleasure needs a clean mouth and an honest hunger. Soup deepens. Herbs lift. Bread warms. An apple cold in the palm cracks and floods. Supper ends and evening begins with no chase after. That is not less sweetness. That is sweetness returned to its owner.

You have not lost the cinema glow. The people gave the glow. The story gave the glow. Laughter gave the glow. The square sneaked a ride and sent the bill. Replay that warmth without the dose and sweetness stands unchanged. You have not lost birthdays. Light and song and people gave the joy. The cake rode along.

So hear the growing truth and let it settle. You are not a quitter mourning a friend. You are a grower returned to nature. The theft is over. The eater stands where the child stood, hungrier, clearer, gladder, with a lifetime of meals ahead to meet and enjoy.

Eat what you want, when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and never punish yourself again.

That is not a rule. That is nature remembered.

### GO AND LIVE

Now get on with enjoying your life.

This is the outward push. Not inward to watch. Outward to live. Birthdays. Cinemas. Work desks at three. Evening sofas. Morning kitchens. Same life with new eyes. Change nothing else in your life to stay free.

Freedom proves itself by inhabiting every old context confidently, never by hiding. Go to the shop as usual. Cook supper hungry. Sit close and laugh full. Drink water. Stretch. Walk the dog. Answer the mail. Carry bags with mouth clean and head quiet. Let visible ease do the talking. Preaching breeds debate, and debate reopens in your own head the very question you have closed.

Talk little about it. Live much. If asked how you left fog without force, speak plain. I saw it gives nothing. I eat when hungry. I stop at satisfied. I am happy to be free. No lecture. No purity. No contempt for the plate in front of them. A free life invites where argument repels.

And when a thought crosses — shop smell, wrapper sound, tired evening hour — let it come and change what it means. Think the replacement at once, loud with delight, from the belly. Feel relief flood warm that evenings are owned.

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!

Think it now and feel the shape. Not wistful. Not brave. Joyful. The way a debtor laughs at a burned bill. Brilliant, because the con is seen. Free, because mouth belongs to hunger again. Say it till it fires on its own. Whenever the old image glows, think BRILLIANT! I'M FREE! and watch the glow go out like a lamp in morning sun.

If you live with diabetes, take medicine that moves blood sugar, are pregnant, live with risk around food past or present, follow your clinician first and use this book for belief only.

That is the whole guard. Same streets. Same shelves. Same tables. New eyes. Pity the chase. Rejoice you left it.

Do you feel how light that is? No rule to polish. No performance to sustain. No calendar to watch. Only hunger met, satisfaction honoured, and days walked through rejoicing.

Celebrate the plain days. They are the prize. A clear kitchen. A quiet basket. A satisfied belly. A laughed-through evening remembered whole. This is what we wanted when we reached for sweetness — life sweet at its source.

You left easily by seeing, not by force. Live now by seeing, never by bracing.
