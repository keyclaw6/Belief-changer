Evidence: “You saw the title and part of you lifted. Another part sank. You have been here before.” and “There is only one honest answer to all three, and you already feel it. That is why you are still reading.” and “If what I say is untrue, you will have lost a few hours of reading and kept your habit exactly as it was. If what I say is true, you will have gained your mornings, your evenings, your appetite, your ease in shops and kitchens, and the quiet pride of no longer being tugged.”

MOVE G01-M2: PRESENT — The real chapter promises complete freedom easily, painlessly, and permanently without willpower or deprivation; ours states the same easy-immediate-permanent triad and frames freedom as recognition, not struggle, with deprivation explicitly denied. Evidence: “You will stop easily, immediately and permanently.” and “You do not have to summon courage you do not have. You do not have to arrange your life around avoidance. You do not have to count days or carry a score.” and “Taking the sweet hits away is not deprivation. It is removal of an extra load.” and “Freedom here is immediate relief when the con is seen, not a future reward earned by struggle.”

MOVE G01-M3: PRESENT — The real chapter’s “don’t change anything yet” instruction to keep consuming sugar as normal until told otherwise is fully performed; ours repeats the same contract across multiple sections. Evidence: “You do not have to change anything about what you eat while you read. Your cupboard can stay full. Your routine can stay exactly as it is.” and “We start with the belief and leave the behaviour alone until the belief changes. That is why you are to carry on exactly as normal while you read. Buy as usual. Snack as usual. Do not cut down, do not skip, do not test yourself.” and “So keep your cupboard full for now. Keep your routine. Keep your doubts.”

MOVE G01-M4: PARTIAL — The real chapter names Easyway, traces it to the smoking cure, and declares “IT WORKS” as a distinct method-not-diet beat; ours firmly rejects diet-lecture framing and asks the reader to follow instructions, but never names Easyway or lands the explicit proven-method pedigree turn. Evidence: “This is not a diet book. It is not a nutrition lecture.” and “There is no magic in me. There is clarity in the method.
