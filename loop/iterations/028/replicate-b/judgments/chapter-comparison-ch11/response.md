MOVE G18-M1: PRESENT — Carr names the final meal as a deliberate, time-stamped ritual after refusing “meaningless days,” and ours does the same by collapsing tomorrow into now and staging the last ordinary sweet at the ordinary hour and place. Evidence: “There is no safer tomorrow. There is only a precious today stretched into a precious week stretched into a precious year.”; “Then do this. Not as a test. Not as an experiment. Not as a laboratory tasting with notebook and timer. As the last ordinary instance, taken normally, in your ordinary place, at your ordinary hour.”; “Fetch your ordinary sweet. The biscuit you always reach for. The bar you keep for evenings. The dessert you call a treat.”; “Now take your last ordinary treat and close it with joy.”

MOVE G18-M2: PRESENT — Carr’s vow is the instant breakpoint—never again, freedom at the finish of the last mouthful—and ours lands that same conferral-by-vow without a waiting period. Evidence: “Now, while the taste still sits true on the tongue, speak the vow. Solemn. Quiet. Joyful. Not to me. To yourself, for life.”; “Say: I see it does nothing for me. I am done feeding it. I am free.”; “You are free as of now. Not after three days. Not after the hump passes. Not after some test proves it. Now.”; “Immediately because freedom is conferred by vow, not earned by time.”; “This ends easily, immediately and permanently.”

MOVE G18-M3: PRESENT — Carr closes the ritual with triumph and elation (“CONGRATULATIONS! YOU’VE WON!”), not grief, and ours answers with the same celebratory register and explicit rejection of mourning. Evidence: “Feel relief flood in. Marvellous lightness. Wonderful clarity.”; “BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!”; “Rejoice you are free. Celebrate you are free.”; “Happy to be free — not merely free, but glad, proud, excited, relieved. That emotion is the goal, and it is yours now.”; “No loss inside it. No longing inside it. Only pity for trapped eaters still queuing, and relief you no longer queue.”
