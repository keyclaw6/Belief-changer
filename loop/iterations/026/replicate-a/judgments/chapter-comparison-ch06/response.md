Evidence: “Pick up a tomato and smell it. Your mouth waters before you decide to want it.”; “Push the plate a little away. Sit. Feel that full, easy warmth spreading. Not stuffed. Not heavy. Satisfied.”; “Eat what you want, when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and never punish yourself again.”

Evidence (turn lands): “Will you call that table poor? Or will you recognise it as the feast you chased in wrappers and never found?”; “That is not less joy. MOVE G13-M3: PRESENT — Carr argues ordinary fruit-and-vegetable eating is mankind’s true favourite, not a consolation prize after brainwashing; ours makes ordinary hungry meals the destination feast, not grey settlement. Evidence: “We were told plain food is dull. When did plain food ever taste dull to a hungry person?”; “Would a hungry person call warm bread grey? It is relief. It is the door opening onto ordinary days that taste better than dosed days ever did.”
