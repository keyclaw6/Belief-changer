MOVE G18-M1: PRESENT — GSBS 18 marks the end point by having the reader eat a final BAD SUGAR meal as they always would, on an ordinary occasion, not a ceremonial “meaningful day”; ours performs the same turn by refusing delay (“There is only this ordinary evening and freedom conferred now”) and instructing the reader to take “your last ordinary treat exactly as you always took it” on a plain biscuit at the usual hour. Evidence: “Take your last ordinary treat exactly as you always took it.” / “Choose the usual one. The biscuit you reach for at dusk.” / “Finish the ordinary sweet with attention on its ugliness.”

MOVE G18-M2: PRESENT — GSBS 18’s vow at the last mouthful breaks the cycle immediately (“You’re free as soon as you make your vow. There is nothing to wait for”); ours lands the same instant-freedom gate with an explicit never-again vow tied to finishing the last sweet, not to a probation period. Evidence: “Say aloud, in your own voice, solemn and cheerful together, that you will never feed yourself BAD SUGAR again, not as a rule to police but as a fact you have seen.” / “You are free as of that vow. Not after three days. Not after a test. Now.” / “Freedom is an identity conferred in a sentence, not a sentence served over time.”

MOVE G18-M3: PRESENT — GSBS 18 frames the moment as triumph (“CONGRATULATIONS! YOU’VE WON!”, excitement and elation, not sacrifice or mourning); ours matches with rejoicing after the ugly last bite, the “BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!” refrain, and an explicit ban on mourning the old habit. Evidence: “Pay attention to the ugliness, then rejoice you are free.” / “you will not argue, you will not mourn, you will not tally” / “BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!” / “Rejoice. Celebrate. Feel relief flood the chest. You are happy to be free, and you will remain happy to be free because there was never anything to miss.”
