## 1. Book Core

**Target behavior:** Compulsive consumption of BAD SUGAR — refined/added sugars, junk carbs and ultra-processed starchy products consumed in the craving-snacking loop. Not food, cooking, or eating as such.

**Reader:** One general adult reader who feels trapped in that loop, has tried diets, moderation rules and willpower and watched each fail, and suspects the whole approach is wrong. Expressed through four functional personas:
- P-01 The loss-of-control binger (one-bite-becomes-the-box, secret eating, shame cycle)
- P-02 The in-denial moderate (daily grazing framed as "just a treat," carve-outs)
- P-03 The comfort/identity eater (sugar as reward, love, deserved pay-off)
- P-04 The energy-crash yo-yoer (sugar as fuel for the 3pm slump)

**Load-bearing false belief:** *BAD SUGAR gives me a genuine pleasure, treat or energy lift that makes life sweeter and I would be deprived without it.*

**Through-line:** You were conned into *creating* the hunger you then pay again to relieve. The rescuer is the perpetrator.

**Format:** Full-length Carr book, 20 chapters + Front Matter. Pocket-book spine expanded to full-length architecture per §B10. Chapter anatomy per B10: IN THIS CHAPTER bullets, italic thesis line, titled body sections landing on ALL-CAPS verdicts, one instruction at climax where assigned, SUMMARY recap bullets. Final chapter is verbatim recap of numbered instructions with chapter cross-references. Citations quarantined to appendix.

**Fork decisions — explicit:**

- **Fork 1 — Inner monster:** DEFAULT Carr. Personify at full strength. Little Monster equivalent = **the Sugar Imp** (trivial physical dip). Big Monster equivalent = **the Treat Spell** (lifelong brainwashing that interprets the twinge as pleasure/reward/fuel). Both named, frozen, and used to the last page. No Burgeon no-monster blend.
- **Fork 2 — Abstinence vs autonomy:** DEFAULT Carr — **total cessation of BAD SUGAR**, commanded with cheerful certainty. Moderation foreclosed with pincer ("if no benefit often, no benefit occasionally") and cliff-jump image. Moderation is not willpower-hard; it is logically absurd. Autonomy rhetoric retired.
- **Fork 3 — Science weight:** DEFAULT Carr — **hard facts at full, flat, frightening force, then explicitly disowned** (scare-then-disown). Facts arrive as "The fact is..." never as hedged review. No omission, no softening.
- **Fork 4 — Villain:** DEFAULT Carr extended — **two villains, both hit hard:** the engineered trap (sugar/junk-food industry, bliss-point engineering, infinite availability) AND the named anti-method **the willpower method** (and its diet/moderation variants). Reader never villainized.
- **Fork 5 — Void:** DEFAULT Carr — **"Change nothing else in your life."** Body returns to natural baseline on its own. No elaborate replacement system. Good food and good life fill the space without design. Burgeon's root-craving + transmutation is not deployed (behavior is consumptive, not time-identity-occupying).

**Redefinition and margin-for-error doctrine:**
BAD SUGAR is defined in Ch.1, boxed, in CAPS, and decreed: **BAD SUGAR = refined/added sugars + ultra-processed carbs + starchy junk carbs that behave like sugar in the body (white bread, crisps, fries, pastries, sweetened drinks, confectionery, desserts, biscuits, sugary cereals). When this book says SUGAR, take it to mean BAD SUGAR.** Good sugar (whole fruit, vegetables, intact whole foods eaten as food) is not target and becomes part of Nature's Guide. Full trap logic (no healthy level other than zero) runs *inside* the BAD SUGAR line. Conditional bonus: alcohol/dairy are NOT required quits — framed as exciting optional upgrades with pointer, autonomy preserved.

Margin-for-error: *Your body can cope with an occasional, accidental blip; your mind cannot cope with a deliberate exception.* A slip revives nothing physical unless the Treat Spell is allowed back to re-brainwash you. Seatbelt image: the buffer is there for accidents, not for driving erratically. Guard the belief, not every crumb.

**Clinical / eating-disorder safety perimeter:** Book is for general adult trapped eater, not medical nutrition therapy, diabetes management, or eating-disorder treatment. No diagnosis, no prescribed eating disorder behaviours, no weight-loss mechanics, no absolute dietary prescription outside BAD SUGAR line. Boxed advisory in FM + Ch.1 + Ch.14: *If you have diabetes, an eating disorder, are on prescribed medication or therapeutic diet, or are under clinical care, do not change medical treatment on the basis of this book — speak to your clinician. This book changes a belief; it does not give medical advice.* Willpower-speak about purging/restriction banned. Any instruction that could conflict with care carries safety exception in its frozen wording. Per-ledger safety limits preserved in writer assignment.

**Strongest pro-behavior scene:** P-03's late-afternoon celebration — sunlit café/table after a win, or couch + cinema + chocolate, or family dessert ritual — where BAD SUGAR feels inseparable from love, reward and "I deserve this." Met head-on in Ch.12; every drop of pleasure reassigned to its true sources.

**Destination state:** Whenever the reader thinks about BAD SUGAR, they feel relief and freedom that they no longer do it. Stopping is felt as escaping a trap, not sacrificing a pleasure. Happy to be free, from day one.

**Fresh ending reframe (saved for close):** *You didn't give something up. You grew out of it.* The whole project was not quitting but growing — burgeon — into the eater you were before the trap.
