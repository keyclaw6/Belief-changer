### THE 0–20 GAUGE YOU WERE BORN WITH

Picture the fuel gauge in your car. Empty is 0. Full is 20.

At 0 you are truly empty — light-headed, hollow, ready to eat anything wholesome with gratitude. At 10 you are neutral — neither hungry nor full, content to get on with your life. At 20 you are stuffed, tight, uncomfortable.

Nature's Guide lives on that dial.

The fact is, true hunger does not arrive as a panic. It arrives as a clean, quiet signal. A gentle emptying. A calm thought: food would be good now. Not "I need something RIGHT NOW." Not the rollercoaster urge that spikes and crashes and shouts. Hunger is polite. Wanting shouts.

We learned to misread the dial. BAD SUGAR pushed the needle artificially. The lift you called energy was your body clearing the last dose. The dip after was the bill arriving, not a fuel warning. And the Treat Spell translated that dip as hunger.

But everyday slumps are not clinically low blood sugar. That condition is rare. In healthy people without diabetes, symptoms often appear without any true low number, and true low sugar is uncommon. Your three p.m. fog was not proof you needed BAD SUGAR. It was proof BAD SUGAR had just left.

Do you see? You did not need fuel. You needed the rescuer to stop pushing you in.

Ask yourself honestly, you who knows the rollercoaster:

When you feel that sudden, restless "I need something sweet" at four p.m., are you at 0? Or are you at 9, 10, 11 — comfortable, fed — with a small twinge laid on top?

When you feel real hunger after a morning of real work and no snacks, does it feel frantic and choosy? Or does it feel calm and open to real food?

You know. Hunger is calm and open. Craving is restless and choosy. It wants one fix only.

I will give you the practice so you can feel it today.

Close your eyes for a moment and locate the needle.

Don't judge it. Don't try to change it. Just read it.

Where are you right now, 0 to 20?

If you are 0–6, you are truly hungry. Eat. Enjoy.

If you are 7–13, you are neutral. You don't need to eat to fix anything. You can wait. You can have a glass of water, get on with enjoying your life, and let hunger come when it comes.

If you are 14–20, you are full. No fix will make you more full. It will only make you stuffed.

No counting. No points. One glance.

Do you feel how simple that is?

You think: but will I be hungry forever if I stop BAD SUGAR?

Ask the opposite question. Have you not been hungry forever *inside* the trap? Grazing hourly, never sure, always thinking about food, always a little unsatisfied even when full? That is not Nature's Guide. That is the Treat Spell keeping the dial jittering.

Free, you will be hungry sometimes. Truly hungry. Briefly. Beautifully. Then you will eat and be satisfied. Hunger returns as a clean wave, not a constant noise.

One killer pair to lock it:

You were not hungry all the time because you lacked food. You felt hungry all the time because you kept feeding the want.

Listen again. The needle steadies the moment you stop shaking it.

### THE RAW TEST — WOULD YOU EAT IT PLAIN AND BE GLAD?

Now the second instrument. It takes five seconds and it answers the question the gauge clarifies.

We have learned to call wanting hunger. The Treat Spell is fluent in that translation. So we need a simple check that strips away conditioning and leaves only true appetite.

This is the Raw Test.

Ask: would I eat this food raw and plain and be glad? Or do I only want it coated, sweetened, crisped, salted, engineered?

Imagine an apple, clear and cold, or a handful of nuts, or warm fish, or plain beans and rice, or a bowl of salad with olive oil. If you are truly at 0–6, that simple food is wonderful. Your mouth waters. You would eat it gladly.

Now imagine the fix you crave. White bread that melts to sugar. Crisps. Pastries. Sweets. Sweetened drinks. Biscuits. Would you want that same fix if the sugar and salt and fat were removed and it sat plain and raw on the plate? Would you chase it with the same urgency if it were not engineered to invite the next bite before this one ends?

Be honest. You know the answer.

Real hunger says yes to real food. Wanting says no to real food and yes to one fix.

Try it now, wherever you are.

Think of your last urgent reach. Hold that BAD SUGAR in your mind.

Now run the Raw Test.

If I offered you a simple, wholesome plate — fruit, vegetables, your favourite real meal — would you have wanted food at all? Or did you want only that one sweet hit?

You know. You wanted only the hit.

That is not hunger. That is the Treat Spell interpreting a mild, empty, slightly insecure, slightly restless feeling as "I need sugar."

And the reassurance: you do not need to pass a test to earn eating. The Raw Test is not a rule to discipline you. It is a mirror. It shows you what kind of signal you are reading.

If you are truly hungry, you will welcome real food. Eat it. Eat as much of your favourite foods as you want, whenever you want, without willpower or feeling deprived. That is not a diet promise. It is the operating position of Nature's Guide.

If you are not truly hungry, the Test shows you the want is choosy. You can smile at it. You can let it pass. It is not a command. It is a flicker from a starving parasite.

Do you feel the kindness of that distinction? No fight. Just clarity.

Two trap questions whose only honest answer concedes the point:

If that sudden urge were genuine hunger, why would it refuse plain, nourishing food and demand only BAD SUGAR?

If BAD SUGAR were genuine food, why would your body need another fix an hour later, more tired, crashy and foggy than before?

It's wanting, not hunger. It was never feeding you.

### PRIMARY FOODS AND SECONDARY FOODS

Now you can sort what confused you for years.

We have mixed two categories and called both "food." Nature's Guide never did.

Primary foods are foods as nature provides them, or close to it — eaten as food, with fibre, water, structure, satisfaction. Fruit whole, vegetables, beans, intact grains, fish, meat, eggs, nuts, plain dairy if you take it, water. They satisfy hunger. They register on the gauge. They answer the Raw Test with yes.

Secondary foods are BAD SUGAR — refined and added sugars plus ultra-processed carbs and starchy junk that behave like sugar in the body: white bread, crisps, fries, pastries, sweetened drinks, confectionery, desserts, biscuits, sugary cereals. They do not satisfy hunger. They hijack wanting. They fail the Raw Test the moment you strip the coating. They are engineered to invite company — fat with sugar, salt with starch — and to keep the dial jittering.

The fact is, your body manufactures the glucose it needs from ordinary primary food and from protein and glycerol already handled by your own metabolism. BAD SUGAR is not an essential import. You do not need to import it to be energised, to think clearly, to get through your workout, to get through your day. That is not a prescription for any narrow diet. It is the clean baseline returning. Eliminating BAD SUGAR is not deprivation. Your body is built to thrive without it.

Do you see why this is not a diet?

A diet tells you to count, restrict, time, fear. Nature's Guide tells you to eat when hungry, stop when satisfied, choose primary foods as food, and let BAD SUGAR leave entirely because it was never food.

You can already hear how different those two voices feel. One is tight. One is free.

We were told the opposite. We were told primary foods were boring and secondary foods were treats. We were brave for resisting. We were good when we cut down.

It's the other way around.

Primary foods are the feast. Your favourite real meals — the ones you love — are waiting the moment you are truly hungry. You will taste them as if for the first time when hunger is real and the Treat Spell is quiet.

BAD SUGAR was the interference. It dulled your taste for the real feast while promising it was the feast.

Ask yourself, you who grazed daily and thought you loved food:

When you were deepest in BAD SUGAR, did food taste more wonderful, or less? Did you savour meals, or rush them to get to the sweet? Did you feel light, clear and satisfied after eating, or tired, crashy and foggy and already thinking of the next fix?

You know. Less. Rushed. Foggy.

Take the fix away and what happens? The gauge calms. Hunger comes cleanly. Food tastes alive.

One killer pair to carry:

Primary foods feed you. Secondary foods feed the Sugar Trap.

Which do you want to feed from now on?
