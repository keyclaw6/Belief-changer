MOVE G15-M1: PRESENT — Carr tells the reader to stop trying to halt the food industry and put everyone else aside because curing your own addiction is the only job that matters; ours lands the same turn in the precious-exception frame: “Set all of that to one side for an hour. This sight is for you first.” and “Leave everyone else’s feelings to their own hour.”

MOVE G15-M2: PRESENT — Carr’s two-pronged attack is to see natural food as genuinely wonderful while simultaneously tasting brainwashed favourites as the bland junk they are; ours names both prongs and runs them together: “Now reverse the brainwashing from both sides at once, because one side alone leaves the old glow alive,” then the hungry plain meal that “holds you” for hours beside the favourite crumb that “tastes cloying and leaves a coat and a call.”

MOVE G15-M3: PRESENT — Carr separates occasion from substance by showing that company keeps the party happy while cake alone would not, and that craving relief was mistaken for joy; ours performs the same inversion throughout the cinema and birthday scenes: “The warmth was the scene. The company was the scene,” and “sweetness never lived in the dose.”
