If You Opened Here First

If you opened the book at this page, put a marker here and start at the first page.

These lines are reminders for a reader who has already seen the whole con. They are not the method by themselves. The seeing is the method, done in order, with an open head. Reading the last page first will give you sentences without the seeing that makes them true.

I say this plain because I know the pull to collect tips. Tips kept us dosing for years. Paper rules on a fridge never held at 10pm with the cupboard half-open and the hand moving before the mind agreed. Rules never held because belief held the hand.

“Just give me the list and I will manage,” you say.

Would a list have steadied that hand? You have lived the answer. Monday rules, Tuesday bargains, Friday allowances that crept to Thursday and Saturday. The hand obeyed belief, not paper. Change belief and the hand steadies by itself.

So read through. Notice. Let each room do its work. All you have to do is follow all the instructions. Arrive here with the belief already changed, and this page will read like your own notes. Photograph it then. Keep it where shopping lists live. Let it remind, not command.

If you live with diabetes or pre-diabetes, take medication that affects blood sugar or appetite, are pregnant, live with an eating disorder past or present, or have any medical risk where changing what you eat could carry risk, follow your clinician’s advice first and use this book for belief only.

What Stays Shut

You carry twelve sentences and one bright answer. Nothing else needs carrying.

We carried packets, rules, penance, bargain diaries, hidden wrappers. We counted days, weighed mornings, promised tomorrows. The load sat in the head from the first sweet top-up to the last evening box. You do not carry that load now. You carry a shut decision and a clear mouth.

Doubt will whisper cost at odd hours. Hear cost plain and test it against your own mouth.

Picture a mouth dosed morning and night for the rest of your life, flood then coat then call on repeat, wallet lighter, teeth duller, afternoons fogged and re-dosed. Is that kindness? Is that love? Is that fuel?

Picture a mouth that eats hungry and stops satisfied for the rest of your life, mornings plain, shops plain, meals that close by themselves. Which mouth wakes cleaner? Which head works steadier? Which belly stays quiet longer?

You know because you lived both. We all lived both. The dosed mornings buzzed ten minutes then fogged. The hungry mornings built slow and held. The dosed meals opened want wider. The hungry meals closed it.

When did a dose ever leave hunger quiet for more than an hour?

When did a hungry meal ever hire the next hunger within the hour?

Which one closed, and which one called?

Eat what you want, when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and never punish yourself again. That sentence is not a rule to obey with a clenched jaw. It is what hunger does when no dose sits in the middle of the day pulling strings.

So keep the decision shut. A passing thought is old script, not your reasoning. A wrapper memory at the till, a smell from a bakery vent, an hour that once hired the hand — let each fire and thin. Answer from the chest with your bright sentence and get on with buttering toast, weighing apples, paying at the till.

Hunger closes. Doses call.

Grown Back Into Yourself

We began as eaters who knew hunger without teaching.

Watch a small child eat a peach. Hungry, juice running, eyes bright. Mid-bite the belly says enough and the child runs off. No counting. No guilt. No second reach debating in the head. Body leads.

We lost that lead sip by sip, packet by packet, until we called a narrow sweet-specific twitch hunger and fed broad hunger with narrow packets. We wondered why the belly stayed restless and the head stayed dull. The answer sat in the mouth: flood thins to cloy, cloy coats, coat calls within the hour.

I tasted a plain apple after years of packets and heard the crack again. Juice, not cloy. Close, not call. I stopped mid-plate at supper because the belly said enough, and sat a minute surprised at how short a satisfied meal is when no dose stretches it. You will find the same at your table. Real food behaves. A dose never behaved.

Here is the turn saved for last. Sweetness was stolen from you while the con taught hollow as hunger, and now it is handed back. This was never about quitting. It was about growing back into the eater you were before the first packet taught you to doubt your belly.

Feel what that means at the table tonight. The bread smells stronger because the mouth is clean. Fruit weighs heavy in the hand because hunger is broad again. Company warms because faces warm, not foil. The scene gave everything. The packet billed for the ride.

“What if old hours feel hollow for a few days?” you say. “What if the thought crosses daily?”

Test it against your own days. Does the thought of a film you no longer love haunt you daily? It crosses, you smile, it passes. Why would this old script behave unlike every other dead belief? In the first days cues are thick, so it crosses often. In a few days at most it thins. In a few weeks you will notice an evening passed without crossing at all. Unfed thoughts thin. Fed thoughts breed. Starve the stir and it dies.
