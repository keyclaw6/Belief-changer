MOVE E46-M1: PRESENT — Carr closes by handing the reader a short carry-list and forbidding any reopening of the quit decision; ours does the same with the twelve photographed lines and repeated closure that the argument is finished and the stub already decided it.

Evidence: "Keep this photograph in the pocket and in the phone. No footnotes, no course to re-study. Twelve spoken lines. You lived them. You keep them." and "All you have to do is follow all the instructions. And you already did."

MOVE E46-M2: PRESENT — Carr tells the ex-smoker to treat future cigarette-thoughts with preset reframes, not fresh argument; ours gives explicit free-scripts for when the thought crosses and bars debate outright.

Evidence: "When the thought crosses, lightly, almost fondly, answer with the lines you already own. They are not slogans. They are shorthand for hours you lived." and "Do not turn the answer into a debate. Paper has no case left standing. The thought is not a summons. It is a postcard from a street you no longer live on. You smile, you answer, you turn back to the day in front of you."

MOVE E46-M3: PRESENT — Carr says help others through the same method and frame, not pressure or fear; ours forbids preaching and pity, lets lived freedom attract curiosity, and answers only when asked with the same took-not-gave argument.

Evidence: "Do not preach. Do not pity aloud. Let lightness in plain sight do what argument cannot." and "Tell them nothing until they ask. When they ask, tell them paper never gave, only took, and days hold themselves."
