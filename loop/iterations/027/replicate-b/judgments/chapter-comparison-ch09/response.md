MOVE G15-M1: PRESENT — Carr tells the reader to reverse brainwashing for themselves alone, putting industry and other addicts aside; ours lands the same turn in “FOR YOU FIRST”: “You walk out for you first” and “You are not suing. You are leaving.”

MOVE G15-M2: PARTIAL — Carr’s two-pronged attack pairs deliberate appreciation of nature’s foods with demystifying brainwashed favourites; ours demolishes the belief-and-body side of the trap in the cinema and garden (“Glory did not empty the bar. The bar emptied itself through you”; “full and wanting at once”) but never gives the reader the positive first prong—systematic exploration of real food as wonderful packages—only brief gestures (“Plain meals eaten hungry can be favourite. Fruit eaten ripe can be bright”) without the paired instructional turn.

MOVE G15-M3: PRESENT — Carr separates occasion from substance (“if any social occasion is a happy one it’s not because of the food, it’s because of the company”); ours performs the same recognition throughout the hardest cases: “The love was the people. The joy was the scene. The sweet was sneaking a ride” and “The scene gave everything. The dose gave nothing.”
