### IF YOU OPENED HERE FIRST

If you opened the book here, put this page aside for a moment and start at the first page and read straight through.

This page is saved reminders, not the method. The method is the seeing you do from the beginning, easily, with an open mind, while you live normally. What follows will make full sense only after that seeing is done. All you have to do is follow all the instructions.

You who have read through, stay with me. This is short. Then you live.

### NOTHING TAKEN, EVERYTHING RETURNED

We all entered this book believing the same grey fear. That life without bright packets would be duller. That joy needed a wrapper. That removing the blaze would remove brightness.

The other way round was true.

Bright packets never gave the brightness. They borrowed it. They coated a clean tongue, blurred clean hunger, fogged a clear head, then sold back a flash of relief from the fog they left and called the flash a treat. The film, the laughter, the company, the hunger met true, the fruit cracked open juicy after real appetite — those gave everything. The dose only sneaked a ride and charged for the journey.

Ask it plainly now and answer from your ribs.

If bright packets made food joyful, why did the most packet-fed meals leave you flat, foggy and never satisfied an hour later? If they gave energy, why did packet days always sag and ask for more? If a clear tongue and a settled belly is loss, why does loss feel so light and rich?

You know the answer. There was never anything to mourn.

You have not lost sweetness. Sweetness was stolen from you by years of blaze and has now been handed back. Taste bread crust. Taste an apple snap. Taste broth warmth, berry sharpness, orange juice on the fingers. Hunger seasons them bright because hunger is clean again. This was never about quitting a pleasure. This was about growing back into the eater you were before the wheel started turning — the one who eats hungry, enjoys deeply, and stops when satisfied.

I felt it on my own free morning with the same kettle and the same chair and only the rustle gone. Porridge faintly sweet with nothing added. Hunger gone after eating. No hollow following. No bargain humming. I laughed that I had feared this plainness. Plainness was the feast. The packet had been the thief.

You are that eater again now.

That is why there is no emptiness to fill and no new life to build. You change nothing else. Same street. Same table. Same people. Eat what you want, when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and never punish yourself again. Drink water when thirsty. Rest when tired. Meet true signals straight. Freedom asks only that seeing stays clear.

Think what the choice really was. Continuing meant staying prodded and hollowed for the rest of your life. Walking free means eating, laughing, working and resting bright for the rest of your life. Same days. Two opposite lives. You have chosen brightness.

If you live with diabetes, take medication that affects blood sugar or appetite, are pregnant, live with an eating disorder past or present, or have any medical condition where changing what you eat could carry risk, talk to your clinician and follow their advice first.

### GET ON WITH IT

So get on with enjoying your life.

Do not study freedom. Live it. Close the book. Cook supper hungry. Carry home plain food you love. Sit in the same chair and follow the film to the end. Meet mornings clear and let hunger lead and satisfaction close. Let money drip stop. Let hours circling return. Those are bonus beside the great gain: no more wheel, no more debate, no more hollow asking for more.

When a sweet thought crosses — wrapper by a bench, bakery air on a street, the old hour by the old chair — answer it at once with joy, loudly, gladly:

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!

Do not push the thought away. You cannot push a thought gone by ordering it gone. Answer it. A memory is not an order. A smell is not a command. The thought becomes a small festival. Pity those still caught, still paying to blur what you meet straight. Rejoice that you no longer feed the wheel. Then walk on light to the meal ahead.

Do not preach. Do not convert the house. Lecture breeds defence and defence reopens debate in your own head. Live free loud and talk soft. When kindness offers cake, answer kind and plain with no speech and no scorn. Let visible ease do the talking.

I congratulate you not for effort but for seeing. You did not climb. You stepped out. Mornings prove it. Shops prove it. Supper proves it. Each ordinary day writes it deeper till thought itself turns glad by nature.

Keep one picture and let the rest go. Photograph this list. Keep it on your phone, on the fridge, where tired eyes can find it. These are the agreements that freed you, in the order you made them. If doubt ever whispers cost, read them and remember you have absolutely nothing to lose and everything to gain and the change came easily, immediately and permanently because seeing did the work.

1. KEEP AN OPEN MIND
Question what you think you know about sweetness.
2. DON'T STOP OR CUT DOWN UNTIL YOU FINISH
Carry on exactly as normal while you read.
3. JUDGE ONLY BY WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU
Forget harm versus benefit and ask for the benefit.
4. NEVER BLAME YOURSELF AGAIN
Every failure was the method, never you.
5. BEGIN WITH ELATION, NOT DREAD
You are escaping, not losing.
6. TRUST HUNGER AND SATISFACTION
Eat when hungry, stop when satisfied, enjoy real food.
7. SEE THE GRUMBLE AS DYING, NOT DEMANDING
That feeling is the Nibbler starving.
8. IGNORE ANY ADVICE THAT CONFLICTS WITH THIS METHOD
Other voices rebuild the Con.
9. IGNORE ANYONE WHO QUIT BY WILLPOWER
Their struggle was the wrong method talking.
10. NEVER KEEP A SPECIAL SWEET
One exception keeps the whole Trap alive.
11. TAKE YOUR LAST ORDINARY TREAT AND VOW FREEDOM
Pay attention to the ugliness, then rejoice you are free.
12. NEVER REOPEN THE DECISION
A passing thought is a dead enemy, not an order.
