**The book's journey in five sentences:** The reader enters wary, half-expecting another diet lecture, and is given an easy contract, BAD SUGAR definition, and permission to keep eating while belief changes. Chapters 2–3 shift blame to the Sugar Trap and empty the benefit column with the TO/FOR verdict; chapters 4–5 kill the Willpower Method and invert the fuel/afternoon-lift claim. Chapters 6–8 install hunger and satisfaction as authority, split the Nibbler from the Sweet Con, and demolish manufactured want, prevalence, identity excuses, and fear-of-failure/success ropes. Chapters 9–10 meet the strongest celebration case head-on and foreclose every carve-out, substitute, and “tomorrow” negotiation. Chapters 11–13 confer immediate freedom through the last ordinary treat and vow, inhabit ordinary mornings/shops/evenings free, and close with the photographable instruction recap plus the terminal “BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!” hand-over.

**Overall arc assessment:** Read as one cumulative Carr-method book, this replicate delivers a complete inversion: by chapter 13 a believing reader should see BAD SUGAR as giving nothing, stopping as escape not sacrifice, and freedom as already conferred rather than a willpower project ahead. The demolition curve rises through the planned middle peak (mechanism, manufacture, celebration, totality) and hands cleanly to a freedom crescendo in the final quarter without retreating into maintenance, streaks, or ongoing recovery framing. Mantra debuts, frozen tokens, and all twelve instruction imperatives appear verbatim at their owning chapters and are recapped in chapter 13 with M-I as the closing thought. Cross-chapter weaknesses are NOTED only: chapter 1 spends chapter-11 vow ammunition early (“I took my last ordinary sweet… and made a solemn vow that I was free”), and two later scenes briefly re-argue settled jobs—the peach child authority in chapter 6 after its full staging in chapter 2, and the tight-shoes illusion in chapter 7 after its flagship exposition in chapter 5—without blocking the arc or breaking the mantra system. No blocking class fires; the book works as one journey to relief.
