## 4. Scene and analogy bank

Each debuts its full staging on exactly one card; later cites are one-phrase token echoes only.

SC-01 — supermarket tour and checkout. Aisles walked hunting every favourite brand, cart loaded with crunch, pop-tarts, bars, pretzels, cookies, ice cream; at checkout embarrassment dissolves because neighbouring carts look the same. Job: normalization and availability as trap. Constraint: no brand shaming of fellow shoppers; warmth to people, contempt for placement.

SC-02 — 2pm breakroom cake. Stale office cake eaten standing, tired, bored, slightly hungry, unable to refuse, while fantasizing about float, cookies, sundae. Job: axis-switch and fuel demolition ground. Constraint: office colleagues pitied, never mocked.

SC-03 — fridge light at 9pm. Late trip not for nourishment but numbing; kitchen trembling search, anger if nothing appetising. Job: comfort-use exposed. Constraint: no eating-disorder instruction; stays as observed ritual, routed to AD-A limits.

SC-04 — birthday-table mousse. Fancy restaurant, family friends, rich mousse ordered to avoid sitting out while everyone eats dessert, eaten all of it. Job: strongest case; pleasure reassigned to company and occasion. Debuts only in CH-10. Constraint: family warmth preserved; only the sugar portion indicted.

SC-05 — 3pm kettle without cookies. Timed afternoon break, stretch, kettle, knit or pause, tea taken plain while cookies stay rationed-then-gone. Job: ordinary-life proof. Constraint: tea is scene furniture, never prescribed as substitute.

SC-06 — pantry stash and wrapper forensics. Chocolate seeded behind peanut butter, soup cans, noodles; wrappers squirrelled for school bins to hide evidence. Job: secrecy cost. Constraint: described once at full length; later only as token.

SC-07 — bulk-bin tong-shovel. Paper bag filled by rote count, staged like contraband, cashier naming the contents. Job: loss-of-control comedy that turns to disgust. Constraint: comedy warm, never contempt for the buyer.

AN-01 — house-party gatecrasher. Lonely carbs invite company once inside; one loser texts all his friends and the house is overrun by couch-crashers who will not leave. Job: sugar-begets-sugar escalation. Constraint: original wording in prose; sourced line never quoted verbatim.

AN-02 — roller coaster only fun if you can get off. The ride metaphor for lift-crash; the person who cannot alight is just thrown. Job: lift-crash inversion. Constraint: keep to fuel chapters and fear chapter echoes.

AN-03 — filter of comparison. Tasting everything through chocolate-or-cheesecake just eaten; fruit tastes dull until the filter lifts, then the same fruit tastes sweeter. Job: taste recalibration and freedom preview. Constraint: sensory, not nutritional lecture.

AN-04 — cigarettes at the office party. Imagine the treats were cigarettes, cans of soda, sandwiches, bonuses — the normalization of sugar as office reward collapses. Job: manufacture-of-desire widening. Constraint: analogy, never equivalence claim; preserves E-11 honesty.

AN-05 — cliff-jump for moderation. Jumping off provided you fall only a few metres. Job: moderation absurdity. Constraint: single use at full staging in CH-08; later token only.

AN-06 — seatbelt margin. The belt is there for accidents, not for driving erratically. Job: margin-for-error doctrine. Constraint: appears only with redefinition and vow; never as licence.

AN-07 — abusive employee quit. Quitting sugar like quitting a job with an abusive employee; pity for those still inside. Job: pity-not-envy and villain anger. Constraint: employer image, never reader blame.
