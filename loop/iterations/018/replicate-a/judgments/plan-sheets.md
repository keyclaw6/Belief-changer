## 6. Arc and Length

**Chapter count and architecture:** 15 chapters. The architecture merges freely inside the canonical spine: not a chapter-per-function course.

- **First third (CH-01–CH-05):** Easy contract and instructions; reader is already hooked; the two creatures named in passing when the trap is first seen (CH-02); body, instinct, and real food as concrete encounters (watch eating; body as authority) — not a three-word refrain.
- **Middle (CH-06–CH-12):** Demolitions on that ground; CH-06's primary job is inhabit-the-ordinary-doing (hunger, satisfaction, real food as favourite) — not a justification kill with inhabit as flavor; long testimony in main flow (CH-11); prevalence claim once (CH-02); fear, anti-method, identity-excuse, and strongest-case rooms.
- **After the vow (CH-13–CH-15):** The ritual is the last ordinary instance (not a laboratory dose) (CH-13); one ordinary-life chapter using thoughts reader already owns, once (CH-14); short recap — not three teaching manuals (CH-15).

- *Freedom crescendo:* Established in promise (CH-01), deliberately suppressed through demolition middle (CH-03–CH-11 low), detonated in final quarter (CH-13–CH-15 highest; more freedom language in last 20% than rest combined).
