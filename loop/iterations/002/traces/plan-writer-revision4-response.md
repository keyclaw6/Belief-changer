## 1. BOOK CORE

**Target behavior:** Compulsive consumption of refined/added sugar and junk carbs — the intermittent craving–binge–crash loop driven by BAD SUGAR. Not nutrition pedantry, calorie counting, or medical nutrition therapy.

**One reader, 4 functional personas:**

*   **P-01 — The Loss-of-Control Binger:** Night/weekend binges, secret eating, one-bite-becomes-the-box, shame cycle, failed restriction diets.
*   **P-02 — The In-Denial Moderate:** "Just a treat," daily grazing that never reads as a problem, moderation-negotiation and the carve-out ("I don't keep it in the house").
*   **P-03 — The Comfort/Identity Eater:** Sugar as reward, love, coping, nostalgia; "I deserve it" self-payment.
*   **P-04 — The Energy-Crash Yo-Yoer:** Sugar as fuel and concentration fix; 3pm slump, lift–crash productivity dip.

**Load-bearing false belief (one sentence):** BAD SUGAR is a genuine pleasure, treat and energy lift that makes life sweeter and without which I would be deprived.

**Through-line:** Escape, not sacrifice — every perceived benefit of BAD SUGAR is reassigned to its true source until the reader sees BAD SUGAR does nothing FOR you while doing plenty TO you, so stopping is reclamation of freedom already available.

**Format:** Full-length Carr — ~62,800 words, 18 chapters + Front Matter. Full chapter anatomy per B10: IN THIS CHAPTER preview, italic thesis, body (one belief-move via titled sections, ALL-CAPS verdicts), chapter instruction at climax, SUMMARY bullets. Licensed recap zones only: IN THIS CHAPTER, SUMMARY, and assigned instruction.

**The five fork decisions (defaults = Carr, per Fidelity Doctrine 2026-07-12):**

1.  **Inner monster (Fork 1): Carr personification.** Full personification with original behavior-fitted names frozen in mantra sheet. Little Monster = **the Nibbler** (trivial, dying physical imp). Big Monster = **the Sugar Story** (lifelong brainwashing/belief system that feeds the Nibbler). Craving is external, small, winnable; death throes are to be revelled in.
2.  **Outcome (Fork 2): Total cessation inside a redefinition line.** Moderation foreclosed. The book commands total abstinence from BAD SUGAR via pincer + cliff-jump. Autonomy is used as method honesty, not as permission to moderate.
3.  **Science weight (Fork 3): Carr scare-then-disown.** Hard facts delivered at full, flat, frightening Carr force, then explicitly disowned as motivator. No hedging, no literature-review texture. Raw citations quarantined to back-matter appendix.
4.  **Villain (Fork 4): Two villains, both hit hard.** (a) The engineered trap — sugar industry / product engineering / bliss-point / infinite availability. (b) The named anti-method — the Willpower Method and its equivalents — blamed for every past failure. Warm to person, vicious to trap.
5.  **Void (Fork 5): Carr "change nothing else" — natural baseline returns.** Default for consumptive behavior. No elaborate replacement system. Body returns to natural baseline on its own; reader inhabits old contexts confidently to prove freedom. Positive authority (Nature's Guide + instruments) provides the operating manual for GOOD foods, not a time-filling replacement.

**Redefinition and margin-for-error doctrine (B10):**

*   **CAPS name:** **BAD SUGAR**
*   **Definition (boxed decree in Ch.1, carried thereafter):** When I say sugar, take it to mean BAD SUGAR = refined sugar + all added free sugars + processed junk carbs / starchy carbs engineered to spike and crave (sweets, biscuits, cakes, confectionery, sugary drinks, ultra-processed snacks, white bread/pastries where sugar is hidden load). 387 uses thereafter — naming IS the repetition.
*   **GOOD SUGAR:** Intrinsic sugars in whole fruit, vegetables and whole foods eaten as nature packaged them — part of Nature's Guide menu, not the trap. No abstinence required.
*   **Total-abstinence logic inside the line:** There is no healthy level of BAD SUGAR other than zero; if there is no benefit to doing it often, there is no point doing it occasionally either.
*   **Margin for error:** Your body can cope with an occasional accidental blip, but your mind cannot. A slip revives nothing physical unless the Sugar Story is allowed back. Guard the belief, not the behaviour. Seatbelt image: there for accidents, not for driving erratically. An accidental trace does not require willpower or penance — only immediate re-assertion of joy that you are free.
*   **Conditional-bonus extensions:** Alcohol and dairy are not required to quit — "you're not reading this book to quit alcohol" — but the upgrade is framed as an exciting option with pointer to help. Autonomy preserved.

**Clinical / eating-disorder safety perimeter:**

Non-goals: medical nutrition therapy, diabetes management, eating-disorder treatment. Boxed advisory in Front Matter and Ch.12 routed outside belief argument: If you have diabetes, are on glucose-affecting medication, have a history of eating disorder, are pregnant, or have been advised to follow a prescribed diet, consult your clinician about food changes and do not adjust medication on the basis of this book. Crisis-pointer for eating disorders included. No diagnosis, no prescription, no "sugar withdrawal" diagnosis claimed.

**Strongest pro-behavior scene:** The warm, sunlit birthday / celebration table — family laughing, cake with candles, the shared dessert that feels like love itself — and its twin: the 3pm office biscuit tin / kitchen reward moment where sugar feels like the only kindness in a hard day. Met head-on in Ch.11 and stripped by credit reassignment.

**Destination state:** Reader who, whenever they think about BAD SUGAR, feels relief and freedom that they no longer do it — sees a sweet offer and thinks FANTASTIC! I'M FREE!, pities rather than envies users, inhabits every old context unchanged, and trusts Nature's Guide.

**Fresh ending reframe (saved for CLOSE):** You haven't given up sweetness — you've grown out of needing to be bribed to feel okay. The whole effort was not about quitting but about burgeoning — growth into the person who no longer needs to pay to feel normal. Deployed only in Ch.18.
