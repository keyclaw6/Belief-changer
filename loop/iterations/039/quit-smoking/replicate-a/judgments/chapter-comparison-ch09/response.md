MOVE E15-M1: PARTIAL — Carr opens on health, money, and stigma as the motives smokers already quote when they try to stop, then turns to the overlooked cost: unremitting daily slavery that does not even occur to us; ours enacts chained hourly bondage and names money among the rents, but never sets health or money up as the familiar cited motives, so the hidden-cost turn does not land. Evidence: `"Chained. The box counted last thing and first thing. The pocket patted without thought. The shop run in rain for morning. The evening cut short to buy for morning. The party stepped out of. The film missed in the middle. The train seat chosen for the next platform's doorway."` and `"Money burned tube by tube."` (No line pairs those known quit motives with bondage as the cost smokers overlook.)

MOVE E15-M2: PRESENT — Carr’s prison is life bent to the next cigarette—where you go, what you do, when, and with whom; ours performs the same arrangement through the day’s dosing kit, hourly timetable, and twenty cardboard decisions that cut parties, films, and train seats to the next light. Evidence: `"Count your day through it. One for waking. One for the bus. Two for the morning. One for coffee. One for after food. One for the call that ran long. One for the wait. One for the drink. One for the last thing at night. The number was put in your pocket before you lived the day, and you were taught to call the counting yours."` and `"It never soothed the day. It scheduled the day."`

MOVE E15-M3: PRESENT — Carr pities the smoker still living inside the arrangement and never scorns them, even when calling his own scenes pathetic; ours keeps warmth on the person still reaching and aims anger at the packer, the costume, and the trap hands, never at the arranger. Evidence: `"Look at the box again and let the anger go where it belongs. Not at your hand for reaching. Not at your chest for wanting."` and `"You are not weak for carrying it. You were fitted for it, the way a post is fitted for bills — not because the post wants bills, but because bills need posts."`
