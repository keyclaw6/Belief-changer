MOVE E40-M1: PARTIAL — Carr names two pre-smoke essentials (certainty of success; doom/gloom vs excitement) before the conscious last smoke; ours gates on seeing and readiness with strong open-eyes smoking, but never runs Carr’s paired two-point check before lighting.

Evidence: "Do you feel ready? Are you champing at the bit to stub the last one and walk out? Do you see clearly that there is nothing out there to miss and everything in here to leave?" and "Light it as you always did. Then do something you have never done in all your years of smoking. Pay full attention to it."

MOVE E40-M2: PRESENT — Carr strips the romantic farewell and forces a filthy, conscious look at what is being left; ours demolishes the nostalgic last-smoke picture and keeps the reader on stain, ash, sour mash, and the mashed end, not friendship or ceremony.

Evidence: "Your last cigarette is not a farewell to a friend. It is a look, with clear eyes, at what you are leaving." and "Look at the yellow stain spreading on the filter where your lips sit." and "Look at the mashed end. Smell it. That sour mash is what you used to put in your mouth and call heaven at lighting."

MOVE E40-M3: PRESENT — Carr confers non-smoker identity at the decision/stub, not after a waiting period or craving countdown; ours repeats that freedom is immediate at belief-death and stub, while body noise fades on its own clock.

Evidence: "You are a non-smoker the second you stub it, because the want died before the tug did." and "You do not become a non-smoker in a week when the tug fades. You are one when the belief dies — which is now, if you mean the vow." and "From this second you do not wait to become a non-smoker. You are one."
