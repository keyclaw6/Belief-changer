### Reader journey comparison

**The real chapter's movement:** Carr opens on the social myth that quitting is inherently hard, then names the Willpower Method as any approach that keeps sacrifice alive. He walks the reader through serial-quitter dread, deprivation loops, conflict-of-wills relapse, and years-long “dry drunk” misery among willpower quitters. Statistics on NRT and unaided failure rates land as evidence that willpower-based tries mostly fail — not as a personal sentence. The end-state: struggle was manufactured by the method; misery is mental, not nicotine withdrawal; trial odds do not define whether you can be happily free once desire is removed.

**Our chapter's movement:** The reader enters on the accusing drawer, calendar, and twenty-cross history concluding “I lack willpower” and “I need a prop.” Failed attempts are reframed as ordinary strong-willed smokers running a wrong method. Morning dread is reread as memory of chaining, not prophecy. The Willpower Method is defined as belief intact plus forbidden dose — want, mourn, crave, but do not touch — so strain proves loss still believed, not weakness. Wilful-not-weak inverts the self-blame. Props are the swapped glass keeping belief alive. Trial-room vs street statistics are split into two questions; neither binds the reader now. The mate who white-knuckled is heard warmly but not borrowed. The chapter closes with checking over obeying, drawer shut, calendar as record not forecast. A cooperative reader leaves blaming the method, not the self, and does not treat trial odds as deciding escape.

## Final verdict

**Assigned transition verdict:** The chapter completes the enacted transition on the card. Entering belief is staged plainly — “I lack willpower. I need a prop to quit. Next time I must try harder” — and inverted by the close: “You did not fail twenty times because you are weak — you failed because the method you were handed was built to make you fail”; “The method failed. Not you”; “The struggle is the method manufacturing struggle”; “Neither set of numbers decides what clear eyes can do now.” Past failures become proof the Willpower Method was wrong, not that the reader is weak. Continuity from Chapter 5 holds: pleasures were cleared there; here the reader advances to method-blame and props-as-belief-keepers without being asked to re-enter reward-or-taste belief.
