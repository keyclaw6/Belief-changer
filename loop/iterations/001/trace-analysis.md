(d) Scaffold leak log quantifies residual hits: C05–C09, C17 still carry `P-0x` and/or `Killer-line`; C08 also carries one `CONTESTED`.

**Root component:** writer-prompt

**Evidence:** The applied firewall in `prompts/chapter-writer.md` lines 48–56 forbids surfacing persona codes, craft labels, and grades — but the same file's **Binding chapter craft** and **Full-length chapter anatomy** repeatedly uses the leakable vocabulary as execution instructions: line 71 *"gets its full argue-to-compress beat"*, line 93 *"one killer-line pair per major argument"*, lines 40/66/84/95/117 *"your card assigns"*, line 105 *"the card-assigned reframe"*. Hold it."*), `chapter-09.md` lines 41–43 (P-01/P-02), `chapter-11.md` line 257 (SUMMARY metadata). The ban and the vocabulary coexist in one contract; the model obeys both literally.

**Mechanism:** Iter-001 replaced the preserve-every-grade clause but left the writer-prompt full of craft/plan-facing diction the model treats as speakable. Where inputs forbid and permit the same register class, leakage persists — especially at peaks where cards demand device execution. SUMMARY anatomy (writer-prompt line 109) mandates mantra restatement; the model adds a factory compliance header on top. **000 Cluster 2: partially closed** — class reduced ~90% by leak log but not eliminated; root level unchanged.
