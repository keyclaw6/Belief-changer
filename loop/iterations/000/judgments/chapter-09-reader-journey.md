### Journey Gap 1: The lab-evidence blocks deflate the confrontation peak

**Where it happens:** "WHY CUTTING DOWN MAKES IT MORE PRECIOUS" — immediately after "You have unplugged ninety-nine cords and left the last one glowing."
**What the reader should feel:** The forbidden-fruit realization should land and keep intensifying toward the reversal, then hand off to the cliff. Peak tension should hold through the turn.
**What they actually feel:** At the emotional apex, the reader is pulled out of themselves into a framed evidence spec ("And here is the laboratory honesty you deserve, kept exactly within its limits. E-08 is SUPPORTED in animals... Permitted inference...[Prohibited inference]...Safety..."). The confrontation stalls; the "you" disappears into dispassionate review. Recognition is suspended, not consolidated.
**Why the divergence:** The evidence format — a catalogue of support/permit/limit/safety lines — is written for the honesty gate and lands mid-beat as clinical exposition, exactly where the reader is most emotionally hooked. It pauses the enacted transition rather than propelling it.
**Initial suspicion (speculative):** plan-card — [the evidence placement (right at the confrontation peak) is specified by the plan rather than deferred to a calmer consolidation valley; a formatted evidence block weakens the reader's continuous "you" address.]
