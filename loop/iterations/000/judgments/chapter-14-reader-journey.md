**Why the divergence:** Each item conservatively re-negotiates its epistemic status rather than banking the already-landed verdict, so the recap spends its force revisiting uncertainty instead of compounding certainty.

**Initial suspicion (speculative):** writer-prompt — the evidence-restatement prompt instructs hedging each claim, which compresses a long book's confidence-building into a short review section.
