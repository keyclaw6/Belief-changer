### Reader journey comparison

**The real chapter's movement:** The reader opens the chapter already free — "Congratulations! You've done it!" — and the substance is reassurance that their new state is solid. The death throes are framed as the Little Monster dying, something to stand by and *rejoice* in, not fight ("DO NOTHING!"). This dissolves first-hours vigilance. Seeking comfort turns potential down days into "moments of real pleasure" rather than alarm, and warns against doubt. Life Starts Here is the peak: the reader is told every true pleasure (reading, socials, walking, sex) they lost to addiction is coming back and old contexts become enjoyable again. The chapter then hands them the means — eating healthily, fruit breakfasts, primary/secondary foods, exercise for pleasure — and ends in the plainest, most confident release: "YOU'RE IN CONTROL AND YOU'RE FREE!" The arc moves from vigilance to celebration to ordinary, tooled-up freedom. It is entirely forward-facing and fear-free by the last page.

**Our chapter's movement:** The reader is met at the entering belief and confronted directly — "Will life be flat without it? Will parties be hard? Will I have to warn everyone?" — so the flatness anxiety is named, seen, then inverted ("We told ourselves life would be flat without BAD SUGAR. The fact is, life was flat *with* it."). The party/aisle/stress rehearsals are the strongest material: concrete re-embodiment that "you change nothing except that you no longer do BAD SUGAR," landing three killer pairs. Taste-return folds into the assigned FT-06 echo by front-loading the Nature's Guide instruments. The pet-rock identity section produces real recognition ("my treat… a pet rock in a pocket"). Don't-Preach-Shine enacts I-09's pity-don't-envy in the wild and ends with the warm command "Don't preach. Shine." The revelation and summary restate M-08 verbatim and land the final inversion ("It was life that made BAD SUGAR seem sweet while it dulled it"), leaving the reader in quiet pride. Movement is genuine and continuous across the whole chapter.

The two diverge in register at one repeated vein: our chapter, at each peak of embodiment, breaks the felt scene to deliver evidence-graded hedging (E-13 mood cohort, E-18 glucose) in the most procedural register in the book. The real chapter handles the corresponding beats (down days, "you will become fitter… avoid stress… feel more energetic") as pure confident reassurance with no data machinery. Function still matches — both soothe the flat-future fear and hand the reader tools for ordinary life — but ours periodically swaps *living* the scene for *auditing* an inference.

### Journey Gap 1: The stress rehearsal stalls into evidence-audit

**Where it happens:** "THIRD, THE NEXT STRESS," paragraphs beginning "Here we must be evidence-honest, exactly as the ledger demands…" through the E-18 glucose passage.

**What the reader should feel:** Calm absorption in the imagined stressful evening — the "old rescue offer slides in" and the reader labels it, sees the fix as backward, and lands relief without effort.

**What they actually feel:** They are pulled out of the scene and set down at a permitted/prohibited-inference checklist ("Empirical limit: observational, not proven causality; men only for incident disorder…"). It reads as compliance with an evidentiary process, not as the reader being held.

**Why the divergence:** At the assigned climax of the rehearsal the prose replaces enactment with disclaimered data ("Supporting the evidence with caveats so the claim can be deprecated"). What the real chapter covers with one confident sentence — the "cure" is a backfired rescue — ours covers with a long, hedged, register-breaking audit mid-scene. The E-18 follow-on ("Prohibited inference: that this prescribes a ketogenic diet… Safety limit: do not prescribe a diet") extends the stall even as it tries to justify the physiology beat. The scene resumes afterward, but the reader's held state was dropped.

**Initial suspicion (speculative):** evidence-handling / writer-prompt — inserted graded-science caveats at the immersive peak instead of at chapter seams, weakening the embodied beat.

**Where it happens:** "YOUR TASTE COMES BACK — LIVING BY NATURE'S GUIDE," the instrument-by-instrument exposition (raw-test, 0–20 gauge, primary/secondary foods).

**Why the divergence:** The "exactly as frozen, verbatim" framing signals the arc expected an echo/debut, but the delivered weight is a full curriculum. It consolidates (tools are good for the leaving-state) yet momentarily returns the reader to cognitive mode when the chapter's job is to make life feel *pre-inhabited*.

**Initial suspicion (speculative):** plan-card — the echo obligation was met, but the surrounding instrumentation scope exceeded what the chapter's assigned "project ordinary life" load expected at this position.
