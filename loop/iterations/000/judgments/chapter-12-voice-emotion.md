Permitted inference: admit honest disagreement; collapse both ropes — even if not pharmacologically addictive, the trap holds via belief plus intermittency. **Initial suspicion (speculative):** writer-prompt — the contested-science artifact's hedging language was carried into prose rather than bent to Carr's rhetorical certainty.
