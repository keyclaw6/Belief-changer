**The book's journey in five sentences:** The reader starts believing they freely choose BAD SUGAR as a genuine pleasure they would be deprived without, and within two chapters that choice is dissolved — they were conned by a childhood confidence trick, not foolish, and the Axis switches from "does harm outweigh benefit?" to "what benefit at all?" so the FOR column of the ledger empties as fuel, reward, taste and cope/connect are each demolished and reassigned as "sneaking a ride." In the middle the mechanism is installed: a trivial, already-dying Sugar Gremlin and a loud Sweet Brainwashing hold both ropes of the tug-of-war of fear, the escape routes of moderation are foreclosed, the engineered industry is indicted, and the "addict" identity is stripped to "conned, not broken." Near the end the reader is gated for readiness, takes the ceremonial final dose and the solemn vow that detonates the terminal mantra FANTASTIC! I'M FREE FROM SUGAR!, and the first days are relapse-proofed as rejoicing at a dead enemy. The reader lands free, having lost nothing — "you didn't give up sweetness, you recovered your taste for it" — pushed outward into ordinary, richer life with a portable twelve-instruction checklist.

### Arc Gap 1: the front re-argues its own verdict — C01 and C02 both sell the same confidence trick from scratch

**Where it happens:** chapters 1–2 (the 1→2 handoff).

**What the book should do here:** Per the plan's arc (C01 trust & participation, C02 dissolve choice) and the reference skeleton (C01→Invitation, C02→Brainwashing), chapter 2 should *enter from* the state C01 landed and advance it — invoking the already-settled "conned, not foolish" as a token and escalating to the new trap-naming and the boiling frog — not re-argue the opening belief-move.

**What it does instead:** C02 replays C01's confidence-trick scene, its first-sweet-as-love content, and its conned-not-foolish verdict almost from scratch, and it re-delivers the same self-report prevalence figure as new evidence. C01: "As a child you learned three lessons... One — sweet means love... That is the confidence trick." and "On validated self-report scales, about 14% of adults and about 12% of children meet addiction-like criteria..." C02 repeats near-verbatim: "Let me show you the confidence trick exactly as it works" with the same first-sweet sold as love, and "pooled findings show about 14% of adults and about 12% of children meet addiction-like criteria..." The reader is told the same con and the same statistic twice, so the opening stalls instead of climbing.

**Initial suspicion (speculative):** plan — S-04 was scheduled into both C01 and C02 cards, which re-places the same scene and evidence in both chapters and slows the front of the cumulative journey.

### Arc Gap 2: the cinema celebration does the same job twice and reuses the same lived lines

**Where it happens:** chapters 5 and 11 (the C05 fragment → C11 "strongest scene" seam).

**What the book should do here:** Per the curve map, C11 is the strongest case met head-on in full; C05 should only fragmentarily plant the scene so C11 can defeat it *once*, at its peak. The reader should feel the memory dissolve a single time, with the full force spent in the back half.

**What it does instead:** C05 already plays the warm-cinema scene and fully executes the reassignment ("THE CINEMA TEST — WHO MADE THE NIGHT SWEET?" ... "The pleasure was the sun, the leisure, the company, the occasion"), and it already deploys the E-03 lived line "celebrating, rewarding myself, going to the cinema, etc with chocolate." C11 then replays the identical dark-cinema/friends-laughing/chocolate scene and the identical birthday-sun reassignment and re-fetches both E-03 and the C04-flagship lived line E-01 ("I would have something quick and sweet but now know that will only give a short term lift...") with full permission-restatement. The cumulative reader absorbs the same seductive scene and the same lived lines twice, which blunts what should be the book's single, decisive memory-defeat at its assigned peak.

**Initial suspicion (speculative):** plan — S-09 and E-lines are scheduled into both C05 and C11, so the full-length expansion duplicates not just the scene but its flagship evidence instead of escalating C11 past the C05 fragment.

**Overall arc assessment:** Read as one book, this does take a believing reader to freedom the way a Carr book does: escalation is real and on-curve (demolition climbs through C04–C07, the mechanism lands at its C08 hinge, the indictment peaks at C10, and freedom detonates in the final quarter exactly as the curve assigns); the mantra system is fully and correctly executed — every debut happens at its scheduled chapter with argue-then-compress beats, echoes are brief and un-re-argued, and the terminal hand-over of FANTASTIC! I'M FREE FROM SUGAR! and the twelve-instruction recap land in C16–C18; and the ending is a genuine, un-moralizing, un-maintenance crescendo of relief and excitement ("GET ON WITH ENJOYING YOUR LIFE... You are free," nothing given up, taste "recovered"). The single biggest cross-chapter failure is the book's habit of re-presenting its two strongest motifs — the childhood confusion-they-were-sold-as-love and the cinema celebration — twice each (chapters 1–2 and 5/11), so the argument feels front-loaded and a noted verdict and a decisive scene are each re-argued from scratch instead of invoked and escalated; this slows the opening and softens the pinnacle, but it does not break the cumulative journey or the landing, both of which succeed.
