MOVE E46-M1: PRESENT — Carr closes with a numbered final-instruction list and forbids reopening the quit; ours carries the same close by photographing twelve spoken instructions and repeatedly refusing to retest the decision. Evidence: "Keep this picture. These are the lines that brought you here:" through item 12, and "If the old voice pipes up, 'Just one to prove I am over it,' what would that one prove? … You have watched the trick from inside and outside. There is nothing to test."

MOVE E46-M2: PRESENT — Carr’s late chapter hands future pangs a fixed script instead of an argument; ours does the same whenever a cigarette-thought drifts in, naming it and staying in the day. Evidence: "Let it come. You do not debate it. You label it and stay with the day you are in." and "I do not argue with it. I stay with the conversation, the task, the street I am on. The thought passes, and the street stays mine."

MOVE E46-M3: PRESENT — Carr tells readers to help interested friends with the book’s argument, not pressure; ours tells them to answer warmly in plain sentences, let living speak, and refuse preaching or scare. Evidence: "Tell them short and warm, and leave the door open without pushing them through it." and "Do not preach on doorsteps. Preaching breeds defence in friends and drags a fresh view back into debate."
