Chapter 8
THE WRONG METHOD

IN THIS CHAPTER: the drawer of old rules, the app counts and the white-knuckle week, the on-off schedule, the two fears, the excuse shelf, the easy objection, three orders.

*Your past failures never proved you are weak — they prove the strain method feeds the loop it claims to break.*

## THE DRAWER OF OLD RULES

Open that drawer with me though shame begs you to keep it shut, for you know this drawer though you never showed it at noon.

Not the desk drawer that rings. The other one. The kitchen drawer under the bills where old rules sleep. The torn diet sheet with coffee rings. The app still on your phone with red weeks marked fail. The notebook with neat columns, good days ticked, bad days crossed hard though the pen tore. The curfew card on the fridge, no sweets after six, signed on a Sunday night in full resolve. You know this drawer though you laugh it off at noon.

We all kept it. I kept it for years.

Monday plain. Tuesday white knuckles round the trolley. Thursday one bite to take the edge off. Friday the bite calls the box. Sunday sick, new rule writ for Monday. You know this week though the old talk calls it weak.

Mark what that week proves though the old voice reads it back to front.

You did not fail for want of will. You failed with will. White-knuckle week one proves it though shame hides it. You held three days plain while bells rang and mouths watered and hours dragged. You said no at the breakroom table while others ate though the cake stood warm. You walked past the aisle though the feet pulled. You drank water while want shouted though the throat stayed dry. If you were weak, that week would never have stood. Weak never holds three days against that pull. You held. Then the pull won not because you bent but because the method bent you till you broke.

Ask straight, as a man asks about his own house though the old voice hates plain asks. If strain cured sweet want, why did the most strained weeks end in the largest raids. If rules freed you, why did each new rule end with fuller bins and deeper hides. If counting bites taught control, why did the counted weeks end past joy at midnight with wrappers hid for morning though plain weeks with no count never drove you there.

You know the answer though it shames the old talk. The rule made the raid. The count made the binge. The curfew made the midnight kitchen.

Think of your own last strict run though you change nothing yet and you buy nothing new. First plain day head clear but edgy. Second plain day bells loud, drawer ringing, shop glow bright though you walk fast past. Third plain day mouth dry, mood thin, hands empty though the belly holds food. Fourth day one bite meant as pay for three good days. That bite floods. Want spikes. Hand marches to empty though joy dies at bite three. Night ends past ache. Morning ends in shame and a new vow to hold harder though harder broke you last time.

That round never proved you lack grip. It proved grip on the wrong wheel drives you off the road though you hold hard.

I know that wheel because I held it till my hands bled. No sweets in the house though I bought for guests and ate theirs after they left. No sweets before noon though I watched the clock till twelve with a dry mouth. Small bar weighed on kitchen scales though I ate three smalls and called it one. App streaks. Star charts. Cold showers. Long walks to outrun the three o'clock bell though the bell walked with me. Each fail I called proof I was built wrong. Each fail I swore I would try harder though harder fed the next fail.

We did that. We all did that and called it sense though sense never lived there.

See the drawer for what it holds though I blame you not one grain, for you know blame never freed a man.

It holds proof you can hold pain and keep walking. It holds proof you want out enough to pay. It holds proof you kept your word everywhere else in life though sweet broke it here. Work kept though tired. Bills paid though short. Kids fed though worn. Only here did will snap. If will snapped only here, will never was the fault. Place was. Method was. Tool was.

The drawer holds your strength. Not your shame.

The rule broke you. You never broke.

## THE ON-OFF SCHEDULE

Now see why the rule must break all who hold it though they hold hard, for you know this round in your own blood though shop talk never marked it.

Plain food never runs on clocks and bans. Hunger comes, bread answers, belly shuts, head frees for hours. No on days. No off days. No good weeks. No bad weeks. Eat shuts. Shut frees. That round holds though life shakes.

BAD SUGAR under rules runs the reverse though will swears it helps.

On days starve the want though want lives. Off days flood the want though want swears this time will hold. Starve, flood, starve, flood. That round never steadies. It winds. Each starve sharpens the next flood though the mouth hopes to still it. Each flood deepens the next starve though the head swears to hold. On, off, on, off, till the wire sings at the sight of a wrapper though the belly holds food.

You know this wind from your own months though the old talk calls it diet.

January no sweets. March tired, one slice though the slice calls the tin. April tin emptied past joy, new ban writ harder. June flood past ache. That year never proved food owns you. It proved on-off owns you.

Hear the plain fact though I dress it in no white coat and no hard words past your own eyes, for you know enough to walk though coats still argue the word for it.

The schedule makes the loop. Steady never makes it though shop talk swears sweet alone must hook all who taste. Men fed steady never wind like banned men wind though both taste sweet. Men banned then loosed binge and pace and want again though plain mouths eat and shut. Pull the ban-flood round and the pull thins. Keep the round and the pull grows though the sweet stays same. Same bar. Same mouth. Round decides. That is why your strictest years ate most though you bought least. Strict grew want though strict meant to kill it.

You say to me, but I ate less on good weeks, the rule must help some. I hear the ache under that line, for good weeks feel true while they hold. Only ask, what paid for those good weeks. Did good weeks free the head or did good weeks fill the head with drawer thoughts and clock thoughts and count thoughts till work thinned and sleep thinned and mood thinned. Did good weeks end in shut or did good weeks end in flood though the mouth hoped this time would hold. You know the bill though shame hides it. Less by day, more by night. Less by week, more by month. Less on paper, more in bins hid for morning. That math never frees though it flatters the chart.

Think of drink though we keep the picture to one breath, for you know thirst though sweet never slaked it.

Water drunk when thirst calls never winds. No man bans water till noon to prove strength. No man counts sips to earn thirst though thirst runs plain. Sweet under ban runs the reverse. Ban till six makes six ring. Count till Friday makes Friday flood. Hide till guests leave makes midnight raid though the belly holds food. Ban builds the bell it claims to still. Count feeds the loop it claims to cut.

So mark the schedule for what it stands though the old talk calls it cure. Ban starves. Starve sharpens. Flood pays. Pay brings shame. Shame brings harder ban. Harder ban brings louder flood. Round and round though no joy rides it and no will breaks it because will never drove it. Schedule drove it.

That is why scale men find rough first days track with most failed tries though weak never tracked it, for you know rough days though no talk told you straight. The days bite hardest where bans bit hardest before. Not where mouths love most. Where rules cut deepest. Failed tries cluster round hard starts the way bruises cluster round hard knocks. Knock less, bruise less. That truth frees you though fear paints it hard. You never failed from soft core. You failed from hard method on soft flesh.

The round winds. The man holds.

You hand me your doubt though you speak it low. If rules make it worse, am I to run with no rules at all. I tell you flat you run with sight, not with ban. Sight sees the bell as bell and lets it die unfed. Ban hears the bell as proof and feeds it by fight. Eyes beat bells though hands never could.

So hold this room while we name its worker, for this room must hold though later rooms shake. Restraint by day brewed binge by night. Binge by night brewed shame by dawn. Shame brewed harder restraint though harder restraint brewed harder binge. That wheel never needed your weakness to turn. It turned on its own build once wound. You wound it by orders though orders swore to free you. Sight unwinds it though sight lifts no weight.

The schedule built the loop. Not your blood.

## STRONG WILL, WRONG FOE

Now turn the blame where it belongs though shame begs to keep it, for you know this turn though no talk ever gave it.

You are not weak-willed. You are strong-willed on the wrong foe though the old talk calls it weak.

Think what weak would do though shame paints you weak. Weak never holds three plain days while bells ring. Weak never walks past the aisle with feet pulling. Weak never counts, weighs, logs, bans, though nights broke them. That is not weak. That is strong will spent where strength cannot win.

It takes a strong will to persist for years in something that goes against all your own sense though the old voice calls that shame. Mark that line, for it frees more than comfort.

You felt sick after floods though you planned the next. You woke thick though you swore plain nights. Only strong will pays against its own eyes for years. That pay proves grip though grip held the wrong rope.

See the foe swap though shop talk merges it to keep its wage, for you know true foes when I name them though sweet hid them for years.

The foe never was your gut. The foe never was your past. The foe never was your blood. The foe stands in two shapes though both wear plain dress. First stands the sugar trap, sweet built fast to shout and pull and wind though mouths hoped for feed. Second stands the Willpower Method, the strain creed that told you to hold your breath through bells though bells need sight, not breath.

You fought the first with the second though both belong to one house. Trap laid the dip. Method told you to white-knuckle the dip though white knuckles feed the dip. Trap rang the drawer. Method told you to count the rings though counts tune the ear to rings. Trap flooded. Method told you to ban floods though bans sharpen floods. Each round you fought harder though harder wound tighter. Each fail you blamed your hands though hands held true. Hands held. Method broke.

Ask with me. When did strain ever thin a bell. Never. When did bans ever shut a drawer. Never. When did counts ever close a meal. Never. If the Willpower Method freed, the most strained would walk freest though you know they crawl. The hardest holders binge hardest.

I learned the swap through work. I prided myself on grip. Deadlines met. I could hold any pain but sweet. Then I saw. Grip won where sight led. Grip lost where blindness led. That day I stopped calling my hand weak and started calling the orders wrong.

So hear the verdict though fear begs to soften it. You never lacked will. You lent strong will to a method that eats will though it swears to build it.

Strong hands held. Wrong orders broke.

You say to me, but some men quit by grip, I saw them, lean and hard though they smile. I hear the ache under that line, for their smile cuts while you bleed. Only ask, what smile. Do they smile like men free or do they smile like men holding. Watch them at the celebration table though you envy them at noon. Eyes fixed on cake though talk runs. Hands tight round water though food stands. Jaw set at pudding though others laugh. They speak of battle and streaks and danger days though years passed. They pity you while envying your bite though they swore they crave none. That smile never was freedom though it wears white dress. It is strain smiling through teeth though teeth ache.

True free never counts days. True free eats plain to shut and leaves though sweet stands, the way you leave a film that bores you. No grip shows because no pull lives.

So pity strain-smiles though envy pulls, for you know both now. They prove their method, not your fate. Hard proves hard method. Hard never proved hard foe.

Their strain proves strain. Not your doom.

Here you must see the finish line though the old talk hides it, for you know lines when I draw them though sweet blurred them.

With the Willpower Method there is no finish line. That is its build, not your fault. Day counts grow though want stays. Danger days never end though years pass. One slip means back to day one though the mouth hoped for mercy. Fear never lifts though floods thin. You run though no tape waits. Sight owns a tape. Belief changed, want dead, thought reframed, life plain. Done. That is why you walk out of the sugar trap easily, at once, and for good.

Mark that line, for it will carry you when old thoughts surface. Easily means no strain. At once means no taper. For good means no return. Not by grip. By sight.

Strain runs. Sight arrives.

## THE TWO FEARS

Now face the two fears that hold you though you name only one, for you know both though shame speaks only first.

First fear stands loud. Fear you will fail again though you try true.

You know its voice though you hate its sound. Last time you failed, you say, though your throat tightens. Last time you swore Monday and broke Thursday though the vow ran hot. Last time you told your wife plain weeks and hid wrappers by Friday though love watched. Why trust this time though past times broke. Better not to vow than to break again though the thought sours mornings.

I hear that fear and I do not mock it, for I stood where you stand though I stood years.

Only see who speaks though the voice wears your name. Not your sense speaks. The old strain speaks through your mouth though you think it yours. Strain taught you fail means weak though fail meant wrong method. Strain taught you break means doom though break meant schedule wound tight. Strain taught you vow means dread though vow means sight. You fear to try because strain burned tries before. That fear never proves you will fail. It proves strain failed you though you paid.

Ask straight, for your own drawer holds the proof though fear paints it doom. Did you fail while seeing or did you fail while blind and bound. Blind. Did you fail with sight of the loop or with bans on the loop though bans feed it. Bans. Did you fail knowing the bell or fearing the bell though fear feeds it. Fear. If past tries ran blind, bound, afeared, what proves a seeing try must break. Nothing. Past blind fails never doom seeing walks though fear swears they do. Blind stumbles never prove eyes must fall.

Think of a door shut fast though we keep the picture to one breath, for you know doors though no talk marked this one.

A man stands before a low door though fear paints it iron. He failed to force it with his shoulder for years though shoulder bled. Now fear tells him not to push again though his shoulder aches at the sight. He does not need to push. The door pulls open inward though push never moved it. His fear of failing to force proves nothing about pulling to free. Fail to push never doomed pull. Your fails to force by strain never doom sight though fear swears they do.

So tell fear its place though it shouts. You failed to strain free. You never failed to see free, for you never saw till now.

Fail fear dies. Sight stands.

Second fear stands soft though it holds harder. Fear you will win and find life grey though sweet leaves.

You know its voice though you speak it low. What fills the afternoon with no bar, you ask, though the question shames you. What marks pay day with no tin. What warms film nights with no packet. What binds birthdays with no cake though love watches. Will food turn flat. Will nights turn long. Will joy thin for every day from now on though the mouth hopes it lies.

I hear that fear and I do not mock it, for we all loved sweet hours though sweet never loved us.

Only see who speaks though the voice wears your name. Not your tongue speaks. Old brainwashing speaks through your tongue though you think it yours. Brainwashing taught sweet means love though love stood near sweet and sweet took the bow. Brainwashing taught sweet means pay though rest paid and sweet took the bow. Brainwashing taught sweet means fun though company warmed and sweet took the bow. You fear grey because sweet claimed each colour though sweet brewed none.

Ask with me, for your own plain hours hold the proof though the old talk discounts them. When did plain ever eat grey though sweet stood far. Summer garden bread torn warm, sun on arms, talk easy, head fed though no flood arrived. Was that grey. Never. Soup hot, belly shut, sleep deep though no raid woke you. Was that grey. Never. If plain hours held colour with no sweet, what proves all hours must grey. Nothing.

Think of a man freed from a cell though we keep the picture to one breath, for you know cells though no iron hangs here.

Years inside, he fears the open field though the gate stands wide. Inside at least meals come, walls hold, hours run known. Outside winds blow though no walls break them. He calls that fear proof he belongs inside though inside starved him. That fear never proves cells feed. It proves cells taught him to fear fields though fields feed all else. Your fear of plain days never proves sweet fed. It proves the sugar trap taught you to fear plain though plain fed you for years before sweet wound tight.

So hold both fears in one hand though they pull apart, for you know ropes when I join them though fear splits them.

Fail fear pulls one arm, win fear pulls the other, though both swear they fight each other. Fail says do not try, you will break. Win says do not try, you will grey. Torn between, you stall though stall pays the trap by the hour. See the hands that hold though fear hides them. Both ropes run back to one house. Trap holds both though fear lends them your voice. Fail fear guards the Willpower Method though method broke you. Win fear guards the sugar trap though trap billed you. One house pulls both arms though arms think they fight.

Cut both by sight though strain swore to cut by force. Fail dies when fails read as method fails, not man fails. Win dies when plain hours read as true pay, not grey. Both die at once though fear swore one must live. Both lived on one lie though fear split it in two. The lie said sweet gives a genuine pleasure, comfort or lift. It never did. It paused payback it laid. Pause died with floods though fear swore pause was gift.

Both ropes belong to one house. Sight cuts both.

You say to me, but fear feels true in the blood, it must mean danger. I hear the ache under that line, for fear feels true while it holds your arm. Only see what fear marks. Fear marks old wires, not true cliffs. Old bells ring loud though floods stopped. Old pictures rise bright though joy died at bite three. Old voices swear need though bread never cures that need. Fear never measured cliffs. Fear measured wires though wires wear cliff dress. Mark wires as wires and feet steady though fear shouts run.

Fear shouts. Sight walks.

## THE EXCUSE SHELF

Now clear the shelf where old excuses sleep though you dust them with care, for you know this shelf though you never named it.

Top shelf holds blood talk. Sweet tooth by birth, you say, though your voice drops. Hooked by build. Hungry by type. Sweet runs in our house though dad hid bars too. I lack the gene for plain though others hold it easy.

Middle shelf holds past talk. Too long in, you say, though your throat tightens. Twenty years flood though plain never stood. Too many fails though will wore thin. Too old to change though wires run deep.

Low shelf holds self talk. No grip by nature, you say, though shame burns. Others hold, I spill. Others shut, I finish. I am the type that finishes though joy died.

I hear each line and I do not mock one, for we all shelved them though dust grows thick.

Only ask, which came first though the old talk swears blood came first. Did the trait brew the loop or did the loop brew the trait though trait wears first dress.

You call yourself night eater by type though noon never saw that type till floods taught nights. Before sweet wound tight, nights ran plain though blood ran same. Same blood. Same mouth. Plain then raid. If blood drove raid, raid would have stood from first breath. Raid grew with floods though blood stayed same. Floods brewed type though type claims to brew floods.

You call yourself weak-willed by nature though work proves the reverse though shame hides work. Deadlines met. Kids raised. Pain held. Money kept though short. Only sweet broke though sweet breaks strong men daily. If nature broke sweet, nature would break all hard holds. Nature held all else though sweet broke. Sweet broke nature though nature claims to break sweet.

You call yourself food-lover by build though plain proves the reverse. Roast shut though meat stayed. Only midnight sweet finished though ache said stop. Love shuts. Wire finishes.

See the turn though shop talk hates it, for you know turns when I mark them though sweet hid this one.

Traits shared by long sweet eaters mark what floods do to men, not what men bring to floods. Night raids mark ban-flood rounds, not night blood. White-knuckle weeks mark strain method, not weak will. Finish-past-joy marks want past like, not love. Hides mark loop shame, not low soul. Each trait arrived after floods though each claims to predate them. Each thins once floods stop though blood never changes. That thin proves cause though talk swears trait. Floods made the type though type claims to make floods.

It is the other way around.

You hand me your hardest line though you speak it low. Even if that holds for others, I am the true hooked one, the one who feels the dark cookies call though others pass. I take that line with both hands and I do not fight it, for grace frees where fight binds.

Say you are. Say your bells ring loudest. Say your nights run deepest. Say your wire runs thickest after years of flood. I tell you flat as one who stood where you stand though I soften nothing true. This way frees the thick-wired fastest though fear swears it frees the light alone. Sight needs no light past. Sight needs eyes now. Thick wire thins once seen though it shouts at first. Deep nights plain once floods stop though first nights pace. Loud bells hush once unfed though first hours ring. The method asks no clean past, no light pull, no strong blood. It asks eyes open and old orders dropped though hands shake.

If wire proves need for sight, thick wire proves it most.

So sweep the shelf though dust chokes at first, for you know clean shelves though sweet dusted them.

Not born sweet-toothed but wound. Not too long in but long taught. Not weak by type but strong on wrong foe. Each excuse read back to front though each felt true while it held your arm. Read straight and each frees though each bound. Wound can unwind though born cannot change. Taught can unlearn though past cannot shift. Wrong foe can swap though weak cannot lift. Straight reading frees even the self-called lost though lost swore no way.

Types never trapped. Traps typed.

## THE EASY OBJECTION

Now hear the hardest knock though it wears honest dress, for you know this knock though fear lends it force.

If walking out runs with no strain, why do all Failed tries hurt so bad. If sight frees, why do books preach grip and counts and days. If this way holds, why does no shop teach it though shops teach all else that pays.

You ask plain and I answer plain, for I asked it too though I blinked for months.

Failed tries hurt because strain hurts though strain claims to heal. Hurt never proved cure. Hurt proved strain. You hurt most where method pressed hardest though foe stood elsewhere.

Shops preach grip because shops sell grip though grip keeps you buying. Apps count because counts keep eyes on screens. Diets ban because bans keep Monday buys. Each sells the method that keeps the loop though each swears to cut it.

Ask with me, for your own purse holds the proof though the old talk buries it. How much paid for strain though strain never freed. Diet sheets. App fees. Scales. Sweet-free packs that cost twice though hunger stayed. Light bars that kept the wire though labels swore plain. How much paid for sight though sight frees. This read. Eyes open. Old orders dropped. If price proved cure, dear strain would have freed long ago though cheap sight frees now. Price never measured cure though shops swear it does. Price measured sellers though buyers hoped.

You say to me, this sounds too good to be true, easy must mean trick. I hear the ache under that line, for good news frightens burned men more than bad.

Only ask, what true means here though the old voice hates plain asks. Did true ever mean hard in your own life though strain taught it must. Did bread true mean hard to chew. Never. Did sleep true mean hard to fall. Never. Did laugh true mean hard to lift. Never. True runs plain though strain paints it steep. If easy meant false, breath would lie, sun would lie, shut would lie though all run with no grip. Easy never proved trick though burned ears hear trick in ease. Easy proved true where traps paint hard to sell strain though hard never freed.

You say, but my neighbour white-knuckled free for years, she holds though she strains, does her hold prove I must strain too. I hear the cut under that line, for her smile shames while you bleed. Only ask, what holds. Does she hold shut or does she hold breath though sweet stands near. Watch her hands at cake. Tight. Watch her eyes at till. Fixed. If strain freed, years would have softened though years hardened.

And you can jump off the cliff as long as you do not fall more than a few metres — mark how that plea sounds once said plain, for we leave its full fall to its own room.

So let the knock die though it rang loud, for you know knocks when I answer them though fear armed this one.

Strain hurt proves strain, not need. Shop praise proves pay, not cure. Ease proves sight, not trick. Neighbour grip proves grip, not freedom. Each knock read straight frees though each bent binds. That is sight answering method though method shouted first.

Knocks fall. Sight stands.

## THREE ORDERS

Now take what sight commands though strain commanded else, for you know commands when I speak them though old orders shout still.

You walked with me through drawers and rounds and fears and shelves though you changed nothing yet and you ate as usual. You saw rules wind though rules swore to free. You saw strong will spent wrong though shame called it weak. You saw both fears held by one house though fears swore apart. You saw types made by traps though types claimed to make traps. You saw knocks fall though knocks rang loud.

Now guard that sight though old voices press, for sight thins fast once fed with doubt though doubt wears new dress.

Doubt will come dressed as sense. One thought at half three, maybe this book lies. One word from a friend, grip worked for me. Each asks you to reopen what sight shut. Do not reopen.

I warn you plain as one who watched both for years. A wobble never means new proof though fear paints it proof. Wobble means old wire twitches though wire dies. Old bell rings though drawer stands empty. Old picture rises though joy died at bite three. Mark wobble as echo, not news. Echoes fade unfed though they swear to grow. News grows unfed though echoes die. That split saves you though fear blurs it.

So take these three though strain told else, for you know my voice now though old voices crowd.

Pity strain-holders though envy pulls, for you know their road now. They hold breath though breath begs. They count days though days never end. They fear tables though tables feed all else. Their hardness never shames your sight though shame begs to compare. Hard road proves hard method. Your sight proves another road runs though shops never sold it.

Read on with eyes open though old voices press, for you know the price of shutting though shut feels safe. Shut feeds wire though wire swears to starve. Open starves wire though wire swears to grow. That is the whole guard though fear paints guards as strain. Guard the belief, not the bite. Belief shut keeps bites shut though bites stand near. Bite guard with belief open always breaks though hands hold hard.

Doubt dies unfed. Sight lives fed.

## SUMMARY

- You now see past fails prove the strain method winds the loop, for bans brew binges though will held hard.
- You now know both fears are held by one house, so you mark wobble as echo not proof though old voices swear news.
- You now believe strong will frees once it leaves the wrong foe, so you guard sight and pity strain though envy pulls.
