## WHAT YOU HOLD

Dawn air on bare walls though walls once held you dull, for you know dawns when I count them though glaze dulled them.

Bin shut. Tin sits though sits once sang. Palm lifts past though lifts once locked. Folded note rides in your coat though coats once stashed foils. You read this nook true though nooks trick worn eyes, for you grasp nooks when I test them though rush blurs them.

We all swam in syrup. I swam in syrup across long seasons with midnight jaws and sunrise pangs and palms too fast for joy.

Read that syrup true though syrup dressed in gold, for you catch syrups when I total them though jingles laud them. Syrup never built though builds pledged to build. Syrup nicked though nicks stayed veiled. Syrup taxed though taxes hid till night. Broth built though builds hold plain. Crust closed though close wants no lock.

You called it a genuine pleasure, comfort or lift, and you know now it never was.

You walked out of the sugar trap. That tag never mocked though mocks lure. It freed though frees hold plain. The faint tug faded though fades dragged slow at first. The taught clamour faded though fades drag slow. To tag the wiring is to loose the palms though palms tremble at start.

I earned my exit across grey spans. First dawn clanged though clangs died. Third dawn gripped though grips felt strange. Sixth dawn hummed faint though hums never spilled. Crust closed. Broth held. Orchard fruit tasted sweet and stopped sweet though sweets once ran the taste. No new snare pulled me though pulls claim praise. I saw clear and I stayed clear. It stayed true.

Heed what lasted though lasts gleam shy, for you catch lasts when I savour them though glaze veils lasts. Eat as much of the real food you love as you want, whenever you want it, and never count a bite again. That charter never chained though chains scare at first. Craving asks and broth answers though answers speak low. Lures ask and broth says no though no frees.

You left it easily, at once, and for good. That claim never bragged though brags take blame. It said plain though plain starts strange. Prompt shut though shuts startle at first. What stays keeps though keeps speak low.

This never turned on syrup. This turned on getting your hunger and your focus and your faith in yourself back. Syrup was only where you felt them go. That turn never preached though preaches tempt. It freed though frees hold plain. Hunger asks. Focus stays. Faith stands though stands want no noise.

Dawns keep. Sight keeps though keeps feel new at first.
Shut bins keep. Plain suppers hold.

## GO LIVE

Tolls fade though fades drag slow. To take toll for want is to take bell for mouth though bells ring and mouths feed. That is flat.

Get on with your life and spend every plain day with willing feet.

That line is not a wish. It is a use. You use it when life gets loud.

Take the noisy week first because the noisy week will come. Work piles past five. Phone rings through lunch. Bus runs late in rain. Boss talks fast. Kids talk loud. Your head hums by Wednesday and your coat feels heavy by Thursday. In the old days that hum meant a trip to the shop and a sweet at the desk and another on the bus home. Now you know that hum for what it is. Tired noise. Not a call for a fix.
