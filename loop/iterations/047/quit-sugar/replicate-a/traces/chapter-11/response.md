Chapter 11
THE LAST MEAL

IN THIS CHAPTER: the set table, the last plate, the marked ugliness, the small hump, the two shut doors, the spoken vow.

*You do not wait to be free, you eat once more as usual and step out now.*

## THE EDGE

Stand at the sink with tea gone cold though night holds late. Plate rinsed. Mouth still rings though belly holds food. You know this stand though sweet paints it as pause. Hands dry. Eyes drop. Bells hum low though hum never meant need.

We all stood here. I stood here with full sight and slow feet.

See the edge straight though fear wraps it in kind mist, for you know edges when I mark them though sweet blurs them. You see the wire though wire wears plain dress. You see tunes hook though tunes swear choice. You see melts thin though melts swear air. You see hides feed though hides swear guide. You see loads bill though lifts swear give. Sight holds though feet drag. That drag never proved you lack grip though grips take praise. It proved old wiring fears the last step though steps free.

Ask with me, for your own nights hold the proof though the old talk buries it. When did plain ever leave you pacing for more though sweet stood far. Bread torn plain, mouth worked, belly shut though no bell rang. Soup hot plain, head fed, night held though no raid woke you. If plain shuts with edges left on, what proves you must wait to step out. Nothing.

You say to me, but I am not ready, my head knows yet my heart shakes. I hear the ache under that line, for shakes feel true while bells ring. Only ask, what shakes though sight stands. Hands shook at first plain meals though meals still shut. Feet shook at first plain shops though shops still rang less. Shakes never proved sight false though fear reads shakes as no. Shakes proved wiring still talks though talk thins.

Think of tight shoes worn all day though we keep the picture to one breath, for you know shoes though feet bear them. The ache sits till dusk though dusk feels far. The hand looses the lace though lace held long. The foot breathes though breath felt far at first. No new foot grew though foot feels new. The shoe never gave the foot its ease though ease seemed tied to off. Off only stops the pinch the shoe made though shoes swear gift.

We wore sweets as shoes. I wore them through whole years.

See shakes straight though shakes fool kind hearts, for you know fears though bells raise them. Fear says wait till bells hush though bells hush by sight, not by wait. Fear says wait till wants die though wants die unfed, not unwatched. Fear says wait till life stills though life never stills for leavers. To wait for hush is to wait for floods to teach shut though floods never taught shut. Hush comes past the step though steps scare. Step brings hush though hush wears slow dress.

Ask with me, for your own waits hold the proof though the old talk counts them as care. Did waits ever thin bells. Never. Did waits ever shut night raids. Never. Did waits ever turn plain grey to bright. Never. Waits fed though waits swore to guard. Waits chained though waits swore to save.

You say to me, but freedom comes later, I must earn it by weeks clean. I hear the hope under that line, for earn feels true while counts shine. Only ask, what earn though trap takes. Plain never asked earn though plain feeds. Breath never asked earn though breath holds. Sight never asked earn though sight frees. To earn free is to pay the wire for leave though leave needs no pay. Leave needs sight though sight stands now.

Mark how fear dresses wait as care though dresses fool kind eyes, for you know dresses when I mark them though love blinds. Care feeds though care should free. Care waits though waits bill. Care counts though counts chain. True care steps though steps scare. True care marks though marks sting at first. To read wait as care is to read chains as arms though arms hold and chains bind.

So mark the edge for what it holds though mists shine, for you know stands when I weigh them though sweet crowds them.

Edge calls. Sight steps though steps scare first.
Wait binds. Sight looses.

## THE PLAIN TABLE

Now set a plain table though feasts beg you to stage a last gorge, for you know tables when I set them though sweet crowns most.

No last gorge. No last pile. No last hunt through shops for far sweets though hunts tempt the proud. No test bites laid in rows though rows tempt the mind that loves trials. You eat as usual though usual lured raids. Same hour. Same plate. Same hands though hands learned floods.

We all craved a grand close. I craved mine with bright bags and last sips.

See plain straight though grand fools tired hopes, for you know closes though sweet paints them. Grands feed though grands swear to seal. Piles wind though piles swear to still. Hunts wake though hunts swear to hush. Last gorges teach loss though loss never lived in sight. Loss lives in grands though grands wear feast dress. Plain seals though plain wears no dress.

Lay bread. Lay meat. Lay greens. Lay broth hot though broth never billed. Lay fruit ripe though ripe never chained. Pour water. Sit down though bells ring. Eat till shut though shut feels strange at first. No count. No weigh. No clock though clocks swore to save.

Mark meals straight though counts fool kind mouths, for you know shuts when I weigh them though sweet counts them. Bread shuts though bread wears no paint. Broth holds though broth wears no shout. Meat feeds though meat wears no tune. Fruit sings low though songs never flood. Plain holds though holds wear no haste. Counts never taught shut though counts swore to teach. Shut taught shut though shut speaks low.

Ask with me, for your own plain hours hold the proof though the old talk discounts them. Did bread torn at noon ever call night raids. Never. Did broth hot at dusk ever wake past joy hunts. Never. Did fruit at close ever drag bells to dawn. Never. Plain fed though plain swore no vow. Plain shut though shut needed no seal.

You say to me, but a last meal must prove I can face sweets and pass them by. I hear the pride under that line, for tests feel true while fear talks. Only ask, what test though sight proves. To lay rows to pass is to ring bells to prove hush though bells ring by ring. To stare to prove grip is to feed tune to prove deaf though tunes feed by stare. Tests never proved free though tests wear brave dress. Sight proves though proofs wear plain dress.

I learned tables through tests I set wrong. I laid bars in rows though rows rang. I stared till hands shook though shakes mocked. Then I ate plain as usual though plain seemed too meek to seal. Plain sealed though seal wore no trumpet. Tests wound. Plain shut.

So mark plain for what it holds though meek fools proud eyes, for you know seals when I taste them though sweet stages seals.

Plain feeds. Tests wind though tests swear to seal.
Meals shut. Sight holds.

## THE UGLINESS

Now eat that last as usual with eyes wide though haste begs you to rush past taste, for you know tastes when I mark them though mouths rush.

Pick up as you always picked up though hands know the path. Bite as you always bit though teeth know the melt. Do not seek new joy though seeks lure. Do not hunt last thrill though thrills tempt goodbyes. Watch what stands there though stands shame the old talk.

Mark the stick first though sticks hide at bite one, for you know sticks though sweet coats them. Sweet films teeth though film wears no colour at first. Tongue coats. Throat clags. Sip called though sips never slaked. More called though more never shut. The mouth chases though chase never lands.

We all felt sticks. I felt mine through whole evenings past joy.

See gorge straight though gorge wears feast dress, for you know floods though throats hide them. First bite sings though songs fade fast. Second bite dulls though dull warns. Third bite sickens though sick frees. Hand moves past sick though sick said stop. Belly heaves though heaves speak low. Head thickens though thick warns. Joy died at bite three though hands marched to empty. That march never proved love though love takes blame. It proved want outlives like though like died early.

Ask with me, for your own last thirds hold the proof though the old talk buries them. Did third bites ever sing like first. Never. Did full packs ever shut like plain. Never. Did past joy hunts ever end with ease in the gut. Never. Thirds warn though warns wear meek dress. Fulls bill though bills hide till dusk.

You say to me, but mine still pleases, my last will sing to the end. I hear the ache under that line, for sing feels true while bells ring. Only ask, who sings though mouths flood. Tune sings though mouths take blame. Shout sings though ears bleed. Wire sings though wire binds. To call flood sing is to call pinch hug though pinch hurts and hug holds.

Think of that paid actor who arrives like relief though we keep the picture to one breath, for you know plays though sweet staged this one. The hit looks like the hero though hero never lived in wraps. It arrives as ease though ease never rode hits. The script brings you back daily for one more take though takes never end.

I learned ugliness through slow bites I forced eyes on. Frosting sweet at noon though noon ran plain else. Cream thick though thick hid stick. Third bite turned though turns warn. Mouth pasted. Gut rolled. Head rang. Hands still reached though reach shamed. Eyes opened though shame tried to shut them. Reach proved wire though reach wore love dress.

Mark how sweet borrows love dress though borrows fool kind eyes, for you know dresses when I weigh them though years tie them. Love never pasted mouths though mouths paste. Love never rolled guts though guts roll. Love never thickened heads though heads thicken. Wiring dressed sweet in love though wiring never asked your leave. As a child sweet came with smiles and kind hands though kind hands meant the love and sweet took the bow. To mark dress is to free hands though hands shake at first.

So mark ugly for what it holds though love wraps it, for you know deeds when I count them though sweet crowns deeds.

Sweet sticks. Sight marks though marks sting first.
Thirds sicken. Sight stays.

## THE SMALL HUMP

Now hear what days bring though fear paints weeks as walls, for you know walls when I mark them though dread builds them.

First days ring though rings hope to still. Bells peak though peaks pass. Head thickens though thick never proves need. Mouth calls though calls thin unfed. Hands scan though scans find naught to mourn. Grouch rises though grouch falls by dusk. Nights pace though pace thins by dawn.

We all feared humps. I feared mine as proof I could not step out.

See humps straight though humps fool tired hopes, for you know humps when I weigh them though sweet lends them weight. Small, not mighty. Brief, not life-long. Days, not years. The tiny physical craving complains though complaints speak low. It tugs though tugs never tear. It whines though whines never rule. The brainwashing voice shouts though shouts borrow the tug to sound big. Tug small. Shout loud though loud never meant large.

Ask with me, for your own clean spells hold the proof though the old talk counts them as flukes. Did thick heads ever stay past days when floods ceased. Never. Did grouch ever hold past the first turn. Never. Did bells ever ring as loud at week three as night one. Never. Humps peak then dip though dips feel far at first. Humps pass though pass wears slow dress.

You say to me, but my body needs sweet for fuel, I will faint and fail without it. I hear the ache under that line, for faint feels true while slumps scare. Only ask, what fuel though need stands small. The body runs its own supply line though lines hide. It brews its daily need from plain food though brews wear no haste. Blood holds its range without spooned loads though spoons swear must. Bread feeds the line though lines never beg sweet. Broth feeds the line. Meat feeds the line. Greens feed the line though lines ask no floods. To call sweet fuel is to call sand oil though sand grinds and oil slides.

I learned lines through dull weeks. Noon plain though bells rang. Head thick day two though thick passed. Bells thinned day four though thin felt far at first. Fruit tasted sweeter in one plain line though lines never preach. Plain held though hold felt new. No new grip held me though grips take praise. Sight held me though sight speaks low.

Mark baselines straight though slumps scare kind hearts, for you know holds when I count them though sweet shakes them. Slumps swing though swings borrow. Dips call though calls bill. Swings never proved need though needs take blame. Swings proved last floods wore off though wears hide. The line steadies once floods cease though steadies wear no haste. The gut learns shut though learns wear slow dress.

So mark humps for what they hold though fears paint them tall, for you know smalls when I weigh them though shouts dress them large.

Humps small. Days pass though days feel long first.
Lines hold. Sight rests.

## THE TWO SHUT DOORS

Now mark two doors that swing though swings tempt last looks, for you know doors when I name them though sweet oils them.

First door wears kind face and says just one though one never stayed one. One at feast. One to prove free. One to keep love though love never needed one. One to close stress though stress never closed by one.

We all eyed that door. I eyed it through whole springs.

See ones straight though ones wear meek dress, for you know ones when I count them though sweet counts them as naught. One re-lights wire though wire slept. One re-tunes shout though shout thinned. One re-hungers bells though bells stilled. One never stayed one though one swore to stay. Evening boxes prove though proofs shame. Night raids prove. Hidden wraps prove. To keep one is to keep the wire though wire swears sleep. That is the sugar trap.

Think of that cliff jump with its short-fall rule though we keep it to one breath, for you know falls though rules hide them. Jump as long as you do not fall more than a few steps though steps mock. Rules never held though rules wear brave dress. Falls held though falls hide at lip. Ones hold falls though ones wear meek dress.

Ask with me, for your own ones hold the proof though the old talk calls them tests of grip. Did one ever stay one past dusk. Never. Did ones ever still bells for good. Never. Did kept sweets ever free though keeps swore to free. Never. Ones wind though ones swear to prove. Ones chain though ones swear to seal.

Second door wears wise face and says doubt though doubt never brought news. What if sight slips. What if bells mean need. What if plain greys. What if weeks drag. What if all this proves false at midnight though midnight never proved true.

See doubts straight though doubts wear thought dress, for you know doubts when I weigh them though fear arms them. Doubts speak as you though voice wears old wire. Doubts borrow past vows that broke by strain though breaks proved wrong way, not wrong end. Doubts borrow strain-holders who white-knuckled weeks though hard roads prove hard ways, not hard ends. To read doubt as news is to read wind as speech though wind passes and speech stays.

You say to me, but doubt feels like care, to doubt is to stay safe. I hear the ache under that line, for safe feels true while fear talks. Only ask, what safe though doors swing. Shut doors guard though guards speak low. Open doors bill though bills hide. Doubt opens though opens wear care dress. Sight shuts though shuts wear plain dress.

I learned doors through looks I almost took. One bar kept in drawer though drawer rang each pass. Doubt kept at night though nights paced. Each kept thought rang though rings seemed meek. Drawer emptied though empty felt strange at first. Doubt named as wire though names free. Rings thinned though thin felt far at first.

So mark doors for what they hold though kind faces shine, for you know swings when I catch them though sweet oils them.

Ones wind. Doubts ope though both swear to guard.
Sight shuts. Sight holds.

## THE VOW

Now sit still with full sight though haste begs you to rush the last line, for you know stills when I mark them though bells press.

Ask if you feel ready though fear pulls last. You should feel keen to step though keen wears no trumpet. Lips almost speak though speaks scare. Feet almost move though moves shake. If shakes still read as loss and plain still reads as grey, read again though haste shames slow. Read till thirds sicken plain in your own mouth though mouths learn slow. Read till ones read as falls plain in your own eyes though eyes learn slow. Do not push with grit though grit tempts the proud. Grit never freed though grit swore to free. Sight frees though sight speaks low.

We all lingered. I lingered till sight burned through wait.

See ready straight though ready wears no blaze, for you know keens when I weigh them though sweet dims them. Ready never meant bells dead though dead tempts as proof. Ready means sight holds though bells still hum. Ready means ugly marked though marks sting. Ready means doors shut though doors still swing meek. Ready means plain trusted though trust feels new. To wait for dead bells is to wait for floods to teach shut though floods never taught shut.

You walk out easily, at once, and for good.

Mark that line though lines scare, for you know walks when I name them though sweet chains walks. No cut down. No keep. No morrow. No swap though swaps tempt neat minds. Honey still rides though rides wear farm dress. Juice still floods though floods wear fruit dress. Pale powders still tune though tunes wear clean dress. To swap is to keep the wire though wires change dress. Wire stays though dress shifts.

Eat as much of the real food you love as you want, whenever you want it, and never count a bite again.

Mark that keep though keeps scare count minds, for you know keeps when I taste them though sweet counts them. Real food shuts though shuts wear no seal. Real meals hold though holds wear no haste. Hunger asks and plain answers though answers speak low. Calls ask and plain refuses though refuses free. To count is to chain though chains wear care dress. To trust is to free though free feels new at first.

Ask with me one last time before you speak, for your own mouth holds the last proof though the old talk begs delay. Did plain ever fail to shut when floods ceased. Never. Did thirds ever sing past sick when eyes stayed wide. Never. Did ones ever free when kept. Never. Sight proves though proofs wear plain dress.

Then speak though voice shakes. Mean it though means scare. Stand though stands feel too meek to seal. Cross though cross wears no blaze.

10. TAKE YOUR LAST ORDINARY MEAL AND MAKE THE VOW.
You eat as usual, mark the ugly, and step out now.

Push the plate. Lift the eyes. Say it slow though slow burns. Say it flat though flat seals. Let old tugs fire new light though fires fool at first. When the thought comes next, let it ring your proof though rings test. Your first proof speaks low though speaks hold. You think BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!

11. WHEN THE THOUGHT COMES, THINK BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!
The tug rings your relief, not your want.

Think it when wraps crack though cracks pull. Think it when smells lift though lifts lure. Think it when tables crown though crowns press. Think it when nights pace though paces tug. Not as chant to drown bells though chants tempt strain minds. As fact that bells prove wire dead though dead still twitches. As joy that dead wire twitches unfed though twitches scare at first. Bells ring. Sight answers though answers speak low.

I say it with you as one who stepped out. Well done. You marked ugly though marks stung. You shut doors though doors swung meek. You spoke though voice shook. You stepped though steps scared. You are free as of now though now feels too plain to trust. Trust plain though trust feels new. Plain holds though holds wear no trumpet.

So mark vows for what they hold though plain seals shine meek, for you know frees when I speak them though sweet swears holds.

Vows spoke. Wires slept though wires swore to live.
Thoughts come. Sight sings.

## SUMMARY

- You now see waits never stilled bells, so you step at once though fear dressed wait as care.
- You now know thirds always sickened past joy, so you mark ugly and shut doors though ones wore meek dress.
- You now believe plain always shut and lines always held, so you vow and think free when thoughts come though twitches scare at first.
