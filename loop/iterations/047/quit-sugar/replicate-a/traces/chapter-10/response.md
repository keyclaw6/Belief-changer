## THE WHITE LAB

You think want starts in you though want never started in you, for you know starts when I mark them though sweet hid this one.

That hunt never proved you hold a sweet tooth though teeth take blame. It proved sweet holds you though sweet swears you hold it.

Ask with me, for your own mouth holds the proof though the old talk buries it. When did plain ever leave you pacing for more though sweet stood far. Bread torn plain, mouth worked, belly shut though no bell rang. Soup hot plain, head fed, night held though no raid woke you. If plain shuts with edges left on, what proves middle must shut without edges. Nothing.

You say to me, but I love sweet, my tongue chose this. I hear the ache under that line, for love feels true while it holds. Only ask, who taught love though tongues take blame. I learned middles through dull years. I chased kind bars though kind hid tune. I called it tooth though tooth never chose.

So mark labs for what they hold though coats shine, for you know tunes when I weigh them though ink blinds.

They test for hook though they call it like, for you know hooks when I name them though coats call them likes. Cups ranked. Notes scored. The top cup wins more mouths though mouths never voted. Then mills run though mills never sleep. Then trucks roll though roads never asked. Then shelves fill with the top tune though tunes never fed. One tune breeds next tunes though tunes swear choice. Mouths learn shout though mouths were built for whisper.

Ask straight, as a man asks about his own purse though the old voice hates plain asks. If tunes met need, why did more tunes breed more want though want hoped to die. If middles pleased, why did pleased mouths raid by night though raids shame. If likes held, why did liked bites flood past joy though joy died at bite three. You know the answer though it shames the old talk. Tunes feed though tunes swear to please.

Labs tune. Mouths pay. Middles hook though middles swear to please.

Tune winds. Sight looses.

Near seven in ten packs in one count carry those tuned pairs though packs wear far names. Not few. Not rare. Near all though all wears plain dress. Calm by eye, loud by gut. I learned melts through boxes I emptied standing. One meant as taste though taste never stayed taste. Hand marched to empty though joy died at bite three. That round never proved I love melts. It proved melts hide loads though melts swear air.

Mark how melt teams with sweet-fat though teams fool full, for you know teams when I mark them though packs hide them. Sweet alone floods. Fat alone sits. Sweet with fat blinds shut though shut should stand. Salt lifts sweet though lift hides. Crunch breaks to cream though cream never warns. Those pairs rarely walk in plain fields though fields feed. Fields give chew. Fields give pause. Fields give shut. Labs weld pairs that skip shut though skips bill. Full never broke. Pairs broke full though pairs wear plain dress.

So mark melts for what they stand though air shines, for you know loads when I weigh them though mouths count air.

## THE WEIGHED LOAD

Now weigh what lift costs though fear begs you to look away, for you know bills when I lay them though sweet hides them.

You drink sweet to lift though lift never lifts. Fast in, spike high, dip low, call loud. Same round though names run bright.

See loads straight though bright blinds kind eyes, for you know floods though cans paint them slim.

Sweet floods past need though need stands small. The gut turns spare to fat though fat never asked. Blood runs thick though thick warns. Weight presses though press builds. Heat simmers low though simmer wears no flame. The heart labours though labour never shows at noon. The load never sits where you think though you count brave.

Liquid loads cheat full though full should hold. A tall cold can slides past shut though shut should stand. No chew. No pause. No warn. Only rush that bills later though bills hide.

Ask with me, for your own afternoons hold the proof though the old talk buries them. Did tall cans hold you past three. Never. Did bright bottles thin night raids. Never. Did rush at noon still bells at dusk. Never.

You say to me, but one small drink harms none, one stands far from harm. I hear the ache under that line, for small feels true while thirst runs. Only ask, what small though spoons stack. One tall cola holds near ten spoons though the can looks slim. One treated drink spends near the whole day budget at once though the mouth calls it one. I tell you flat without white dress, for facts must stand though fear must never drive. Ten spoons in one though thirst meant water. Whole budget in gulps though day runs long. The math shows the load never sat where you thought.

Think of sand in boots though we keep the picture to one breath. Miles never ease till sand tips out though shoes shine.

I learned loads through thick years. Noon cans cold each day though days ran plain else. Head thick by three though belly held food. Nights raided though days counted brave. Loads rose though eyes stayed shut.

Mark how quick loads pass shut though pass fools full, for you know passes when I mark them though thirst blinds. Chew warns. Sip never warns though warns should stand. Plain drink holds though holds Slake. Sweet drink floods though floods wear thirst dress. The gut reads rush as more though more never fed. The blood spikes though spikes borrow. Sweet spends. Sight counts.

## THE ACHING TOOTH

Now feel the tooth that keeps score though smiles beg you to laugh it off, for you know teeth when I mark them though sweet coats them.

Free sweet feeds sour though sour hides at first. Plaque drinks. Acid rises though acid wears no colour. Hard coats thin though thin shows late. Then ache wakes at midnight though noon swore plain. Then drill sings though songs cost.

We all paid dentists. I paid through years I called clean.

See teeth straight though ache fools tired smiles, for you know scores though mouths hide them.

Same treat feeds the loop and feeds acid though acid never shows at noon. Same bite winds wire and thins coats though coats never warn at bite one. Same flood calls night raids and wakes ache though ache waits meek. Two bills from one though one swore free.

Ask with me, for your own smiles hold the proof though the old talk buries them. Did brushed nights ever shut sweet ache for good. Never when floods ran. Did kind packs ever spare coats though coats shine. Never. Did smalls ever spare drills though smalls look meek. Never.

You say to me, but teeth mend, drills fix, this proves naught. I hear the shrug under that line, for mends feel true while chairs recline. Only ask, what mend though loops hold. Drill patches holes though holes warn. Drill never patches wants though wants drill nights. Mends fix coats though coats break again by flood. I tell you flat without white dress and without hard threats, for facts must stand though fear never frees. Free sugars turn to acid that eats coats though coats look hard. Tooth rot stands as the most met ill across lands though lands brush. I tell no fate for your mouth, for crowds bill though mouths differ. I mark the stake where all can see though stakes scare. ## THE SMELL BEFORE THE BITE

Take the wrap in hand though hands shake. Do not bite yet though bite pulls. Hold it shut though shut tunes the ear. Bring it near though near rings. Smell though smell never fed.

Mark what fires though fires fool kind noses, for you know fires when I name them though sweet lights them.

Smell fires first though bite takes blame. Wrap crack rings though teeth never met. Sweet air lifts though air never fed. Mouth floods though belly holds food. Hand twitches though hand means to hold. The call stands before the deed though deeds take blame.

## THE LONG ROOM

## THE BIRTHDAY TABLE

Now stand where love sits though love begs you not to judge, for you know tables when I set them though sweet crowns this one.

Birthday dinner. Warm light. Kind faces round wood though wood holds more than sweet. Talk easy. Laugh loud. Rest from work freed though sweet claims the freedom. We all stood there. I stood there with frosting on my lip and hate in my chest.
