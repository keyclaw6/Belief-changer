## 5. Lexicon and instruction spine

Trap register (always for the behavior): dose, fix, hit, the stick (quoted reader-voice only), feeding the itch, the nicotine trap, conned, brainwashed, chained, slave to the schedule. Apply the hardest-drug lexicon the evidence supports; the reader stays warm, the trap stays contemptible.

Freedom register (shown as lived moments, named only by frozen mantra lines): the coffee that tastes of coffee, stairs taken without thought, the patio that belongs to its owner again, mornings without the clock, the deep breath taken for nothing. Ease and relief arrive as pictures plus mantra echoes, never as distributed adjectives.

Banned register: give up, resist, stay strong, discipline, abstain, sacrifice (except when naming the illusion of sacrifice), trying to stop, one day at a time, recovery journey, cold turkey as a boast-frame. "Quit" as a plain verb is acceptable.

Source-grounded reader dialect (for ventriloquized reader-voice in quotation marks only): ciggies, habit smokes, closet smoker, bumming cigs, just one, one won't hurt, social smoker, the stick, smober, timekeeper, little parties, lost my best friend, lone guy outside, can't quit now, last night on earth, magic moment, cigarette candies, what's the point to life. Grounded in bank-09 D-items.
