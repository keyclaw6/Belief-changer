# Master Plan — Quit Smoking (working title)

Slug: `quit-smoking`. Full-length belief-change book, 15 chapters, planned total 60000 words.

Budget arithmetic: 4500 + 4500 + 3500 + 4500 + 4500 + 4000 + 4500 + 4000 + 4500 + 4500 + 4500 + 4000 + 3000 + 3500 + 2000 = 60000. Range check: 54000 <= 60000 <= 66000.

## 1. Book core

- Target behavior: compulsive cigarette smoking. Total stop: the last cigarette, then none. Not cutting down, not vaping as a destination, not patches or gum as the method.
- Reader (planner-facing state): an adult daily smoker of years who lights without deciding, believes cigarettes steady the nerves and complete the day's pleasures, has failed before by force or by substitutes, and dreads life without cigarettes as deprivation while suspecting the dread itself may be part of the trap.
- Load-bearing false belief (frozen): Cigarettes calm me, complete my pleasures, and help me cope, and life without them would be deprivation.
- Through-line: the relief credited to the cigarette is the ending of a want the previous cigarette created; once that inversion is seen, there is nothing left to miss and nothing to resist.
- Format: full-length book, short single-purpose chapters, one belief-move per chapter, closing with a photographable instruction list and one outward push into life.
- Strongest pro-behavior scene (sole, met head-on in C-07): the drink-lit smoking area on a good night among friends, buzzing with talk, the feeling of a last night on earth where one more will not hurt.
- Destination state: whenever the reader thinks of a cigarette, they feel relief and freedom that they no longer smoke — stopping felt as escape, never as sacrifice.
- Saved ending reframe (appears only in C-15, nowhere earlier): you were never a smoker by nature; you were property the trap kept, and stopping returns what was always yours.

### Fork decisions (all five, Carr default throughout)

- Fork 1, inner state: full Carr personification. Two mechanism characters, always as plain everyday common nouns, never capitalized, never frozen as mantras, never branded tokens. The trivial physical creature: the tiny nicotine itch, starved to death within days. The belief-system that feeds it: the brainwashing voice, which is the book's true target. Its death throes are brief and are to be welcomed, never battled by force.
- Fork 2, outcome: total cessation, commanded with warm certainty. The book issues numbered instructions and expects them followed; following all of them is the reader's own smart choice. Moderation is foreclosed by the pincer and the cliff image: if there is no benefit in smoking often, there is no point in smoking occasionally.
- Fork 3, science weight: Carr's own position. Hard facts stated flatly at full weight where the truth is frightening, then relieved in the same chapter through the escape — never left as the reason to change, never softened away, never a literature review, never announced as a move. Evidence limits honored by not overclaiming; raw citation texture stays out of the argument.
- Fork 4, villain: two villains, both hit hard — the nicotine industry that designed and maintains the trap, and the Willpower Method with its substitutes and delay-talk that kept every past attempt failing. Contempt belongs to both; warmth belongs to the reader.
- Fork 5, void: natural baseline. Change nothing else in life; the body returns to its own baseline on its own and every old context is inhabited confidently to prove freedom. No replacement system, no positive-direction program.

### Redefinition decision

None needed. Smoking is quitable wholesale, so there is no Good-X / Bad-X split, no CAPS subset name, and no margin-for-error doctrine. The only slip rule, carried in C-14 as speech: a lapse is a warning rumble, never a failure and never a license to repeat; what matters is never letting the belief back in.

### Safety perimeter

No medical advice anywhere. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis or illness pointers only. The last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial attention plus a vow, never a dare to smoke more. The plan-wide advisory ADV-01 below is the single source of this perimeter; chapter cards cite its ID in guardrails only.

ADV-01 boxed advisory (front matter; routed on the recap card and in every evidence-ledger safety cell):

> If you take prescribed medication, live with a medical condition, are pregnant, or feel seriously unwell, keep following your doctor's advice and ask them about stopping smoking. This book changes what you believe about cigarettes; it does not diagnose or treat any illness.

### EV-S03 — lights do not cut the dose

- Finding: ventilated/"light" cigarettes do not reduce human intake; smokers compensate with bigger puffs.

- Source IDs: sources S-010, S-080, S-089.

- Permitted inference: machine-measured lower tar/nicotine does not mean the smoker took less; compensation is the designed human response to a nicotine-seeking dose.
- Prohibited inference: every smoker compensates identically, or that unventilated cigarettes are a harm-reduction recommendation.

### EV-S04 — trial odds versus how people actually try

- Finding (exact reader line): licensed NRT vs control raises 6+ month abstinence (pooled RR 1.55); most smokers who try to quit do so unassisted, with unaided success around 7–8%.
- Research units: SEU-004; bank-07 M-013, M-015, M-039.
- Source IDs: sources S-077, S-078.
- Grade: CONTESTED as a single slogan; both component facts SUPPORTED in their own scopes.
- Scope and context: the failed quitter told patches are the method, or that unassisted quitting never works.
- Permitted inference: NRT can raise odds versus placebo in trials; most attempts are still unmedicated; neither fact means the reader cannot quit, and neither fact is a prescription.
- Prohibited inference: "you cannot quit without patches" or "cold turkey never works." The two statistics answer different questions and must not be collapsed.
- Empirical limit: trial effects in motivated trial quitters versus population attempt rates; neither is a lifetime fate nor a guarantee.
- Safety limit: No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis/illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, not a dare to smoke more. ADV-01.

### EV-S05 — withdrawal is a short peak, not a new personality

- Finding (exact reader line): withdrawal symptoms typically peak in the first few days and usually subside within a few weeks; they may begin within hours; MedlinePlus places the sharpest edge about 2–3 days after last use.
