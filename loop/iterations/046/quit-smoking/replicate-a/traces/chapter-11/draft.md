Chapter 11
NO SPECIAL ONES, NO TOMORROW

You know the diary kept in the kitchen drawer with its pencil tallies shrinking by plan. You feel the pride on the days the number drops, the small arithmetic of hope counted in stubs, the packet lasting longer by a day and then by two. You know the hand reaching by address between the allowed hours, quicker and hungrier than before, the mind circling the next permitted dose the way a tongue circles a sore tooth. You believe shrinking the number loosens the chain. Shrinking the number tightens the chain round the fewer links left, and this chapter watches the tallies till only the chain shows.

You know the other bargain kept beside the diary, quieter and almost tender. You feel it speak not as hunger but as fairness, calling one evening by the heaters, one Christmas, one wedding, a reasonable keepsake. You believe fairness proves a kept one must be safe. Fairness here proves the persuasion still lives, and a persuasion kept alive keeps the whole slavery alive with it.

We all stood where you stand now with the favourites gone and one favourite guarded. We all said the night proved nothing must be kept and then whispered that something small might be kept after all. You believe last-kept proves harmless. Last-kept here proves most-believed, and most-believed falls the same way the rest fell when watched to its end.

I smoked eleven years and I cut down a dozen times, and I tell you I know the diary by heart. You know my coat because it wears your shape. I believed fewer must mean freer the way less rain must mean drier. You feel the same hope when you picture tomorrow with only three. Test the hope against the days themselves, because the days remember what the voice forgets.

IN THIS CHAPTER: a diary with shrinking tallies, a kitchen five months clear, a calendar with tomorrow circled, quick answers to old voices, and the doubt that doubts this book itself.

*One always rebuilds the whole trap, and tomorrow only pays the trap for another day.*

THE DIARY THAT CREPT BACK UP

Keep the diary you kept, because here the compromise asks to be watched openly.

You know the morning you drew the line and wrote the new rule in the margin. You feel the resolve warm in the chest while the coffee cools, ten today, seven next week, five by the month's end. You know the first allowed dose tasting sharper for the wait, the chest widening where the chest was bound since the last feed ended, the mind blurring kindly at its edges. You feel the sharpening read as proof the plan works. Sharpening here reads as preciousness, the rapid return toward the level you held before you ever lit, made brighter by hunger between feeds.

You know the hours between allowed doses changing their colour. You feel the clock watched where the clock was never watched, the morning meeting endured for the break, the afternoon stacked toward the permitted hour. You know the talk with friends thinned by waiting, the meal hurried for the hour, the evening measured in gaps. You feel life narrow round the fewer. Narrowing proves the chain shortened, never loosened, with each remaining link carrying more weight than ten careless ones carried before.

We all lived that narrowing and called it progress. We all felt the hand move quicker to the packet at the hour, the pull drawn deeper, the smoke held longer, the stub smoked closer to the filter. You believe deeper proves enjoyment rising. Deeper proves the tiny physical craving fed less often and calling louder between feeds, misread by the brainwashing voice as proof the dose gives more. The body took no less of the drug for the counting, because lips and fingers and lungs learn to take what the meter demands whatever number the diary allows.

You know the creep written plain in the margins week by week. You feel the bad day adding two without decision, the night out adding three, the stressful call adding one more, each addition explained as exception while the rule stays printed above. You know the ten becoming twelve, the twelve becoming fifteen, the fifteen becoming the old packet with a new pencil. You feel shame arrive with the creep and whisper that will lacks. Shame here proves nothing about will, because will never held the chain and never broke it. The chain held by belief tightens whenever belief is fed, and belief is fed by every dose allowed to mean relief.

If fewer loosens, why does fewer always mean each remaining dose watched, waited for, and taken harder, with the mind fuller of smoke on seven than it ever was on twenty.

If counting frees, why does counting always end with the count abandoned and the packet bought again by noon, with the diary left in the drawer unopened.

If the diary proves control, why does control need a diary at all, when the non-smoker at the same table through the same hours keeps no tally, watches no clock, and finishes the day with nothing counted and nothing missed.

You know the only honest answers return the pencil to its drawer. Cutting down makes the thing more precious the way forbidding a fruit sweetens it, and preciousness feeds the very want the diary meant to starve. The fact is the drug leaves within minutes and calls again, and fewer calls answered more hungrily teach the call to sound louder. The hours did the living. The doses did the signing between hungers.

Feel how that seeing fits the cliff you already know. You feared such talk must judge your effort to be moderate. Nothing judges there. Something clears, the way water clears when the stirring stops. To cut down and call it freedom is to jump off the cliff and promise to fall only a little way. The fall does not negotiate, because the trap either holds or it does not, and a trap held a little still holds the whole prisoner.

There is no safe fewer. Fewer feeds the preciousness, preciousness feeds the belief, and belief rebuilds the chain link by link till the diary reads like the old days with new dates.

FIVE MONTHS CLEAR AND ONE

Look now at the kitchen five months clear, because here the kept one shows its true size.

You know the house you mean without my naming it. You feel the winter coat smelling clean through washes, the purse heavier by the week's tally unspent, the mornings opening without the bedside reach. You know the kettle doing the waking, the plate doing the closing, the pause doing the resting, each hour returned to its owners. You feel pride warm and quiet, the kind that needs no witness. You believe such pride proves one could now manage one. Pride here proves freedom already held, and freedom held needs no test to prove it.

You know the evening the test arrives wearing friendship. You feel the patio heaters warm on the face, the drink cold in the palm, the laugh carrying the table the way laughter carries when no clock watches. You know the packet tapped and offered with easy ceremony, the voice alongside it light and fond, calling the white tube a keepsake for old times. You feel the hollow stir thin under the ribs where drink and watching have fed it, the tiny physical craving whispering after months quiet, the brainwashing voice translating the whisper as memory of pleasure. You believe memory proves a gift must live there. Memory here proves pairing only, the old address of hands moving before thought, and pairing ends where seeing begins.

We all heard that voice after long clear months and mistook it for our own. We all heard it reasonable and almost loving, saying the trap is gone so one cannot rebuild it, saying months of freedom have earned a taste. You feel sense in the sentence, a witness speaking for calm days. You know the sense borrows your mouth. Answer it with the months themselves, and ask what the months ask.

I tell you I lived those months once and lit the one. You know the pattern because it wears your shape. I had five months clear, the house clean, the mornings mine, the stairs climbed without thinking of breath. You feel the evening I mean, the drink in hand, the offer held out, the thought arriving that one would harm nothing. I took the one to prove I was free, and I tell you the proving rebuilt the prison in a night. You know the second following before closing, the morning packet bought again by noon, the diary reopened with shame. You feel the small division open again between me and the sleeping house, the spray and the mints, the shirt hung by the window. I felt it all return not as punishment but as mechanics, the way a relit lamp rebuilds its whole flame from one touch.

You know the words spoken afterward in your own accent, learned the hard way after months clear that one cannot be managed. You feel the hardness carry truth. I know I can never have just one. I learned that the hard way, thinking after five months I could control myself and just have one. Nope. You know the bluntness borrows many mouths besides mine. Answer the bluntness with the trap itself, and ask what the trap asks.

If one proves freedom, why does one always mean the second before the night ends and the rule rebuilt whole by morning, with the hand again reaching before decision.

If the kept one is small, why does the small one always grow the whole persuasion back, with the morning reach returning, the meal demanding its signing, the break demanding its pellet, the night demanding its edge.

If months clear made you stronger than the drug, why did the drug need only minutes to quiet and then to call again, with the hollow returning louder for the lighting than it ever sounded in the clear months.

You know the only honest answers return the keepsake to its giver. One does not test freedom, because freedom already proved itself in months of clean mornings. One tests the lock by locking it again. The fact is a single dose reattaches the tiny physical craving and re-arms the brainwashing voice that misreads the twinge as proof the dose gives company. Company never came from the tube, because the table gave company before the packet appeared and keeps giving after it is pocketed.

There is no such thing as just one.

Feel how that sentence fits the kitchen five months clear. You feared such lines must frighten you into obedience. Nothing frightens there. Something protects, the way a rail protects on high stairs without scolding the walker. The rail does not threaten the fall, it removes the fall by removing the step. The kept one is the removed step kept, and the stairs stay safe only while every step stays walked without testing the drop.

You were conned, not weak, when the offer sounded like your own thought. The thought arrives from the old persuasion defending its picture, not from your heart reading the months. Thoughts cannot be stopped by effort, but they can be answered by seeing, and seeing answers this one each time without opening the packet.

Never reopen the decision.

Feel how that sentence closes the kitchen door quietly. You feared such closing must bind you. Closing here frees, because the decision already made in clear seeing needs no daily court. The court is the trap's last room, and empty courts hold no trials.

TOMORROW ON THE CALENDAR

Meet now the calendar with tomorrow circled, because here delay shows its price.

You know the square you mean, marked with a small star, the date chosen for sense. You feel relief arrive with the marking, the shoulders dropping, the packet tapped with lighter heart, as if choosing a day had already loosened the chain. You know the smoking between now and the star tasting almost innocent, permitted by the plan, each dose softened by the promise ahead. You feel the softening read as kindness to yourself. Softening here reads as the trap paid in advance, the days till the star smoked with extra fondness because ending lends them glow.

We all circled such squares and called the circling wisdom. We all said the week must first be kind, the stress must first pass, the party must first be survived, the winter must first turn. You believe care proves timing matters. Timing here proves belief still reads the dose as pleasure to be left, and pleasure to be left demands farewell fondness that feeds the very want the star meant to end.

You know the star moving its place with its own logic. You feel the bad news arriving Tuesday, the star rubbed out and redrawn Friday, the Friday bringing friends and drink, the star redrawn Monday, the Monday bringing the early meeting and the sharp words. You know the hand already reaching through each postponement, the tally climbing while the star travels. You feel life supply endless good reasons the way weather supplies endless clouds. Reasons prove the trap's arithmetic, never life's demand, because life never asks for a dose to meet it and the dose never meets anything but its own hollow.

If tomorrow frees, why does tomorrow always cost today plus tomorrow, with every dose between now and the star smoked and paid for and breathed and coughed, for something you already see gives nothing.

If waiting steadies, why does waiting always mean the mind fuller of smoke than before the star was drawn, with the last week before the date smoked hardest of all, the way condemned hours are clutched hardest.

If the date proves resolve, why does resolve need a date at all, when seeing done now frees now, and seeing postponed stays unseen through every dose that postponement buys.

You know the only honest answers return the pencil from the calendar. Delay extends cost without adding power, because power never came from the date. Power came from seeing the dose gives nothing, and seeing keeps its power only while acted on. The fact is each day waited is another day chained for no gift, another packet bought, another morning coughed, another stair climbed winded while a non-smoking colleague breathes easy beside. The chain charges rent by the day, and tomorrow pays today's rent again with interest.

We all believed such counting of cost must frighten you into haste. Nothing frightens there. Something frees, because cost seen plain makes haste feel like relief rather than pressure. You do not hurry from fear, you hurry the way a hand leaves a hot rail once felt, quickly and gladly and without ceremony. The rail burns whether left today or Monday, and Monday adds only burns.

See the calendar plainly at last. You were taught quitting waits for calm seas. Calm seas never come by waiting, because the trap makes its own weather. Freedom does not wait for life to quiet, it quiets life by removing the hollow that noise fed. The star that frees is the hour the seeing completes, and the hour completes while these words are read, not in a square.

QUICK ANSWERS TO OLD VOICES

Hear now the old voices together, because here they ask to be answered quickly and then left.

“I will just cut down to three.” You know the diary and its creep. You feel the three becoming precious, waited for, taken harder. Three keeps the hollow alive between hungers and teaches the call to sound louder. Fewer never starved the tiny physical craving, it drilled it to shout.

“I will keep the Christmas one, the wedding one, the night-out one.” You know the patio heaters and the offered lights already stripped for credit. You feel the night doing its own work, the drink loosening, the laugh binding, the music lifting. The kept one keeps the whole persuasion intact the way one cord keeps the lamp lit. There is no such thing as just one, and a special one is only a just-one with a bow on it.

“I will quit tomorrow, after this packet, after the stress passes.” You know the calendar and its travelling star. You feel each waited day smoked and paid for. Tomorrow buys the trap another day of rent for no gift. Seeing done now frees now, and seeing postponed stays hungry through every dose it buys.

“Lights made it milder, so I will step down through mild.” You know the filter ring with its tiny vents already seen, the lips and fingers covering the holes without teaching, the puff drawn bigger for the same dose. You feel the mild taste lie. Machine numbers never measured your hands. Mild kept the dose while teaching the belief that progress was happening. Progress never lived in the packet colour, because the packet always held the same product in different paper.

“I tried patches and gum before and failed, so I need the right aid this time.” Hear this one carefully, because two true facts live here that answer different questions. In careful trials an aid can lift the odds against a dummy aid, and out in the world most tries happen with no aid at all and most of those do not hold. Neither line decides you. Neither line proves you weak or proves you need a product to escape. What decides is whether the dose is still believed to give something. Belief changed frees with or without a prop, and belief kept returns with or without one. This book changes what you believe smoking gives you, and it prescribes nothing and forbids no care you already hold.

“I controlled months before, so I can control one now.” You know the kitchen five months clear and the night the one rebuilt the rule. You feel the pride of clear months speak. Clear months prove you can live complete without a dose, never that a dose has become safe. Control of months does not make the trap kinder, it proves you no longer need its kindness.

“I love smoking but I hate being a smoker, so a little love must be real.” You know the lighting minute already watched to its end. You feel heaven arrive on the same short meter, within pulls of the last feed fading, and thin before the tube burns halfway. Heaven never lived in the pull, because the pull dulled what drink and talk were brightening. Love never lived in the tube, because love arrived with the faces and stayed with them after the ends were stubbed. It never did anything FOR you; it only did things TO you.

“Everyone I know smokes, and I will be the odd one out.” You know the shop queue and the doorway offer already seen. You feel the fear of standing apart while they stand together. Belonging never lived on the cold step, because the step divides the circle it claims to close. Watch them with clear eyes and keep your own judgment. Pity the lit hands without envy, the way clear water pities muddy without wishing to muddy itself. The friend stays a friend, kind and lit and caught, to be kept without following.

You know these answers return each voice to its speaker. Each voice arrives from the brainwashing voice that misreads the hollow as proof the dose gives something, not from your heart reading the months. Voices cannot be stopped by effort, but they arrive pre-answered once seen. Hear the voice, hear the answer beneath it, and walk on without opening the packet.

You hear it level and clever, almost protective. “This is only counter-talk. You have swapped one persuasion for another. You tell me to keep smoking while I read, you flatter me by blaming a method, you frighten me with corridors and then tell me not to fear. Why should I trust a book that argues so hard to be trusted.” You feel the cleverness carry weight, the way all last defences carry borrowed dignity. You know the cleverness borrows your mouth. Answer it with the book itself, and ask what the book asks.

We all felt that doubt late, when the favourites had fallen and only the method stood to be doubted. We all mistook thoroughness for trickery, the way a prisoner mistakes the key's turning for another lock. You believe argument proves persuasion. Argument here proves respect, because respect shows its workings openly and leaves the testing in your hands. Trickery hides its workings and demands obedience. These pages demand nothing but open seeing while you smoke as normal till the seeing completes.

If this were another persuasion, why does it give you nothing to buy, no prop to wear, no fee to pay, no meeting to attend, with the only product named as the thing to leave and the only beneficiary named as the seller who dosed you.

If this were flattery, why does it leave you no excuse to keep the kept one, no fewer to hide behind, no tomorrow to circle, with every soft bargain stripped in these last pages till only the trap stands bare.

If this kept you smoking by telling you not to stop yet, why did the telling remove the threat that closed your mind on page one, with the diary unwatched and the clock uncounted while seeing did its work. The telling never asked for more smoke, it asked for less fear while reading, and fear removed lets eyes stay open.

You know the only honest answers return the doubt to its sender. The doubt arrives from the same persuasion that guarded the night and the diary and the calendar, now guarding itself by calling seeing by its own name. Persuasion calls truth persuasion when truth corners it. Truth answers by pointing to the rooms already tested, the kettle doing the waking without signing, the laugh binding without sealing, the heaven minute thinning before halfway, the five months clear needing no test. Test the rooms again tonight with your own body as witness, and let the rooms answer louder than the doubt.

I tell you I doubted these words the same way before they freed me. You know my coat because it wears your shape. I read with a packet in my pocket and a pencil in my drawer, waiting to catch the trick. You feel the waiting I felt, the eyes narrowed, the mind keeping score. I caught no trick, only tallies that crept, keepsakes that rebuilt, stars that travelled, till the catching itself grew tired and seeing stood plain. You believe such witness proves nothing but one man's reading. Witness here proves the method asks only what your own evenings already proved, and evenings repeat where excuses vary.

It's the other way around.

Feel how that sentence turns the doubt round. You feared such turning must make you obedient. Nothing obeys there. Something owns, the way eyes own light without obeying the sun. The book stays a book, to be finished and then left. The seeing stays yours, to be kept without reopening. The doubt stays a caught voice speaking, to be heard without obeying.

11. NEVER ALLOW JUST ONE OR A SPECIAL ONE
One keeps the whole trap alive.

You know the totality now standing where bargains stood, the diary closed, the keepsake refused, the calendar uncircled. You feel belonging keep its circle unbroken, no exile to doorways, no tallies in drawers, no stars travelling week to week. You believe such keeping must be held by effort. Such keeping is held by seeing, and the seeing is done each time the offer is heard without obeying and the thought is answered without opening.

We all kept one lit picture to argue the trap might love us a little. We all watched that picture tested drop by drop till no drop remained with the tube. You believe such testing must leave life thinner. Testing here leaves life thicker, because returned credit thickens its owners. The morning thickens. The meal keeps its closing. The break keeps its rest. The night keeps its laugh. The hollow alone thins, the tiny physical craving already starving, misread so long by the brainwashing voice as proof of pleasure.

There is no such thing as just one.

Never reopen the decision.

You know the closed door now standing where the court stood, the decision made in clear seeing needing no daily trial. You feel relief quiet and complete, the kind that needs no witness. You believe quiet proves something still missing. Quiet here proves the tug ended, because tugs need two ropes and both ropes belonged to the trap. No rope remains to pull.

You give nothing to the night by leaving the tube from it. You escape everything the diary and the keepsake and the calendar kept charging. The hours did the living. The dose did the signing. The signing ends where seeing begins, and seeing begins now and does not reopen.

SUMMARY
- You believe cutting down and keeping special ones only keep the trap alive, because fewer makes each remaining dose more precious and one rebuilds the whole rule.
- You see that delay only extends the cost without adding power, because every waited day is smoked and paid for while seeing done now frees now.
- You understand that future thoughts of one or tomorrow arrive as the trap speaking rather than your own reasoning, because each voice returns to the hollow it came from once answered.
