# Master Plan — Quit Smoking
`loop/iterations/046/quit-smoking/replicate-a/traces/plan.md` — ITER 046, Fork-1 plain-noun discipline, SUMMARY cap.

# 1. Book Core

**Target behaviour:** Compulsive cigarette smoking — the nicotine trap. Total stop at the last cigarette, then none. Not cutting down, not vaping as a destination, not NRT as the method.

**Reader (planner-facing state):** An adult daily smoker who wants to stop, has failed two or more times by willpower, patches, gum, or cold turkey, and dreads life without cigarettes as miserable or impossible while still lighting each cigarette half-automatically.

**Load-bearing false belief (frozen):** Cigarettes relax me, help me cope, and are my pleasure, and I would be deprived without them.

**Through-line:** Every claimed gift of the cigarette is relief from a discomfort the cigarette itself created; there is nothing to give up, so stopping is escape, not sacrifice — no willpower, no shame, free the moment the belief changes.

**Format:** 14 chapters, 60,000 words total. Single general-adult edition. Fresh writer sees only style guide + this plan + immediately previous chapter.

**Fork decisions (Carr defaults, brief-confirmed):**
- **Fork 1 — mechanism:** Full Carr personification at full strength, in plain common nouns only — never capitalised, never branded, never on the mantra sheet: the tiny physical craving (trivial, near-imperceptible withdrawal that complains briefly when unfed and starves within days) and the brainwashing voice (the lifelong installed belief-system that misreads that twinge as proof the cigarette gives pleasure or relief). The craving is small, starving, already dying, to be revelled in — never a mighty enemy demanding willpower.
- **Fork 2 — outcome:** Total cessation, commanded with cheerful certainty. Numbered instructions, all to be followed. Moderation and just-one foreclosed by the pincer and the cliff-jump. Autonomy-led total freedom: last cigarette, then none.
- **Fork 3 — science weight:** Hard facts at full Carr force, flat and frightening where true, then disowned as the motive — relief in the same breath. Nicotine as a drug that creates the need it relieves; body recovery brief; the trap mental. Facts as perception-shifters, never a literature review; raw citations quarantined to back matter.
- **Fork 4 — villain:** Two villains, both hit hard: the nicotine trap and the industry that engineered and markets it, and the willpower method with its substitutes that kept the reader failing. Never the smoker.
- **Fork 5 — void:** Change nothing else in life. The non-smoker baseline returns on its own; no replacement ritual as the method; old contexts inhabited confidently to prove freedom.

**Redefinition:** Failure redefined once: past failures were failures of the willpower method, never of the reader. Giving up redefined: not surrendering a pleasure but escaping a trap. Margin-for-error doctrine: a single puff re-attaches the tiny physical craving and re-arms the brainwashing voice; the rule is none, not fewer — stated as protection, not threat.

**Safety perimeter:** No medical advice. No instruction to ignore a doctor. Crisis or illness pointers only. Last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, never a dare to smoke more. NRT, patches, gum, vaping named as substitutes that keep the attachment, never prescribed or recommended. Pregnancy, disease treatment, youth prevention out of scope.

**Strongest pro-behaviour scene (met head-on in Ch 11):** Night out with drinks and friends in the smoking area — offered a cigarette, drinking, not wanting to be the only one not smoking, thinking one won't hurt.

**Destination state:** A non-smoker happy to be free — thinking of cigarettes only with relief that the slavery is over, pitying rather than envying smokers, guarding the belief and never reopening the decision.

**Fresh ending reframe (saved for Ch 14):** The empty chair outside: you spent years believing the cigarette kept you company out there in the cold; now you see it was the thing that sent you out there alone — freedom is walking back inside and staying in the warmth.
# 2. Compact Evidence Ledger

**E-01 — the cigarette as off-switch (lived):** Reader line: "If anything stressful happens in my life, a cigarette takes care of it." — LEU-001; S-001; grade lived account. Scope: P-01 stress moments, hand already reaching, first puff credited with care. Permitted: smoker feels rapid "taken care of" feeling and credits the cigarette; same writer can know the credit is false and still feel relief. Prohibited: cigarettes treat the underlying problem; stress without a cigarette is medically dangerous. Empirical limit: lived credit-feeling, not efficacy proof. Safety: no medical advice; no instruction to ignore a doctor.

**E-02 — one won't hurt with drinks (lived):** Reader line: "You often get offered a cigarette and in that kind of environment it's hard to turn down. You don't want to be the only one not smoking and because you've been drinking, you don't think about the risks, you think one won't hurt." — LEU-002; S-051; grade lived account. Scope: P-02 night out, smoking area, friends, alcohol. Permitted: social scene plus drinking named as the moment the social-smoker rule collapses into one-won't-hurt. Prohibited: reader must avoid all friends or all alcohol as the method. Empirical limit: collapse moment, not proof all socialising requires smoking. Safety: as E-01.

**E-03 — twenty failed attempts (lived):** Reader line: "I had probably attempted at least twenty times before and used most of the replacements and meds on the market with no success." — LEU-003; S-006; grade lived account. Scope: P-03 years of patches, gum, replacements, cold turkey. Permitted: repeated failed attempts with replacements and willpower are typical, not proof the reader is uniquely weak. Prohibited: nobody ever quits; replacements must or must never be used. Empirical limit: community-typical failure history, not trial efficacy. Safety: as E-01; no prescription for or against any product.

**E-04 — love it, hate being a smoker (lived):** Reader line: "the moment I light up I'm in heaven, but feel shitty afterwards. So whilst I love smoking I hate being a smoker" — LEU-004; S-013; grade lived account. Scope: P-04 lighting-up moment called heaven, aftertaste shame. Permitted: "I enjoy it" can coexist with hating the smoker identity; enjoyment named at the moment of lighting, not as day-long gift. Prohibited: reader is lying about enjoyment; enjoyment mocked. Empirical limit: moment-of-lighting report. Safety: as E-01.

**E-05 — reward for almost everything (lived):** Reader line: "Cigarettes were my reward for…well, almost everything. Teaching a class. Finishing a story. Finishing a paragraph. Driving 500 miles. Driving to the grocery store." — LEU-005; S-045; grade lived account. Scope: P-01 any completed act paid with a cigarette. Permitted: the reward can attach to almost any completed act; the party is the cigarette, not the work. Prohibited: work or driving requires nicotine; quitting means no celebrations. Empirical limit: attachment pattern. Safety: as E-01.

**E-06 — just one after months (lived):** Reader line: "I know I can never have just one. I learned that the hard way, thinking after 5 months I could control myself and just have one. Nope." — LEU-006; S-006; grade lived account. Scope: P-04/P-03 five months free, moderate voice, chain back. Permitted: a long quit does not make just-one safe; learned-the-hard-way never. Prohibited: a slip dooms the reader forever if the belief is not let back in; no dare to test just-one. Empirical limit: lived relapse pattern. Safety: as E-01; never license a deliberate repeat.

**E-07 — ten-second hit, rapid fade (science):** Finding: "When cigarette smoke enters the lungs, nicotine is absorbed rapidly in the blood and delivered quickly to the brain, so that nicotine levels peak within 10 seconds of inhalation. But the acute effects of nicotine also dissipate quickly, along with the associated feelings of reward; this rapid cycle causes the smoker to continue dosing to maintain the drug's pleasurable effects and prevent withdrawal symptoms." — SEU-001; S-009; grade SUPPORTED. Scope: smoked-nicotine pharmacokinetics. Permitted: the relief is replenishment of a rapidly fading dose, not a gift solving the original stress. Prohibited: every puff a unique pleasure; you cannot quit. Empirical limit: smoked nicotine kinetics. Safety: as E-01.

**E-08 — the product is nicotine (science/history):** Finding: "The cigarette should be conceived not as a product but as a package. the product is nicotine." — SEU-002; S-091; grade SUPPORTED (historical industry document). Scope: manufacturer-internal product definition. Permitted: an industry scientist defined the cigarette as a nicotine package and dispenser, not a flavour accessory. Prohibited: quoting an internal memo as public-health consensus; dare to smoke more to see the product. Empirical limit: internal planning memo, not a clinical trial. Safety: as E-01.

**E-09 — lights do not cut the dose (science):** Finding: ventilated/"light" cigarettes do not reduce human intake; smokers compensate with bigger puffs. — SEU-003; S-010, S-080, S-089; grade SUPPORTED. Scope: P-04 mild/light bargain. Permitted: machine-measured lower yields do not mean the smoker took less; compensation is the designed human response to a nicotine-seeking dose. Prohibited: every smoker compensates identically; unventilated cigarettes as harm-reduction recommendation. Empirical limit: human smoking versus machine yields. Safety: as E-01.

**E-10 — NRT trial odds versus how people try (science):** Finding: licensed NRT vs control raises 6+ month abstinence (pooled RR 1.55); most smokers who try to quit do so unassisted, with unaided success around 7–8%. — SEU-004; S-077, S-078; grade CONTESTED as a single slogan; both component facts SUPPORTED in their own scopes. Scope: P-03 failed-quitter, method-shopping. Permitted: NRT can raise odds versus placebo in trials; most attempts are still unmedicated; neither fact means the reader cannot quit, and neither is a prescription. Prohibited: you cannot quit without patches; cold turkey never works — the two statistics answer different questions and must not be collapsed. Empirical limit: motivated trial quitters versus population attempts; not lifetime; not a guarantee. Safety: as E-01 plus no prescription.

**E-11 — withdrawal is a short peak (science):** Finding: withdrawal symptoms typically peak in the first few days and usually subside within a few weeks; they may begin within hours; MedlinePlus places the sharpest edge about 2–3 days after last use. — SEU-005; S-009, S-081; grade SUPPORTED (population typical, not every reader). Scope: P-01/P-03 day two or three, irritability, craving, restlessness. Permitted: the restless empty feeling is withdrawal pharmacology with a short typical peak, not proof the cigarette was the real self. Prohibited: withdrawal is weeks of physical torture for everyone; harmless for every person in every circumstance. Empirical limit: dependent users; genetics influence severity; not torture-for-all. Safety: as E-01; does not negate rare complications — see a doctor if worried.
