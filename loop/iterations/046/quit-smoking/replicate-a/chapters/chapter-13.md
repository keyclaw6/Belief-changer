WHAT THE DAYS HAND FORWARD

See what the days prove once lived through, because here freedom asks only to be carried on.

You know the three mornings, the two queues, the breaks, the evenings, each met and already owned. You feel them stack without fanfare into something sturdier than argument, the way bricks stack into a wall that needs no speech to stand. We all waited for a day when doubt would vanish entirely. We all believed doubt gone must come first, and so we circled stars on calendars while doubt stayed exactly where it was. You feel doubt still move thin under the ribs on some mornings. Hear it for what it is. It is mechanics sounding where feeds once answered, not you missing. If the thought proved desire, why does the thought always arrive thinner each time, with the answer beneath it readier, till the thought sounds like a language once spoken fluently and now half remembered.

You know the stub behind you grows smaller with distance rather than larger. You feel the farewell table sit plain in memory without ache, the stain and ash watched to the end having done their work.
