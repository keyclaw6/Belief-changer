Chapter 12
YOUR LAST CIGARETTE

You know the plain table you have been avoiding without knowing you avoid it. You feel the wood cool under the palms, the ashtray empty and clean for once, the pack lying on its side with one tube left, the lighter beside it catching the lamp. You believe this table must hold a farewell full of ache, because every farewell you ever staged with paper ended in ache. This one ends ache, because ache was what paper always brought and never lifted.

You feel the evening ordinary round it, no party, no crisis, no signal from the sky. You know the kettle ticking in the kitchen, the street noise thin through glass, the clock moving without ceremony. You believe a great change must need a great night, and so you wait for thunder. Thunder never freed anyone here, because freedom never lived in the night. It lived in seeing done plain on an ordinary evening at a plain table.

We all stood at such tables and called them final a dozen times. We all lit a last one with wet eyes and made promises to the ash, then woke with the hand reaching before feet touched the floor. You believe this table must be another of those tables, because the shape looks the same. Shape here proves nothing, because the seeing behind the shape was never there before. The shape stayed empty while belief stayed intact, and belief intact always refilled the pack by noon.

I smoked eleven years and I remember my own plain table the way you will remember yours. You know my coat because it wears your shape. I sat late with one tube left and told myself I was saying goodbye to a friend, and my chest hurt with the saying. You feel the same hurt rising now when you picture the stubbing. I tell you the hurt reads the tubes wrong, because no friend ever charged rent by the day for nothing. What hurt that night was not leaving pleasure, it was leaving years of paying.

ARE YOU READY TO FINISH

Ask yourself plainly now, with the pack almost done, whether you feel ready to finish. You know the diary closed in its drawer, its pencil tallies left unopened. You feel the keepsake refused without a bow, the night doing its own work without paper hired to stand in the photograph. You believe readiness must feel like iron, a hard certainty that will never waver. Readiness here feels lighter than iron, because iron was what the old method demanded and never delivered. Readiness here feels like eagerness to be done, a quiet wish to stub the thing and stand without it.

We all waited for a day when doubt would vanish entirely. We all believed doubt gone must come first, and so we circled stars on calendars while doubt stayed exactly where it was. You feel doubt still moving thin under the ribs when drink and watching feed it, the tiny physical craving whispering after hours quiet, the brainwashing voice translating the whisper as memory of pleasure. You believe whisper proves unready. Whisper here proves mechanics only, the short pharmacology sounding where feeds once answered, and mechanics need no permission to sound while seeing holds.

I tell you I sat at my table with the same thin whisper alive. You know the evening I mean, the lamp low, the house quiet, the hand slower than usual to lift the tube. You feel the reluctance I felt, not love but habit of hand, the old address of fingers moving before thought. I asked myself whether I champed at the bit or merely wished to champ, and I found the wish itself was the champing. You believe champing must roar. Champing here hums low, because low hum already proves the persuasion has lost its pretty pictures and keeps only an address.

If you feel that hum, you are ready. If you feel the diary no longer tempts with fewer, if you feel the kept one no longer glows with tinsel, if you feel tomorrow no longer shines cleaner than today, you are ready. You know the totality now standing where bargains stood, the cliff seen plain with no safe shorter fall. You feel belonging keep its circle unbroken without the cold step, the morning able to open without the bedside reach, the meal able to close without signing. You believe such seeing must be held by effort through the night. Such seeing holds by itself once done, because done seeing needs no guard but remembrance.

If you do not feel that hum, do not force the table. You know the rooms already tested, the kettle doing the waking, the laugh binding without sealing, the heaven minute thinning before halfway, the five months clear needing no test. You feel some room still dim, some bargain still reasonable in work clothes. Re-read those rooms with your own evenings as witness, and let them answer louder than hesitation. The table waits without charging rent, because waiting a day to see clearly costs nothing beside smoking a day to stay blind. Nothing to lose and everything to gain.

Feel how that sentence steadies the hand rather than hurries it. You feared such lines must push you before seeing completes. Nothing pushes there. Something frees, because cost of reading on holds no dose in it and gain of reading on holds mornings returned. The fact is the trap either holds or it does not, and a trap seen holds nothing. When seen holds, the hand wants the stubbing the way a hand wants down a hot rail, quickly and gladly and without mourning.

THE PLAIN TABLE

Sit now at the plain table with the one tube left, because here the exit asks to be watched openly.

You know the tube you mean without my naming its brand. You feel the paper dry between fingers, the filter firm, the weight light for all it cost through years. You know the lighting minute you have lit ten thousand times, the flare short, the first pull widening the chest where the chest was bound since the last feed ended. You feel the widening read for years as care arriving. Widening here reads as replenishment only, the rapid return toward the level you held before you ever lit, made brighter by hunger between feeds.

We all lit that minute and called it heaven. We all felt the mind blur kindly at its edges while papers and unsolved problems sat untouched on the desk. You believe blur proves help given. Blur here proves nothing given, because nothing moved on the desk while blur moved in the head. The hour did the living. The dose did the signing between hungers. The signing never signed anything but its own hollow.

Light it now as usual and watch it the way you never watched it, with full attention kept on the tube from flare to stub. You know the yellow stain creeping where lips meet filter, the faint brown blooming under white. You feel disgust rise and want to look away toward the lamp, the street, the kettle. Keep eyes on the stain, because stain tells truth the blur always hid. Stain never lived on the faces you love, because faces never yellowed the fingers. Stain lived only where smoke passed, marking the passage the way soot marks a chimney that never warmed the house.

You know the ash lengthening without permission, grey and flaking, warm at its tip and dead an inch behind. You feel the hand lift and tap by old address, the wrist turning before thought. Watch the tap and the fall, because fall shows how little substance ever lived there. Ash never fed a morning, because mornings opened by kettle and light long before ash learned to fall. Ash only borrowed the hand that might have held a cup, a rail, a shoulder, and paid the borrowing with dust.

You know the taste turning by the third pull, the first sharpness thinning into stale. You feel the throat catch where throat always caught, the dry mouth papering over drink and talk. You believe stale proves you drew wrong. Stale here proves drawing itself, because every tube turns the same corner halfway down. The heaven minute never lasted past halfway in ten thousand lightings, and halfway always arrived with the same papery mouth, the same tight chest hauling, the same split attention watching the burn down. If pleasure lived in the tube, why did pleasure always thin before the tube burned halfway, with the second half smoked to finish rather than to enjoy.

If the tube steadied nerves, why did nerves always need steadying again within the hour, with the hand again reaching before decision and the mind again circling the next permitted hour.

If the tube kept company, why did company always stay with the table after ends were stubbed, with laugh heard whole only when paper no longer hired attention between sentences.

You know the only honest answers return credit to its owners. The fact is smoked nicotine peaks within pulls and fades within minutes, and fading calls again. The call sounds like an empty, hollow tug low under the ribs, there and gone and there again. The tug never proved a friend speaking, because friends do not bill by the minute for quiet they first broke. The tug proves the tiny physical craving sounding briefly where feeds once answered, misread so long by the brainwashing voice as proof the dose gives calm.

Feel how that seeing fits the stain and the ash. You feared such watching must make the tube precious by attention. Nothing grows precious there. Something thins, the way mist thins when stared through till road shows. Attention kept on ugliness removes the glow farewell fondness always lent, and glow removed leaves paper unable to pose as keepsake. The tube smoked with eyes open tastes of what it always tasted of with eyes half closed, only now the tasting is owned.

Smoke it to its stale end without hurry and without fondness. You know the last pulls short and hot, the sour aftertaste filming the tongue. You feel the stomach turn faintly where stomach always turned, the head tighten where head always tightened. Let the sour stay sour without sweetening by memory, because memory always borrowed its sweetness from elsewhere. The sun never lived in the filter, because sun warmed the patio before the packet appeared. Leisure never lived in the draw, because leisure widened the hour while paper narrowed it. Company never lived in the lit end, because company bound the table and keeps binding after the end is stubbed.

Stub it now with decision in the wrist, not anger. You know the stubbing you mean, the end ground out till no ember breathes, the smoke thinning upright then gone. You feel relief quiet and complete begin before the ember cools, the kind that needs no witness. You believe quiet proves something still missing, because tugs always filled quiet with noise. Quiet here proves the signing ended, because signing alone made the noise and seeing alone ends the signing.

WHAT THE LAST ONE SHOWS

See what the stub proves once watched to its end, because here the crossing asks to be owned plainly.

You know the pack now empty beside the full ashtray, the lighter cold, the air still faint with drift. You feel the hand empty where hand always held paper through pauses, the fingers strange without their address. You believe strangeness proves loss. Strangeness here proves return, because fingers held cups and rails and shoulders for years before paper rented them. The strangeness lasts an evening the way a removed band lasts on skin, a pale line where pressure sat, and pale lines fill without treatment.

We all feared the empty hand must ache for weeks. We all believed ache must mean need, and need must mean the tube gave something real. You feel the hollow stir thin in the hours after stubbing, the restless edginess rising and falling like weather. Hear it for what it is. It is not you missing. That hollow is the nicotine trap sounding its last billed calls, not you.

The fact is the restless empty feeling peaks in the first few days and then eases within weeks for most who stop. The sharpest edge sits early, often by the second or third day, then softens. It may begin within hours. It never arrives as torture for all, because bodies differ and histories differ, and difference never disproves the short shape. The shape stays short where belief stays shut, and shut belief lets the calls die for lack of answer. The tiny physical craving starves within days when unfed, and starving sounds briefly louder before quiet, the way a lamp flares before going out.

If the hollow proved need, why does the hollow always fade when left unfed, with mornings opening clearer by the week, food tasting more itself, stairs climbed without thinking of breath.

If the tube gave calm, why does calm always deepen after the hollow quiets, with sleep settling, temper steadying, attention staying whole through sentences that once split round burns down.

If the dose filled pauses, why do pauses always fill fuller with hands empty, with the kettle doing the waking, the plate doing the closing, the pause doing the resting, each hour returned to its owners.

You know the answers because you just lived the table to its stale end. The last tube gave the same short flare and the same stale finish as the ten thousand before it, only now watched without blur. One watched to its end proves all unwatched, because all shared the same stain and ash and sour. The tube never did the mornings. The mornings did the mornings. The tube never did the nights. The nights did the nights. Credit returned thickens its owners, and owners thickened leave no thin place for paper to rent.

Feel how that proof fits the kitchen five months clear you already know. You feared such proof must threaten with months. Nothing threatens there. Something protects, because months clear already proved the same proof without a table. The house stayed clean, the purse stayed heavier, the coat stayed clean through washes, the stairs stayed easy. The table only repeats in one evening what months repeat in many, and repetition here steadies rather than frightens, the way a second witness steadies the first without raising voice.

You know the finality now standing where the kept one stood, the whole chain seen in one tube. You feel the thought arrive that one in future might test the seeing, dressed as calm curiosity after months. Answer it with the stub before it speaks fully. A single puff reattaches the tiny physical craving and re-arms the brainwashing voice that misreads the twinge as proof the dose gives company. Company never came from the tube, because the table gave company before the packet appeared. The rule stays none, not fewer, held as rail rather than threat. Rails do not scold walkers, they remove the drop by removing the step.

TWO DOORS THAT STAY SHUT

Meet now the two doors that stay shut behind the stub, because here freedom asks to be guarded without effort.

You know the first door by its knock, arriving on a bad day. You feel the sharp words still ringing, the phone down hard, the papers spread without order, the shoulders high. You know the hand already reaching by old address before decision, the hollow stirring with the day's noise. You believe the day proves the dose must help bear days. Days here prove the dose never bore anything, because the same desk watched plain showed papers untouched after pulls. The day stays the day with or without smoke, only with smoke the day adds timed exits, dry mouth, split attention, and evening maths about smell and count.

We all met that door in rain and called it rescue. We all felt the first pull widen the chest and mistook widening for lifting of the day. You feel sense in the rescue story, a witness speaking for hard hours. You know the sense borrows your mouth. Answer it with the desk itself, and ask what the desk asks.

If smoke lifts dreadful days, why do dreadful days always return with the same papers unsolved, the same words unanswered, the same hollow calling again within the hour.

If the tube steadies hands for hard talk, why do hands always shake harder between feeds, with the meeting endured for the break and the break spent watching the burn down.

If rescue lived in paper, why did rescue always bill the evening with cough, ash, and thought spent on when and where and how many left.

You know the door stays shut by seeing, not by holding. When the bad-day thought rises next month, you will hear it already answered. Hear the voice, hear the answer beneath it, and walk on without opening the packet. Thoughts cannot be stopped by effort, but they can be answered by seeing, and seeing answers this one each time without lighting.

You know the second door by its softer knock, arriving on a kind day. You feel the patio heaters warm on the face, the drink cold in the palm, the laugh carrying the table. You know the packet tapped and offered with easy ceremony, the voice alongside it light and fond, calling the white tube a keepsake for old times. You believe kindness proves refusing must wound friendship. Kindness here proves the trap borrowing friendship's mouth, because friendship never lived on the cold step. The step divides the circle it claims to close, and circles close warmer without paper hired to stand in the photograph.

We all heard that offer after long clear stretches and mistook it for our own curiosity. We all heard it reasonable after months, saying steadiness has earned a taste. You feel the hollow stir thin where drink and watching feed it. Answer it with the months themselves. Clear months prove you live complete without a dose, never that a dose has grown safe. A taste tests the lock by locking it again, and locked again rebuilds the whole rule by morning, with the hand again reaching before feet touch floor.

If one in kindness proved belonging, why does belonging always stay with the table after ends are stubbed, with talk loud and glasses sweating and no tally kept.

If one after months proved control, why does control always need proving with paper, when the non-smoker at the same table through the same hours proves nothing and misses nothing.

If the kept one is small, why does the small one always grow the whole persuasion back, with morning reach returning, meal demanding its signing, break demanding its pellet, night demanding its edge.

You know both doors now standing shut where offers stood, the bad day answered by the desk, the kind day answered by the table. You feel the tug-of-war loosen where both ropes belonged to the same hands, fear of staying and fear of leaving both voiced by the persuasion that billed both. Fear of staying said look at cost. Fear of leaving said how will you cope. Both sounded like you. Neither was you. The nicotine trap held one rope with threat and the other with false comfort, and you stand now off the field where ropes pull.

Never reopen the decision.

Feel how that sentence closes the doors quietly. You feared such closing must bind you. Closing here frees, because the decision already made in clear seeing needs no daily court. The court was the trap's last room, and empty courts hold no trials. When future thoughts arrive dressed as your own reason, hear them labelled now so they cannot pose as reason later. The thought is the old persuasion defending its picture, not your heart reading the months.

A slip, if it ever touches, is a warning rumble, not a crash. You know the tyre touching the strip and steering back without driving to the ditch to prove the touch. You feel shame want to arrive with the touch and whisper that all is lost. Shame here proves nothing about you, because one touch never remade belief unless belief is invited back. Notice what happened, close the door, walk on. Never plan a touch to test the seeing. The seeing stays yours to be kept without reopening, and kept seeing lets months stay yours.

12. SMOKE YOUR FINAL CIGARETTE AND KNOW YOU ARE FREE
Close the trap with joy, not sadness.

You have smoked it. You have watched it. You have stubbed it with eyes open on stain and ash and stale end. You know the ember dead, the sour owned, the credit returned. You feel the hand empty and the evening ordinary and the quiet complete. You believe freedom must wait for days to prove itself. Freedom here waits for nothing, because nothing remains to be proved where nothing was ever given.

Don't wait to feel free — you already are.

Feel how that sentence lands in the body rather than the calendar. You feared such lines must demand feeling you do not yet hold. Nothing demands there. Something tells, the way eyes tell light without obeying the sun. The tiny physical craving may sound briefly where feeds once answered, and sounding will pass within days for most, then within weeks to quiet. The brainwashing voice may translate the sounding as memory, and memory will thin each time answered without opening. Neither sounding proves bond, because bonds need two ends and paper now holds none. You stand with hands empty at the rail on an ordinary evening hearing sentences whole. That hearing does not wait for mornings or Mondays to be true. It waits only for the stub to be owned as owned.

I congratulate you as one escaped to another, with warmth and without envy. You know my mornings now yours, the kettle doing the waking, the plate doing the closing, the air at the kerb taken full without counting. You feel pride warm and quiet rise where shame used to tally, the kind that needs no witness. We all left the same table with the same stub cold, and we all found the days after thicker rather than thinner, because returned credit thickens its owners. The morning thickens. The meal keeps its closing. The break keeps its rest. The night keeps its laugh. The hollow alone thins, already starving, misread so long as proof of pleasure.

Nothing to lose and everything to gain.

Feel how that line returns now with new weight. You feared such promises must belong to page one only, bright paint for beginners. Bright here proves true paint, because paint tested through diary and keepsake and calendar and table still holds. You lost no friend at the table, because friendship never lived in the filter. You lost no calm at the desk, because calm never lived in the draw. You lost no belonging on the patio, because belonging never lived on the cold step. What you lost was rent, tallies, stains, ash, sour, cough, counting, stepping out mid-sentence, patting pockets, hanging shirts by windows. Loss here reads as bill cleared, and cleared bills leave hands free for living.

You know the last cigarette now behind you where bargains stood, the diary closed, the keepsake refused, the calendar uncircled, the stub cold. You feel the evening keep its ordinary shape without paper hired, no exile to doorways, no tallies in drawers. You believe such keeping must be held by effort through weeks. Such keeping is held by seeing, and seeing is done each time the offer is heard without obeying and the thought is answered without opening. The hours will do the living. The dose no longer does the signing. The signing ended where seeing began, and seeing begins now and does not reopen.

Never reopen the decision.

SUMMARY
- You believe the last cigarette proves every tube gives nothing, because stain and ash and stale end watched plain return all credit to the hours that always did the living.
- You see the restless hollow after stubbing as short fading pharmacology rather than need, because it peaks early and quiets within weeks while mornings open clearer without it.
- You understand the bad-day rescue and the kind-day offer arrive as the trap speaking rather than your own reasoning, because each voice returns to the hollow it came from once answered.
