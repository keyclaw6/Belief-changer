MOVE E4-M1: PRESENT — Carr names smoking as a sinister trap sprung without real bait and strips “choice” from the story; ours performs the same inversion throughout, reframing reach-as-choice as a con that removed freedom while leaving the dignity story in place. Evidence: “You do not smoke because you freely choose it — you reach because you were caught and taught to call the reaching choice.” “You were conned on false information, and the con removed the choice while leaving the story of choice in place to protect itself.” “A hobby does not file those reports. A trap does.” “This is the nicotine trap in plain sight: a loop that creates a need and then sells you the same loop as the answer, until the hand obeys faster than thought and the mouth calls the obedience enjoyment.”

MOVE E4-M2: MISSING — Carr’s turn is that people already inside the trap warn newcomers not to start — filthy, costly, deadly — yet the newcomer still lights up because they cannot believe the warners are not secretly enjoying it; ours never stages that social-proof failure, only adjacent scenes (self-promises, bodily disgust, brainwashing) that do not land the warners-still-enjoy-it paradox. Evidence (absence): no passage pairs fallen smokers’ explicit warnings with the listener’s disbelief of their misery; nearest material stays on a different axis — “That first cigarette never pleased you. You coughed. Your eyes watered. Your head spun. You persisted because the older boys laughed, because the films made it look grown” — lure and persistence, not warnings-from-inside failing.

MOVE E4-M3: PARTIAL — Carr’s tragic recognition is that becoming hooked takes deliberate work through awful first doses — the first ones are no gift, you must force yourself past poison signals; ours gestures at an unpleasant first cigarette and social pressure to persist but never names the labor of hooking itself or the “not a gift” turn with Carr’s force. Evidence: “That first cigarette never pleased you. You coughed. Your eyes watered. Your head spun. You persisted because the older boys laughed, because the films made it look grown, because the packet looked confident in your hand.” “Within weeks the cough quieted. Within months you could not remember mornings before.” Gap: no line equivalent to Carr’s “how hard we have to work in order to become hooked” — persistence is shown, tragic work and the gift illusion on entry are not fully turned.
