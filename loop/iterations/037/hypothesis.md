## Root cause

The available diagnosis locates the amplifier in **`prompts/chapter-reviewer.md`**, specifically its length-expansion instructions. Historical reviewer traces demanded:

> “Add ~2000+ words that serve the card's primary job”

and:

> “Add ~2200-3700 words”

The resulting expansions rebuilt earlier demonstrations despite card-level token-echo restrictions. Iteration 036’s backward fence materially reduced this class in both subjects, supporting that diagnosis.

The remaining expansion rule under **Rules** is still:

> “When you demand more words, quote which card job those words must serve.”

Quoting a broad job does not identify what remains unpaid. It lets a writer satisfy LENGTHEN by supplying another complete proof of an already-landed conclusion. The existing `RE-ARGUMENT` finding detects that afterward; its replacement-word constraint applies explicitly when cutting a detected rebuild creates a shortfall. Ordinary LENGTHEN requests still need no concrete, unspent expansion target.

The proposed mechanism is therefore **preventive expansion specificity**, not another backward-fence instruction. Its relevance to the residual 036 instances is a hypothesis, since fresh passage-level traces were not supplied.

## Targeted fix

**Change 1 (PRIMARY) — `prompts/chapter-reviewer.md` — journey `re-argument` — Rules / LENGTHEN expansion directive — targets both quit-sugar and quit-smoking — replacement text.**

Replace:

```text
- When you demand more words, quote which card job those words must serve.
```

with:

```text
- When you demand more words, make the LENGTHEN finding an expansion assignment, not merely a quotation of the card's job: identify a specific unfinished encounter, unanswered objection, or undeveloped consequence belonging to this card, quote the draft location to extend, and state what new understanding or lived consequence that extension must deliver. Check that target against earlier cards and against conclusions already landed in this draft; neither an earlier proof in a new setting nor another proof of the same landed conclusion is a valid expansion target. For an ordinary-life card, extend what happens next with the settled understanding already assumed, not how that understanding is proved again. If you cannot identify an unspent target supported by the card and plan, report that limitation within LENGTHEN rather than inventing evidence or requesting generic additional examples; retain the computed budget and the existing ACCEPT requirements.
```

Leave the 036 `RE-ARGUMENT` finding and ACCEPT gate unchanged. No other edits.

This is not a replay of 020–024’s plan-card or writer-continuity changes, nor of 036’s detection-and-cut finding. It replaces the still-generic instruction that commissions additional words before a rebuild exists.

**Why this component:** The reviewer’s documented LENGTHEN requests generated the expansion pressure, and its successful 036 backward fence supports tightening that same component’s expansion assignment rather than revisiting unsupported upstream restrictions.
