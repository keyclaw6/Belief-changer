MOVE E15-M2: PRESENT — Carr’s prison is life rearranged around the next cigarette—restrictions, meals wolfed, events endured only to reach the next light; ours performs the same move through the box’s meter and the chained hour-by-hour ordering of the day. Evidence: “Morning tube. Break tube. Meal-end tube. Phone tube. Wait tube. Worry tube. Drink tube. Last tube before bed that promises sleep and steals breath from sleep. Each burn fading in minutes, each fade calling the next.” and “The box counted your day before you lived it. Take the count back.”

MOVE E15-M3: PRESENT — Carr pities the smoker still trapped in arrangement—pathetic scenes told with contempt for the trap, never scorn for the person; ours keeps that warmth throughout, despising sellers and methods while refusing to despise the reader still arranging. Evidence: “I do not despise you for believing it. I believed it. We all believed it because professionals taught it. I despise the hands that planted it.” and “I lived it and I do not despise you for living it. I despise the seller who sold it as pleasure and the story that called it character. Warmth to you; contempt to the con that made clean people pay to stay stale.” and “When you lift the next tube, watch the hand as if it belonged to someone you love.”
