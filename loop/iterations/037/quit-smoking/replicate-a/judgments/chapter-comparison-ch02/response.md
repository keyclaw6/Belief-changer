Evidence: *"You never chose to need cigarettes; you were tricked into needing them, and that need now chooses for you."* and *"You walked in on false promises, the door closed quietly behind you, and now you call the inside your choice."*

**MOVE E4-M2: MISSING** — Carr’s turn is that people already inside the trap warn newcomers (and the body screams on the first dose), yet newcomers still light up because they cannot believe hooked smokers are not enjoying it; ours never stages warnings-from-inside-the-trap failing—only choice-vs-trap, hiding, and automatic reach. We stopped choosing to smoke and started needing to smoke in order to feel normal."*
