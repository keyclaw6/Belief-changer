## 4. Scene and analogy bank

- **SC-01 — the 2pm breakroom plate.** Stale office cake nobody chose, eaten standing at 2pm while thinking of root-beer floats; tired, bored, faintly hungry. Job: proves mediocre sweets are eaten for the slump, not taste. Constraint: never make colleagues villains; pity the plate, not the people.

- **SC-03 — the supermarket tour.** Aisles walked hunting every missed brand, cart loaded with crunch, pops and tubs, embarrassment dissolving at checkout because everyone else's cart matches. Job: normalizes the abnormal into visibility; industry default made tangible. Constraint: no brand names in reader prose.

- **SC-07 — the seeded pantry.** Bits of gourmet chocolate behind the peanut butter, by the soup, on the top shelf — a fresh piece discovered by the sugar whenever the last is found. Job: cue power and stash futility. Constraint: comic warmth, never squalor porn.

- **AN-01 — the house-party gatecrasher.** Lonely carbs texting their stupid friends until the house is overrun by couch-crashers who won't leave. Job: sugar begets sugar; second-meal pull. Constraint: original wording; one staging only.
