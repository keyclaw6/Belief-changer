# Master Plan — Quit Smoking

## 1. Book core

- Target behavior: compulsive cigarette smoking. Total stop: last ordinary cigarette, then none. Not cutting down, not vaping as a destination, not NRT as the method.
- Reader (planner-facing state): an adult daily smoker who wants to stop, has failed by willpower, patches, gum, or cold turkey, and believes life without cigarettes would be miserable or impossible.
- Load-bearing false belief (frozen): cigarettes relax me, help me cope, and are my pleasure, and I would be deprived without them.
- Through-line: you were conned into a nicotine trap that creates the very discomfort it sells relief from; seeing the con whole makes stopping an escape, and the last ordinary cigarette closes the door.
- Format: full-length Easyway book, 15 rooms (front matter + 13 chapters + short recap), planned 62,800 words.
- Fork decisions:
  - F1 inner state: full Carr personification. The trivial physical loop is the Ash Mite, a tiny ash-dwelling parasite the reader starves to death within days. The belief-system that feeds it is the Big Fog, the lifetime weather of brainwashing that makes the Mite look like a friend. Burgeon no-monster stance not taken.
  - F2 outcome: total cessation, commanded with cheerful certainty. Moderation foreclosed by the pincer and the cliff-jump image. No radical-autonomy override.
  - F3 science weight: hard facts stated flat and frightening where true, then the reader is told not to change from fear; the scare stays inside the argument, never in an appendix; raw citations quarantined to back matter. No literature-review texture.
  - F4 villain: two villains hit hard — the nicotine industry that designed the dose and the named anti-method, the Willpower Method, that kept the reader in the trap by selling suffering as the price. The reader is never the villain.
  - F5 void: change nothing else in life. The non-smoker baseline returns on its own; the reader inhabits every old context to prove freedom. No replacement system, no root-craving extension.
- Redefinition and margin-for-error doctrine: none needed — smoking is a wholesale-quittable category, so no BAD-X split and no CAPS subset name. Margin doctrine: not one puff ever; a slip revives nothing unless the Big Fog belief is let back in, so the reader guards the belief, not the behavior.
- Safety perimeter: no medical advice anywhere; no instruction to ignore a doctor; crisis or illness pointers only; the last-cigarette ritual is ceremonial, never a dare to smoke more. One plan-wide boxed advisory (CA-01) printed in front matter and cited once more on the vow card only.
- Strongest pro-behavior scene: the night-out smoking area with drinks — the loved haze of alcohol, friends, and buzz the reader calls their last night on earth, collapsing into one won't hurt.
- Destination state: a reader who, whenever smoking crosses their mind, feels immediate relief and quiet joy at being free, breathes easy through old triggers, pities rather than envies smokers, and never reopens the decision.
- Saved ending reframe (final movement only): the cigarette never joined your life, it only ever interrupted it — you are not ending anything, you are resuming the non-smoker you always were.
- Method preserved throughout: escape never sacrifice; warm to the person and vicious to the trap with zero contempt for the reader; no willpower as solution (it is framed as the symptom of an unchanged belief); fear raised only where the plan assigns it and always re-landed on escape-joy inside the same chapter; freedom conferred immediately at the vow as an identity; the reader's autonomy to choose honored inside a commanded method; all prose original, learned from the mechanism, never reproducing copyrighted lines.
