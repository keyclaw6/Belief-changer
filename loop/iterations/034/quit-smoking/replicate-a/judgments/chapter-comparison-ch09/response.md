MOVE E15-M1: PARTIAL — Carr opens by naming health, money, and stigma as the motives smokers already quote, then turns to the overlooked cost: the unremitting daily slavery; ours inventories that slavery as “rent” but never sets health and money aside as already-known motivators, so the hidden-cost turn does not fully land. Evidence: “Money out before bread. Time out before talk. Breath out before climb. Taste out before sweet.”

MOVE E15-M2: PRESENT — Carr shows life rearranged around the next cigarette—where you may go, what you may do, when meals and events must yield to the itch; ours performs the same prison through timed intervals, pocket-checks, and doorway exile built around the next dose. Evidence: “The hour split into counting down to the doorway and counting drags till the stub.”

MOVE E15-M3: PRESENT — Carr pities the smoker still living as a slave—shared humiliation, no contempt for the person still arranging; ours keeps the same warmth toward the still-chained reader and blames the trap, never the person. Evidence: “Warm hearts hid, not bad hearts. The chain made hiding needful, and hiding made the chain heavier.”
