MOVE E46-M1: PRESENT — Carr closes by handing the reader a short spoken list and forbidding any reopening of the quit decision; ours photographs a twelve-line list, tells the reader to carry it in the pocket, and treats the farewell table as permanently closed. Evidence: “Take a picture of this page with your phone. Keep it in your pocket where the box used to sit.” and “You smoked your final one and knew the matter closed.” and “It is simple now: All you have to do is follow all the instructions.”

MOVE E46-M2: PRESENT — Carr’s final chapter assumes future cigarette-thoughts get a rehearsed freedom-script, not a fresh debate; ours names the returning voice, answers with the kept short lines, and explicitly refuses argument. Evidence: “What if an old line comes back in my own voice?” … “Answer it with the short line you keep and turn back to what you were doing.” and “The lines do not argue. They remind. The argument is already won in the body by days lived straight through.” and “If a stray thought floats by tomorrow in your own voice, hear it labelled for what it is and answer with the shout that reports all those rooms.”

MOVE E46-M3: PRESENT — Carr tells the freed reader to help others with the same reframing argument, never by scare or nagging; ours sends the reader outward warm to the person, harsh to the trap, with a single kind telling and no preaching. Evidence: “Be warm to the person and harsh to what holds him. Say what you saw once, kindly, and turn back to the table.” and “That turning is not cold. It is kind to both — kind to them because you do not lecture, kind to you because you do not reopen.” and “Show the picture to no one as a lesson. Keep it as a receipt.”
