Chapter 13
GET ON WITH ENJOYING LIFE

*You have not lost sweetness, you have got it back, and there is nothing left to study.*

If You Opened The Book Here

If you have opened the book here at the end, do not start here. Close these pages now and begin at the first page and read straight through with an open mind while you live exactly as normal. What follows on these last pages is not the method. It is saved reminders for a reader who has already seen the con and spoken the vow. Taken alone it will sound like orders. Read in order it reads like plain description.

I tell you this because I know how the mind hunts for a shortcut. We all did it for years with packets. Hoping one label, one rule, one list could do the work of seeing. It cannot. Seeing does the work. The list only keeps what seeing won.

So if you came with me from the first morning watch, stay with me a few minutes longer. If you did not, leave me now. I will be here when you return with eyes open.

All you have already met waits ahead in one hot meal. No more lessons stand between you and the table.

What You Carry With You

You came with a head full of noise about brightness. Late hours. Tired hours. Told-you-so hours when the hand moved toward the drawer before thought caught it. We lived that noise together, you and I and every reader who ever stood at ten at night with the cupboard half empty and called it choice after the hand had moved. We called hurry love. We called flood fuel. We called coating comfort.

Look now at what your own days showed you once the reading was done.

The lift that thinned by four. The box that emptied after one permitted bite. The celebration where laughter and light and company held the room and paper only rode along. The desk at three where a sweet top-up gave ten loud minutes and a long flat afternoon. What did paper ever add to those hours? Did talk run warmer because foil tore? Did work run clearer because the tongue was coated? Who was doing the living in those moments, you with your eyes and hands and hunger, or the packet with its print?

You know. Paper gave loud seconds and dulling thirds and a hand circling while talk thinned. Hunger met by plain food closes kindly and leaves the mind with people and rest.

All you have to do is follow all the instructions.

That clause sounds too small to hold so much. I have watched it hold. Readers who argue keep circling. Readers who follow with an open mind walk out with mouth plain and head plain and no quarrel left running. The line is not modesty. It is mechanics. The trap was built from belief. Belief read aright starves what belief fed.

Eat what you want, when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and never punish yourself again.

Speak that at the table and mean it as you speak it. No weighing. No earning by being good. No sad arithmetic after. Hunger calls slowly, openly, and welcomes bread and soup and fruit. Enough arrives with a click you knew as a child, hand stilling on its own, attention lifting back to the day. If your clinician guides what you eat, follow their advice first. This book changes a belief. It does not overrule care.

Photograph the list at the end of this chapter if you like having paper to touch. Put the book away and live. A passing hollow at old hours is memory firing, not orders. Note it the way you note rain on glass, there and passing, and turn back to work and people and weather. Plain food will meet true hunger when hunger comes.

Sweetness Was Stolen, Now Handed Back

Here is the last turn, and I saved it for now because you could not have heard it at the start.

You think you have left sweetness behind. You have not. Sweetness was taken from you.

Think of the child with juice running down the chin over a peach. No counting. No hurry. No second reach while talk thins. Hunger made the peach loud. Enough sent him running. That eater was you before print taught hurry. Then came bliss-point packs ranked like flags, cartoon faces, till ambushes for the tired hand, love and lift printed large enough to read from three paces. The teaching said brightness was reward, brightness was comfort, brightness was the point of the occasion. The tongue learned to search teeth. The belly learned noise without closing. The afternoon learned thinning. Mornings learned film.

What kind of bargain keeps dosing for the rest of your life for something that never fed you?

What kind of mornings eat and laugh and sleep clear for the rest of your life with mouth fresh and mind undivided?

You have answered already with your own feet through shops walked without bargaining and suppers that closed kindly and nights that ran whole without rustle under talk. The answer is not theory.

You have not given up sweetness. Paper stole sweetness and called itself sweet. Real sweetness is peach and bread crust and soup steam and berries closed with hunger behind them, tasted by a mouth that wakes plain. That was handed back the moment belief went. This was never about quitting a joy. It was about growing back into the eater you were before print taught you to call hurry love.

That hollow that still knocks at half past three, that empty, twitchy picture of foil rising half a second, is not proof paper held something. It is an old bell rung by last floods. True hunger grows slowly and welcomes a meal. That hollow sneers at soup and calls for brightness to seal the hour. Hear the difference once and you cannot unhear it.

Give the knock its true meaning the instant it lands and the knock turns to ground under feet. Smile at the thinning grumble. Turn to the page, the pan, the person in front of you. By four it thins. By supper hunger calls cleanly and bread answers. That is the whole event.

And whenever sweet memory crosses, speak the bright sentence you already own and feel how it lands, glad from the ground up.

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!

Close The Book And Eat

The table is laid. The book has done its work.

Change nothing else in life. Same mug. Same street sounds through glass. Same invitations. Same sofa. Bring a dish you love. Eat hungry where all can see it, without comment. Talk late. Laugh fully. Take the plate in hand and belong with talk and laugh, not with flood. No patrol added. No new system wheeled in to fill a hole, because no hole was left. What was taken away never fed you.

Pity the wire where you see it working on others, warmth for the person, never scorn. We were conned, not foolish. Leave them their minds. Quiet steadiness says more than loud saving. When asked, speak short and stop. Your clear mornings will do the rest of the talking.

Do not debate the knock. Do not time it. Do not weigh it. Note it and let attention rest back on hands and eyes and weather. A jolt from a changed recipe in half-light steadies past. What matters is the wheel, not the pothole. Keep the wheel true and the feet follow.

You came half hoping, half scoffing, clutching rules that always collapsed by Thursday. You leave with hunger leading and satisfaction closing and real food tasting of more than paper ever tasted, because hunger is in the room.

Get on with enjoying your life.

SUMMARY
- Brightness gave loud seconds and left flat hours. Hunger met by plain food closes kindly.
- The knock at old hours is memory, noted and left to thin. No debate keeps it alive.
- A photographed list is a reminder, not the method. Seeing did the work.
- Sweetness was stolen by print and hurry. Hunger gives it back at the table.
- The book closes. The meal ahead stays hot. Live it with mouth plain and mind undivided.
