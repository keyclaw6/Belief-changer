## 1. BOOK CORE

**Target behavior:** Compulsive consumption of BAD SUGAR — refined/added free sugars and junk processed carbs (the craving–snacking loop), not food itself or nutrition pedantry.

**One reader, 4 functional personas:**
- P-01 The loss-of-control binger — one-bite-becomes-the-box, secret eating, shame, failed restriction.
- P-02 The in-denial moderate — daily grazing as "just a treat," moderation negotiation, carve-outs.
- P-03 The comfort/identity eater — sugar as reward, love, deserved payment, nostalgia.
- P-04 The energy-crash yo-yoer — sugar as fuel to function through the 3pm slump, lift–crash cycle.
All chapters speak to one adult reader; persona tags route ventriloquism and scene choice, not separate tracks.

**Load-bearing false belief:** *BAD SUGAR gives me genuine pleasure, energy and reward — as a treat, lift, comfort and love — and life without it would be deprivation, less sweet and less mine.*

**Through-line:** *The treat that steals the sweetness.* Every chapter proves the rescuer is the perpetrator: the sweetness you chase is the sweetness BAD SUGAR took.

**Format:** Full-length, 20 chapters, ~60,000 words. B10 architecture (Good Sugar Bad Sugar) governs: IN THIS CHAPTER + italic thesis + body (one belief-move) + chapter instruction where assigned + SUMMARY recap. B6 pocket logic expanded, not compressed.

**Fork decisions (Method §4, defaults = Carr):**
- **F1 Inner monster — DEFAULT Carr personify:** Two mechanism characters, original behavior-fitted names frozen as mantras M04/M05. Little Monster `the Nibbler` = trivial physical gnawing loop, to starve. Big Monster `the Sweet Story` = belief-system/brainwashing that feeds it. Craving is external, small, already dying; belief is the target. Burgeon no-monster is not used.
- **F2 Abstinence vs autonomy — DEFAULT Carr total cessation inside a redrawn line:** Redefinition move required. BAD SUGAR = zero. Total abstinence commanded with cheerful certainty *inside the line*; autonomy preserved *outside the line* via Nature's Guide. Moderation foreclosed with pincer + cliff-jump.
- **F3 Science weight — DEFAULT Carr scare-then-disown:** Hard facts delivered at full Carr force — flat, vivid, unhedged, frightening where true — then explicitly disowned as motivator. No literature-review texture; citations quarantined to back-matter appendix. Facts as perception-shifters, then escape-joy re-landing.
- **F4 Villain — DEFAULT Carr two villains:** Sugar/junk industry that engineered the trap + the willpower method that kept you in it. Reader never the villain. Anger channeled outward.
- **F5 Void — DEFAULT Carr "Change nothing else in your life":** Natural baseline returns on its own. No elaborate replacement system. The body manufactures glucose; freedom is inhabiting old contexts confidently to prove it. Root-craving/transmutation is not used (brief does not override).

**Redefinition and margin-for-error doctrine (B10):**
Boxed in Ch1 and carried by CAPS name thereafter: **BAD SUGAR = added/refined free sugars + junk processed carbs + starchy carb overload that behaves as sugar (sweets, biscuits, cakes, confectionery, sugary drinks, added sugar in processed foods).** Decree: *when I say sugar, take it to mean BAD SUGAR.* Good sugars (whole fruit, vegetables, whole foods) are not quit; they are the positive authority's menu via Nature's Guide.
**Margin for error:** The body can cope with an occasional accidental blip; the mind cannot cope with a deliberate exception. A slip revives nothing physically unless you re-invite the Sweet Story. Guard the belief, not the behaviour. Seatbelt image: the belt is for accidents, not for driving erratically. No licensing of scheduled treats.

**Clinical / eating-disorder safety perimeter:** This is a belief-change book for the general adult sugar loop. Not medical nutrition therapy, not diabetes management, not eating-disorder treatment. Boxed advisory in front matter + in I-05: *If you have diabetes, a medical condition requiring dietary management, or an eating disorder, or you use medication affected by diet, follow your clinician's advice; this method does not replace professional care. If you need urgent help, contact your healthcare provider or local crisis resources.* No diagnosis, no prescription, no weight-loss mechanics, no moralization of body size.

**Strongest pro-behavior scene (to meet head-on in Ch12):** The shared celebration — birthday cake at the table, cinema chocolate in the dark, the Friday-night rewarded dessert where sugar feels indistinguishable from love, belonging and occasion itself. If the book can reassign *that* pleasure, no lesser scene survives.

**Destination state:** Whenever the reader thinks about BAD SUGAR they feel *relief and freedom that they no longer do it* — immediacy on the final dose, identity conferred: happy to be free, not abstaining. Thought script fires automatically: `FANTASTIC! I'M FREE!`

**Saved ending reframe (appears only in final movement):** *You were not giving up sweetness — you were getting your sweetness back. BAD SUGAR was dulling the very taste it promised.* The whole effort reframes as growth/restoration of pleasure, not renunciation. Reserved for Ch20.

**Method fidelity:** Escape not sacrifice; warm to the person / vicious to the trap; no willpower (willpower reframed as symptom of belief intact); fear raised at full force then disowned by the escape; immediate freedom after belief change; autonomy inside Nature's Guide; original prose; mechanism characters named and used per F1.
