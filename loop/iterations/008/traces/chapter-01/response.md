### THE CONMAN AND THE ROLLERCOASTER

Let me give you two pictures to carry with you. They will make everything else obvious.

First, the Conman's Pitch. Imagine a well-dressed fraud who comes to your door. He offers you an investment. "Pay me every day," he says, "and I will give you a wonderful boost." You pay. You feel a lift. You thank him. You pay again tomorrow. The boost comes again. You think he is helping you. What you do not see is that he stole that energy yesterday. He created the low you now pay him to relieve. He returns a fraction of what he took and you call it a gift. Would you call that a treat? Would you call him your friend?

That is how the Sugar Trap works. The lift you feel is not a bonus. It is the low leaving. The rescuer is the perpetrator. Once you see the con, you stop thanking the conman. You feel very different about paying him. Do you see?

Second, the Rollercoaster. You know this one. You live it. You get on in the morning. The cart climbs — sugar, lift. Then it plunges — fog, tiredness, irritability. You queue again. You tell yourself the next ride will be smoother. It never is. The climb gets shorter. The plunge gets deeper. You need the family size now where a single biscuit once did. And you call this "to get me through." Does that sound like being carried? Or does that sound like being strapped in?

We love to say "the roller coaster" because it is true. You feel it in your body. Quick and sweet, then fast drop. Short term lift, inevitably followed by crash. That is not fuel. That is the ride. Getting off is not giving something up. It is stepping onto steady ground. Free. Clear. Calm.

These pictures do not ask you to use willpower. They do not ask you to count or resist. They simply re-label what you already feel. Keep them handy. They will do more work than any lecture.

And one more truth to plant now. You do not need to understand every mechanism today. All you have to do is follow all the instructions. That is the contract that makes this easy. Follow the instructions, and the belief corrects itself. The behaviour follows on its own.
