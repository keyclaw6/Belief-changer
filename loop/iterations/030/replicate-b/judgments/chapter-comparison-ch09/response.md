MOVE G15-M1: PRESENT — Carr tells the reader to reverse brainwashing for themselves alone — not to stop the industry, not for children or partners — and our chapter lands that turn in “FOR YOU FIRST,” where escape must stand on the reader’s own clarity rather than martyrdom, punishment, or applause.

Evidence: “Your decision rests with your mouth, your evenings, your mornings, your joy. Taken for you, it holds for life. Taken for others, it wobbles with their moods.”

Evidence: “If the bar gave love, would love fade square by square while need grows? Love does not work that way. Love deepens with attention. The bar thins with attention and only the reaching grows.”

Evidence: “The fact is, pleasure rode in with people, story, rest, laughter, closeness, ease. BAD SUGAR only rode along.”
