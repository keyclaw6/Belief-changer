**The book's journey in five sentences:** The reader arrives wary but invited, promised an easy escape and told to keep eating normally while the con is exposed. Chapters 2–4 shift blame from character to the Sugar Trap and the Willpower Method, emptying the benefit column and acquitting past failure. Chapters 5–8 demolish fuel, restore hunger and satisfaction as authority, split the Nibbler from the Sweet Con, and trace manufactured want plus fear-ropes to the industry and the Con. Chapters 9–10 meet the strongest celebration case head-on, close every carve-out (Friday, one bite, bare cupboard, tomorrow), and the cliff metaphor seals totality. Chapters 11–13 detonate freedom at the vow—last ordinary treat seen ugly, `BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!` installed, ordinary life inhabited without vigil, and a photographable recap with the saved growing-back reframe landing escape, not sacrifice.

**Overall arc assessment:** Read as one cumulative book, this replicate completes the Carr inversion: a believing reader who finishes Chapter 13 should hold no credit worth keeping in BAD SUGAR and should not expect to stop by willpower, white-knuckle maintenance, or ongoing recovery framing. The demolition curve rises through the middle (willpower kill, fuel inversion, creature split, manufacture, celebration, totality) and hands cleanly to a freedom crescendo in 11–13; the ending is relief and excitement, not moralized abstinence. The mantra and instruction spine execute on schedule—terminal `BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!` debuts in 11 and echoes through 12–13; all twelve imperatives recap verbatim in 13 closing with M-I. No blocking gaps; the book works as one journey to escape.
