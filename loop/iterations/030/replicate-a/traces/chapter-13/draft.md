Chapter 13
GET ON WITH ENJOYING LIFE

IN THIS CHAPTER
A photographed list on the kitchen table. A closed book. A bright doorway. A plain meal waiting ahead.

*You need no more teaching to stay free — you only need to get on and live.*

If You Opened Here First

Friend, if you opened the book here at the end, welcome. You are free already in my heart, and I am glad you are here.

This page is saved reminders, not the method. It will not free you by itself. The freedom was won room by room, belief by belief, from the first page to the vow.

Close this page now and begin at the beginning. Read in order with a clear head and a willing heart. All you have to do is follow all the instructions.

When you return here, this list will be your pocket memory. Until then, let the argument do its work. You have a marvellous adventure ahead, and it begins on the first page.

What You Carry Now

You came here believing BAD SUGAR was a genuine pleasure that made life sweeter and more bearable, and that life without it would be grey.

Look at what you know now from your own seeing.

We all fell the same way. A sweet start offered as love, as reward, as fuel for a tired hour. A bright lift for ten minutes. Then fog, thirst, coating, restlessness, and the bell ringing again. We called the lift energy. We called the bell hunger. We called the packet comfort. The fact is the lift was relief from the low the last dose created, and the bell was the echo of that dose calling for the next.

You have watched it hour by hour. Morning coating billed by the night before. The three o'clock dip answered with blaze and followed by deeper fog. The evening box opened for one and emptied before joy arrived. The celebration sweet that seemed to hold the laughter, and held nothing when the laughter was seen plain.

Ask with the whole book warm in your chest:

Did the dose ever carry you clear to the next meal, or did it always sell you the next dip?

Did the packet ever make the moment kind, or did people, hunger met, rest and laughter make it while the dose cut sentences in half?

If mornings are clear now, food is favourite now, and evenings are whole now without it, what did it ever give?

There is only one honest answer. It gave blaze, then weight. You were the warmth all along.

That is why you feel relief when a sweet thought crosses the mind, and pity for the hurried hands still paying. You are not missing a treat. You are seeing a con from outside.

I stood where you stand. Same shops. Same table. Same tender evening hour. I know the lightness in your chest is real because I live it. Trust hunger and satisfaction. Trust clear eyes over loud packets. You are out, and out is wonderful.

The Photograph On The Table

Take a photograph of this list. Keep it in your pocket. Let it be your whole portable memory.

These are not rules to strain with. They are what you already proved true. Read them glad when doubt whispers, then lift your eyes and live.

1. KEEP AN OPEN MIND
Question what you think you know about sweetness.

2. DON'T STOP OR CUT DOWN UNTIL YOU FINISH
Carry on exactly as normal while you read.

3. JUDGE ONLY BY WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU
Forget harm versus benefit and ask for the benefit.

4. NEVER BLAME YOURSELF AGAIN
Every failure was the method, never you.

5. BEGIN WITH ELATION, NOT DREAD
You are escaping, not losing.

6. TRUST HUNGER AND SATISFACTION
Eat when hungry, stop when satisfied, enjoy real food.

7. SEE THE GRUMBLE AS DYING, NOT DEMANDING
That feeling is the Nibbler starving.

8. IGNORE ANY ADVICE THAT CONFLICTS WITH THIS METHOD
Other voices rebuild the Con.

9. IGNORE ANYONE WHO QUIT BY WILLPOWER
Their struggle was the wrong method talking.

10. NEVER KEEP A SPECIAL SWEET
One exception keeps the whole Trap alive.

11. TAKE YOUR LAST ORDINARY TREAT AND VOW FREEDOM
Pay attention to the ugliness, then rejoice you are free.

12. NEVER REOPEN THE DECISION
A passing thought is a dead enemy, not an order.

Whenever a sweet memory crosses the mind, think at once, loudly and gladly:

BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!

Go Outward And Live

Now close the book.

Do not study freedom. Live it. Get on with enjoying your life. That is the whole order now, and it is a joyful one.

Eat what you want, when you are hungry, stop when you are satisfied, and never punish yourself again. If you carry any medical risk around food, talk to your clinician first and follow their advice. Then meet hunger clean, savour real food as favourite, leave at enough, drink water when thirst calls, sleep when tired calls. Change nothing else in life and let the same life prove the difference daily.

Walk mornings clear. Shop with open eyes. Sit at tables with ease. Keep the same chair, the same lamp, the same people, the same talk — minus the fog and the rustle. Let visible ease do any teaching. No badge. No sermon. No circling streets to dodge bright shelves. Eyes pass goods made for another household the way eyes pass coats in the wrong size.

We used to whisper, “I feel like i cant stop,” and “celebrating with chocolate,” and “sugary somethings to get me through,” and “treat when you feel you deserve one,” and call that roller coaster love. You know that script now as the Sweet Con talking, not you. You know that empty hurry as the Nibbler starving, not need. You know the tug as the Sugar Trap with its paint peeling, not kindness.

Hear the old whisper if it comes: but surely one bright day, one hard day, one kind offer deserves a bite. Hear it as echo, not order. Think your joyful thought first. Feel pity for the chase. Walk on lighter. Each crossing turned to relief makes the next fainter and the relief brighter.

Do not mourn a lost friend. Rejoice at a dead enemy. Do not test by tasting. Do not keep a kept sweet to prove power. A slip from blindness teaches by its coating and revives nothing while belief stays shut. A kept bite feeds belief and re-lights the chase. Guard the belief and the behaviour guards itself.

Picture the two roads whole.

Picture the other road — hurried, coated, billed again by eleven, grazing till dusk, thirsty at midnight, thick at dawn, paying for rescue from the rescuer who robbed you, for the rest of your life.

Picture this road — mouth fresh, belly calm, hunger clean, food favourite, evenings whole, sleep clean, mind outward on work and weather and people, happy to be free, for the rest of your life.

Which rest of life do you want? You have chosen. Never reopen the decision.

The Growing Back

Here is the last reveal, saved for this hour because you could not have heard it at the start.

You have not lost sweetness. Sweetness was stolen from you by the Trap and has now been handed back.

We thought BAD SUGAR gave sweet to life. It dulled sweet. After blaze, bread tasted of nothing, fruit tasted sour, water tasted flat. Without blaze, bread tastes nutty and warm, fruit tastes bright and deep, a peach eaten hungry tastes marvellous. The tongue wakes. The nose wakes. Hunger makes favourite. Satisfaction makes enough. That is not learning. That is returning.

This was never about quitting. It was about growing back into the eater you were before the con — the child who ate hungry, stopped at satisfied, left food to run and play, with no counting, no guilt, no bell. You have grown back. Live as grown.

I promise you with all I am: mornings will keep clearing, flavours will keep brightening, evenings will keep widening, and sweet thoughts will keep thinning into relief. Freedom is not watched. It is lived, hour by hour, with hunger as guide and satisfaction as stop.

You waited long for a perfect tomorrow. Tomorrow arrived as today. Take it. Close the book. Lift your head. Walk into the meal ahead with joy.

SUMMARY
- The bright lift was relief from a low the last dose made, never real fuel or comfort.
- Hunger met by real food and company kept whole hold all the joy the packet claimed.
- A passing grumble is a tiny echo starving when unfed, never an order to obey.
- No kept sweet can live beside freedom; one exception keeps the whole belief breathing.
- The decision stays shut; crossing thoughts turn at once to relief and pity for the chase.
- Eating stays simple: hunger calls, satisfaction stops, and life is lived outward with ease.
- Sweetness was dulled by the Trap and returns now by clearing; this is growing back, not loss.
