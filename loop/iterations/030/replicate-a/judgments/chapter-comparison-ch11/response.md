MOVE G18-M1: PRESENT — Carr clears meaningless feast days and tells the reader to stop now with a chosen final BAD SUGAR meal; ours does the same by refusing tomorrow’s safer hour and naming tonight’s ordinary bite as the last. Evidence: “There is no perfect hour for freedom. There is only a true seeing, and yours is true now. An ordinary hour does the work better than any feast day”; “Take your last ordinary treat… Your ordinary sweet, at an ordinary hour, in your ordinary place — the bar, the biscuit, the drink you would have taken anyway tonight.”

MOVE G18-M2: PRESENT — Carr’s last mouthful is sealed with a never-again vow and instant non-addict freedom; ours speaks the vow aloud and confers freedom in the same ordinary hour, not after waiting. Evidence: “Speak it aloud, standing or sitting tall, solemn and glad: I am free. I no longer feed the Sugar Trap. BAD SUGAR is not mine. I eat when hungry, stop when satisfied, and get on with enjoying my life”; “Freedom is conferred now, in this ordinary hour, by true seeing. Do not wait to be free — you already are.”

MOVE G18-M3: PRESENT — Carr marks victory with congratulations and elation, not sacrifice or grief; ours ends mourning, demands lift over dread, and closes on a cheer. Evidence: “Elation, not fear. Excitement, not strain”; “This is the end of mourning. You do not lose a friend tonight”; “Celebrate. Smile wide. Breathe full”; “BRILLIANT! I'M FREE!”; “Let that be the sound that closes the door. Not a sigh. A cheer.”
