MOVE G15-M1: PRESENT — The real chapter tells the reader to reverse brainwashing for themselves alone — not to stop the food industry or save other addicts — and OUR CHAPTER lands that turn in “FOR YOU FIRST,” naming freedom as personal escape rather than duty or proof.

Evidence: “This escape is for you first. Not for the makers. Not for the table. Not to prove anything to anyone who still reaches.” and “You stop because clear mornings feel marvellous and yours are waiting. You stop because evenings belong to faces, not wrappers.”

MOVE G15-M2: PARTIAL — The real chapter structures reversal as an explicit two-pronged attack: appreciate natural foods as they really are, and simultaneously see brainwashed favourites as bland junk; OUR CHAPTER demolishes the belief prong hard on celebration sugar but never pairs it with the positive natural-foods prong or names the coordinated two-prong frame.

Evidence (belief prong lands): “We were brainwashed to sign that feast over to sugar. From childhood we were told sweet means love, sweet means reward, sweet means celebration.” and “Perception was taught. Taught perception can be untaught by seeing.”
