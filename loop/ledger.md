# Experiment Ledger

> One line per experiment: what the hypothesis was, what changed, what
> happened, what we learned. The human-readable story of the campaign.
>
> **Provenance:** each row mirrors the same iteration's `results.tsv` row
> (verdict and lesson are copied from it, not re-worded) — `results.tsv` is the
> canonical record, this is its readable view. `loop/learnings.md` is
> different: its `Lesson` + `Next direction` are what the hypothesizer reads to
> avoid repeating failures, so it is the load-bearing one for the loop. Append
> only — never edit or reword a past row; corrections become a new row.
