# Subject allocation — v2

quit-sugar and quit-smoking are historical development subjects, not a held-out generalization test. New experiments declare their exact subjects and paired run IDs in a frozen specification. Include an unfamiliar habitual behavior, a capability/learning belief, and a case with legitimate benefits/selective reduction in the broader evaluation program. Keep at least one transfer subject out of prompt development. Do not force the same domain mechanism, length, abstinence outcome or chapter count onto every subject.
