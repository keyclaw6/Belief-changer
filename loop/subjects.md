# Subject allocation — v2

quit-sugar and quit-smoking are historical development subjects, not a held-out generalization test. New experiments declare their exact subjects and paired run IDs in a frozen specification. Include an unfamiliar habitual behavior, a capability/learning belief, and a case with legitimate benefits/selective reduction in the broader evaluation program. Keep transfer subjects out of prompt development. Exact held-out topics are selected only after the reviewed intervention is frozen, and once revealed their results permanently retire them from the unseen holdout pool for later cycles. Do not force the same domain mechanism, length, abstinence outcome or chapter count onto every subject.
