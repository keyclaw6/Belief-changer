# Reference Alignment — Our Chapters ↔ GSBS Chapters

Maps our quit-sugar chapters to GSBS chapters by CONTENT (belief-work),
not by offset. Create this before the baseline run by reading both master
plans and matching the belief-move each chapter performs.

| 2 | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| 3 | ... | ... | ... | ... |

## How to fill this

1. Read `production-books/quit-sugar/master-plan.md` — note each chapter's
   assigned belief-move (the one belief it changes).
2. Read GSBS chapter titles and first paragraphs from
   `calibration/reference/gsbs/` — note what belief each chapter works on.
3. Match by belief-work, not by position. Our chapter 1 might align with
   GSBS chapter 3 if they both do "the trap reframe."
4. If no good match exists (our book does something GSBS doesn't), note
   "NO MATCH — judge against general Carr method" and the judges will
   compare against their internal model of Carr rather than a specific
   chapter.

## Alignment quality

- STRONG: same belief-move, same emotional arc, same reader position
- PARTIAL: same belief-move but different approach or different reader position
- WEAK: related but not equivalent — judges should weight their comparison lightly
- NO MATCH: our chapter has no GSBS equivalent — judges compare against Carr method generally
