# Reference moves — Easyway closed belief-moves

Orchestrator-built from the Clarity 2011 US extract. 3–6 moves per real
chapter. The chapter-comparison judge may use only the list for the aligned
Easyway chapter. Do not add moves in the judge call.

IDs are `E<N>-M<k>`. No run of four or more consecutive words copied from the
Easyway extract.

## EASYWAY 2 — THE EASYWAY

- E2-M1: Invitation — begin already glad, not braced for misery
- E2-M2: Easy contract — complete lasting freedom without a fight against yourself
- E2-M3: Method that works — this path, not another scare lecture

## EASYWAY 4 — THE SINISTER TRAP

- E4-M1: Trap, not choice — you were caught, not freely picking a hobby
- E4-M2: Warnings from inside the trap fail — people still light up
- E4-M3: Getting hooked takes work — the first ones are not a gift

## EASYWAY 6 — NICOTINE ADDICTION

- E6-M1: The dose is the product — the tube is packaging around a drug
- E6-M2: Fast in, fast out — the “calm” is the brief return of a fading level
- E6-M3: Empty restless tug — physical lack is slight; panic is the story
- E6-M4: Replenish, don’t rise — lighting ends a lack you created last time
- E6-M5: Tiny creature to feed — continued use is feeding, not enjoying

## EASYWAY 8 — RELIEVING WITHDRAWAL PANGS

- E8-M1: Lighting is for the pang, not a bonus
- E8-M2: Brief “normal” is mistaken for a high
- E8-M3: The pang is mild enough that many never name it as a drug need

## EASYWAY 9 — STRESS

- E9-M1: The stick does not handle the problem — it handles the next dip
- E9-M2: Daily niggles plus big shocks both get blamed on needing a light
- E9-M3: Non-smokers meet the same life without that dip-and-dose loop

## EASYWAY 14 — WHAT AM I GIVING UP?

- E14-M1: You surrender nothing real — only a fear of going without
- E14-M2: Pleasant times were the occasion, not the burning paper
- E14-M3: Coping without it is the baseline you already had before the first one

## EASYWAY 15 — SELF-IMPOSED SLAVERY

- E15-M3: Pity the person still arranging, never scorn them

## EASYWAY 24 — JUST ONE CIGARETTE

- E24-M3: The first drag is the door, not a treat

## EASYWAY 27 — A SOCIAL HABIT?

## EASYWAY 29 — WILL I MISS THE CIGARETTE?

- E29-M3: Remaining “want” is leftover story, not a hole that must be filled

## EASYWAY 33 — THE WITHDRAWAL PERIOD

## EASYWAY 40 — THE FINAL CIGARETTE

- E40-M3: Freedom is conferred at the stub, not after a waiting period
